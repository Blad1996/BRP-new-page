Who we are

Biz Rescue Pro provides IT support, email, cybersecurity, backup and managed IT services to small businesses and farms in Ontario, Canada, from our office at 30 Bertrand Ave, Unit B106, Scarborough, ON M1L 2P5. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Biz Rescue Pro.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), which sets the rules for how private businesses handle personal information.

What we collect

When you contact us

Through the contact form we collect your name, email address, business or farm name, the topic you choose and what you tell us about your setup. If you email or call us, we keep the details you share in that conversation.

When you become a client

To provide our services we may need contact details for you and your staff, account names, and information about your devices, systems and network. Some of this can include personal information stored on your systems. How we handle it is also set out in your service agreement.

When you visit this website

Like most websites, the servers that host this site may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and set up a first conversation.
  • To review your setup and prepare a proposal.
  • To provide, support and bill for the services you hire us for.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell or rent personal information. We don't send marketing emails unless you've agreed to receive them, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only what they need to do their job:

  • Website hosting and form delivery. The companies that host this site and deliver contact form messages to us.
  • Google Fonts. This site loads its typefaces from Google. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Technology vendors. When we manage services for you, such as Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or backup tools, those vendors process data under their own terms.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics, advertising or social media tracking. If that changes, we'll update this policy before it does.

Where it's stored and for how long

Some of our service providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States. When that happens, the information is subject to the laws of that country.

We keep inquiries that don't lead to work for up to 24 months, then delete them. We keep client records for as long as we work together and for up to 7 years afterwards to meet tax and legal requirements.

How we protect it

We protect personal information with measures that match its sensitivity: multi-factor authentication on our accounts, encrypted connections, access limited to the people who need it, and secure deletion when we no longer need it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we've used it.
  • Correct information that's inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent, for example to stop receiving emails from us. This may limit the services we can provide.

We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
