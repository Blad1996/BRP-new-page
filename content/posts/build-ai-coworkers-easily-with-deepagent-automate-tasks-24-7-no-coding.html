🛠 How to Build Your First AI Workflow with DeepAgent

Getting started with DeepAgent is straightforward. The secret is crafting a detailed prompt that clearly outlines your desired workflow. Here’s the basic process:

  1. Go to DeepAgent and select “Create an AI workflow.”
  2. Describe your task: Explain what you want the AI to do, specifying inputs, outputs, constraints, and the business problem.
  3. Define the logic: Break down the task into step-by-step instructions so the AI can build sub-agents accordingly.
  4. Submit and wait: DeepAgent generates the workflow code, runs tests, and prepares the AI agents.
  5. Run and automate: Launch the workflow manually or schedule it to run automatically.

Let me share a concrete example I created to show you how precise prompts translate into powerful autonomous workflows.

Example: Lead Generation for a Web Development Agency in Florida

I wanted to build an AI agent to help my web development and design agency generate qualified leads specifically from Florida. Here’s how I described the workflow:

  • Objective: Find 25 local business leads from industries like cafes, clinics, and real estate that would benefit from web redesign, SEO, branding, or CRM setup.
  • Lead Criteria: Leads must match at least two of the following:
    • Outdated websites with poor SEO
    • No clear or consistent social media presence
    • Public email or contact form available
  • Priority Logic: Prioritize businesses with moderate to high Google reviews indicating reputation and foot traffic, plus signs of poor digital presence.
  • Output: Business name, website URL, contact info, missing elements, suggested service offering, and source link.
  • Input options: Target location (Florida), industry focus, max leads (25).

Once I submitted this prompt, DeepAgent automatically built a project called Florida Lead Generator. It generated the AI workflow, executed Python code to scrape directories and Google Maps, filtered leads based on the criteria, and compiled a list—all autonomously.

Within minutes, I had a curated list of 25 qualified leads, complete with contact details and personalized service suggestions. This AI team worked tirelessly without any manual intervention.

🔍 How DeepAgent Works Behind the Scenes

DeepAgent builds AI workflows by generating code—usually Python—that orchestrates multiple AI sub-agents. Here’s a simplified overview of the process:

  1. Prompt Processing: You describe your workflow in detail, including tasks, inputs, outputs, and constraints.
  2. Workflow Generation: DeepAgent creates a project that defines each AI sub-agent and their interactions.
  3. Code Execution: The platform runs the generated code to perform tasks like web scraping, data processing, email sending, and more.
  4. Testing and Validation: The workflow is tested to ensure accuracy and functionality.
  5. Deployment: You can run workflows on demand or schedule them to run automatically at set intervals.

Throughout this process, you don’t need to touch the code unless you want to customize further. Your role is to orchestrate and refine the prompts, guiding your AI coworkers to deliver exactly what you need.

💡 Tips for Crafting Effective AI Workflow Prompts

To get the most out of DeepAgent, your prompt must be detailed and logically organized. Here are some best practices:

  • Be Specific: Clearly state the task, desired inputs, expected outputs, and any constraints.
  • Break Down Steps: Outline the workflow step-by-step so the AI can build sub-agents accordingly.
  • Define Priority Logic: If filtering or ranking is needed, specify the criteria and weighting.
  • Include Use Case Context: Explain the business problem or goal to help the AI optimize the workflow.
  • Test and Iterate: Run your workflow and refine prompts based on results for better accuracy.

Remember, the clearer your instructions, the smarter and more efficient your AI coworkers will be.
