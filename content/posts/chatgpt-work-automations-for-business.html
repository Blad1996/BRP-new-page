Start With the Right ChatGPT Work Model

Before setting up anything inside ChatGPT Work, the model selection matters. The quality of the results is directly tied to the model doing the work.

ChatGPT 5.6 includes three model options: Sol, Terra, and Luna. If you are using ChatGPT Work for serious tasks, use Sol.

  • Sol: The model to use for complex ChatGPT Work tasks and deeper automation.
  • Terra: A lower-cost model positioned as competitive with GPT 5.5 performance.
  • Luna: The fastest and most affordable option.

Terra and Luna may be useful when cost or speed is the priority, but ChatGPT Work is designed for substantial work. When you want a detailed business report, multi-source research, tool integrations, and action-ready output, Sol is the choice.

You can also adjust the effort setting. Sol offers lighter and more intensive modes. Light and fast can still produce extremely useful results, but the important point is simple: do not expect premium automation results if you are using the lowest-powered model available.

1. Turn Business Data Into an Executive-Level Analytics Report

The first use case is probably one of the most valuable for business owners: uploading a customer report and asking ChatGPT Work to perform a full business analysis.

For ContentBuddy.ai, the request was not just “summarize this spreadsheet.” The goal was to analyse the customer data, review the website, identify product opportunities, look at churn, examine the trial experience, and return a report that could support real decisions.

That is a much bigger assignment than a normal spreadsheet summary. The system went through numerous sources, opened a cloud browser, used sub-agents, and worked for roughly 13 minutes. The output was not a few generic suggestions. It became a full consulting-style report.

What a ChatGPT Work business analysis can include

  • Executive summary of customer and revenue performance
  • Key findings and top recommendations
  • Website messaging and conversion copy improvements
  • Product additions or positioning changes
  • Trial-to-paid conversion analysis
  • Retention, repeat-payment, refund, and dispute observations
  • Churn measurement gaps and recommended instrumentation
  • Opportunity sizing and priority ranking
  • A focused 90-day action plan
  • Questions, caveats, and assumptions to validate before acting

The report also turned into a separate, accessible webpage with visual breakdowns and organised findings. It looked at metrics such as the number of customers, payment patterns, net spend, trial behaviour, country-level performance, and repeat payments.

One key finding was that a seven-day trial could expose a conversion-quality issue. The website may be optimized for getting people to begin a trial, rather than helping informed buyers understand the value well enough to convert. That distinction is huge. More trial starts do not automatically mean a healthier business.

Another important insight involved retention. The analysis suggested an early retention leak, while also noting that churn was not yet properly measurable. This is exactly the kind of caveat a useful analysis should surface. If the measurement is weak, the answer is not to pretend the data is perfect. The answer is to fix tracking so better decisions become possible.

2. Use Gmail and Google Calendar Like an AI Executive Assistant

Connecting Gmail and Google Calendar unlocks a completely different type of automation. Instead of manually scanning your inbox, checking meetings, making a daily list, and trying to remember what matters, you can ask ChatGPT Work to review the rest of your week and tell you where to focus.

A simple request can ask it to review email and calendar commitments, identify priorities by day, flag calendar issues, and recommend work it can help complete.

The result can include a structured breakdown of:

  • Your primary focus for each day
  • Upcoming meetings or calendar conflicts that deserve attention
  • Important emails, including emails you have already read
  • Items that are urgent, worth monitoring, or require no action
  • Tasks ChatGPT Work can prepare or begin immediately

The fact that it can assess read emails matters. Unread does not always mean urgent, and read does not always mean handled. Important requests, loose commitments, and follow-ups can easily disappear into an inbox even after you opened them once.

From there, ChatGPT Work can move from diagnosis to action. It can prepare materials such as Zapier-approved scripts, recording checklists, meeting briefs, cost audits, video starters, or other project assets based on what it finds.

That is the real shift. It is not only telling you what needs attention. It can help put the work into motion.

Schedule the review so it happens automatically

You do not need to prompt this manually every Monday morning. A weekly planning review can be scheduled daily, weekly, or at whatever cadence makes sense for your work.

A recurring ChatGPT Work routine could review upcoming commitments, identify open email loops, create a daily focus plan, and prepare the documents needed to move projects forward. For anyone juggling meetings, content, clients, and team communication, that is an enormous amount of mental overhead removed.

4. Research Brandable .ai Domains at Ridiculous Speed

Here is a use case that may not apply to everyone, but it shows exactly how powerful ChatGPT Work can be.

Imagine you are building an AI skincare app. Users upload a photo of their face and receive product recommendations, routines, and skincare guidance. You need a name that feels premium, memorable, and capable of becoming a major brand. You also need to know whether the matching .ai domain is available and what it costs.

Normally, this is tedious. You brainstorm names, search domains one by one, compare availability, lose promising options, and eventually wonder how much time you just spent opening browser tabs.

ChatGPT Work can take that brief and research a broad set of possibilities. In the example, it searched through a huge number of sources and websites in under four minutes, produced name ideas, checked .ai domain availability, and surfaced prices.

That does not mean you should blindly buy the first name generated. Brand decisions still need taste, strategic judgment, and trademark diligence. But as a research and shortlisting machine, it is wild.

A stronger domain research prompt

Be specific about the business, customer, desired brand personality, domain extension, and decision criteria. Include constraints such as short names, easy pronunciation, premium positioning, or names that can support a broad future product line.

The clearer the brief, the better the shortlist. “Find a domain” is weak. “Generate premium, distinctive .ai brand names for an AI skincare recommendation app, then check availability and pricing” gives the system something real to work with.

5. Build a Cost-Effective Luxury Multi-City Travel Plan

Travel planning is another task that can become unbelievably time-consuming, especially when multiple destinations, premium cabins, hotel standards, and flexible dates are involved.

A request was made through Expedia for a trip between October and December, covering Dubai, India, and Japan. The constraints were clear: business-class flights, five-star hotels, flexibility on travel dates, and the ability to depart from anywhere in the United States if that lowered the cost.

Within a little over seven minutes, ChatGPT Work compared routes, dates, flights, hotels, and combined costs. It identified a lowest-cost itinerary travelling from San Francisco to Tokyo, then Delhi, Dubai, and New York.

That kind of route optimisation is exactly where an AI system can save serious money and serious frustration. A multi-city itinerary may look obvious at first, but the order of destinations, departure city, hotel timing, and flight availability can radically change the total.

In this case, the system found an option around $15,000, compared with a trip that had been booked for roughly $25,000. That is why it is worth asking for research before booking a big trip.

Important: Pricing and availability change constantly. Treat the output as a high-speed research starting point, then confirm flights, rooms, rules, and final prices before making any purchase.

If you create YouTube content, a weekly channel audit is one of the most practical recurring ChatGPT Work automations you can set up.

By connecting a tool such as vidIQ, you can schedule a complete channel audit every Monday at 9 a.m. The automation can review performance and produce a report that tells you what needs to improve, what content should be the focus for the week, and what opportunities you may be missing.

What a recurring YouTube audit can cover

  • An executive diagnosis of channel performance
  • The most important focus for the coming week
  • Format-by-format performance breakdowns
  • Where views are coming from
  • Retention patterns and what is working
  • Content that is underperforming
  • Priority actions to take next
  • New video opportunities
  • Older videos that should be repackaged
  • Relevant benchmarks and a clear bottom line

The best part is that this is not a one-time report. It keeps running. Each week, the process can identify new patterns, track ongoing priorities, and give you a much clearer picture of what deserves your attention.

Content creators often lose momentum because they are trying to make decisions from memory. A weekly automated audit replaces guesswork with a repeatable operating rhythm.

Use Sol for complex ChatGPT Work tasks. Terra is positioned as a lower-cost option, while Luna prioritises speed and affordability. For deeper research, sub-agent tasks, and serious automation, Sol is the recommended choice.

Can ChatGPT Work analyse business data and make recommendations?

Yes. It can analyse uploaded customer information, review website messaging, identify conversion and retention issues, highlight measurement gaps, and produce prioritised recommendations with a 90-day action plan.

Yes. A Gmail and calendar review can identify important messages even if they have already been opened. It can classify items by urgency, monitoring needs, or lack of required action.

Can I schedule recurring automations in ChatGPT Work?

Yes. Recurring tasks can be set to run daily, weekly, or on another cadence. A weekly YouTube audit or weekly email-and-calendar planning review are strong examples.
