How URL to Video Ads Work

The easiest way to understand Pollo AI is to look at the simplest use case: turning a product page into a video ad.

The process works like this:

  1. Open the marketing studio.
  2. Select the agent workflow for video ad creation.
  3. Paste in a product URL.
  4. Add a short instruction about the angle you want.
  5. Choose your settings such as length, ratio, platform, and language.
  6. Let Pollo AI pull the product metadata and assets.
  7. Review the product title, description, and selected visuals.
  8. Pick a script and avatar.
  9. Generate the ad.

That is the whole system. The platform reads the page, extracts product information, builds script options, and packages everything into a short-form ad.

One product example used was a pair of viral baseball-themed ice cream shorts. After dropping in the link, the system retrieved the title, description, and product assets. From there, it was possible to remove a few images, choose a preferred script variation, select an avatar that matched the target demographic, and generate the final ad.

This is where the tool becomes practical. You are not starting from a blank page, but you still get enough control to shape the output.

How to Get Better Results With AI Video Ads

The tool is fast, but speed alone does not guarantee a winning ad. A few smart choices improve output significantly.

1. Give a clear prompt angle

Do not just paste the product link and stop there. Tell the system what to emphasize. A discount, a pain point, a transformation, a lifestyle benefit, or a specific audience angle will usually produce stronger creative.

2. Test multiple scripts

If the platform gives you several script options, do not assume the first one is best. Pick different variations and compare them.

3. Match the avatar to the audience

The presenter should fit the product and market. That small decision can make the ad feel more natural.

4. Try more than one format

The same product may work better as a problem and solution ad than as an unboxing ad. Or the reverse. Test more than one structure.

5. Re-run the same product with different hooks

One of the biggest advantages here is volume. Use it. Generate multiple ads from the same URL and compare performance.
