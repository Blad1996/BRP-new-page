🧪 Comparing AI Models: Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI in Action

To truly understand Lovable’s power, I ran a test by asking each AI model to clone Airbnb’s homepage, matching photos and vibe as closely as possible. Here’s what I found:

Google AI

Google’s AI was the fastest to generate a simplified Airbnb clone. It produced a clean UI with a good layout but struggled to match the photos to the property descriptions and used incorrect logos and names. The resulting site was visually appealing but lacked full functionality and precise detail.

Anthropic AI

Anthropic took longer but delivered a more accurate clone. It correctly used the Airbnb logo and provided a better match of photos to listings. However, some images failed to load properly, and a few details like location visuals didn’t align perfectly. Still, Anthropic’s output looked polished and professional.

OpenAI

OpenAI was the quickest to start and gave detailed design and structure notes, along with styling information and editable files. The initial results were promising, but it didn’t follow the photo instructions as well as Anthropic. The interface was functional but required some manual fixes.

Overall Verdict: Anthropic demonstrated the best balance of accuracy and quality in this test. Google was the fastest but less detailed, and OpenAI provided a great starting point with rich information but needed further refinement.
