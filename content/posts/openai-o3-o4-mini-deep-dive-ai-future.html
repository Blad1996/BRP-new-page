Introduction to the New Models: O3 and O4 Mini 🧠

Let’s dive into the specifics of these two models. O3 stands tall as the flagship offering, boasting a larger architecture designed to tackle complex tasks across various domains. In contrast, O4 Mini, while smaller, carries the legacy of O3 into a more cost-effective and agile framework.

The naming might be a bit confusing, but it signifies a clear evolution in AI capabilities. O3 is the powerhouse, ideal for heavy-duty tasks, while O4 Mini provides a glimpse into the future with its optimized performance.

Capabilities and Features of O3 and O4 Mini ⚙️

Both models come packed with remarkable features that enhance their usability. O3 excels in coding, mathematics, and visual perception, while O4 Mini is optimized for quick reasoning and cost efficiency.

  • Complex Problem Solving: Both models can analyze intricate prompts and provide actionable insights. They can run code, create visualizations, and even perform web searches to gather more data.
  • Multimodal Input: For the first time, these models can integrate images into their reasoning processes, allowing for a richer interaction with visual content.
  • Tool Utilization: The ability to use external tools effectively is a game-changer. Both models can manipulate images and gather information seamlessly to provide detailed responses.

Benchmark Comparisons and Performance 📊

When it comes to performance, the benchmarks speak volumes. O3 demonstrates a 20% reduction in major errors compared to its predecessor, O1, particularly excelling in fields like programming and creative ideation.

In benchmark tests, O4 Mini shows impressive results, often rivaling O3, especially when tool use is incorporated. These models are not just faster; they provide more accurate and reliable outputs across various tasks.

  1. Accuracy: O4 Mini achieves a remarkable score of 98.7 in AIM 2025 when using Python tools, showcasing its prowess.
