What is DeepAgent’s Task Automation? 🤖

DeepAgent is an AI-powered platform developed by Abacus AI that enables you to automate virtually any task by simply instructing the AI what you want done. The newly introduced tasks feature allows you to schedule these AI-driven tasks to run repeatedly — daily, weekly, monthly, or on a custom schedule — without ongoing manual intervention.

Unlike traditional automation tools that require complex workflows or coding, DeepAgent’s tasks let you write simple, natural language prompts that the AI understands and executes. This means you can:

  • Create and publish content on your chosen platforms automatically
  • Generate and send detailed reports via email or Slack
  • Build AI-powered apps and dashboards
  • Conduct deep research and analysis
  • Generate presentations, blogs, newsletters, social media posts, and more

All of this happens on your preferred schedule, freeing up your time and making tedious, repetitive work a thing of the past.

How Does Task Scheduling Work? ⏰

Once you create a task in DeepAgent, you can customize it extensively to fit your needs. Here’s a breakdown of the scheduling options and controls available:

  • Frequency: Choose how often the task runs — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or at custom intervals.
  • Time of Day: Set the exact time the task should execute, down to 15-minute increments.
  • End Date: Optionally specify when the task should stop running.
  • Email Alerts: Enable notifications to be sent when a task completes or requires attention.

For example, you can set a daily task that scours the latest AI developments every morning and sends you a comprehensive report, or a weekly task that publishes a blog post every Saturday without you having to write a single word.

Getting Started with DeepAgent: How to Set Up Your First Task 🚀

Setting up your first task in DeepAgent is straightforward. Here’s a step-by-step overview:

  1. Log into DeepAgent and navigate to the Tasks section.
  2. Click on New Task and select either a normal task or a deep agent task.
  3. Write a clear prompt describing what you want the AI to do.
  4. Answer the AI’s clarifying questions to fine-tune the task.
  5. Configure the schedule, time, and any notification preferences.
  6. Test the task to see the AI’s output before activating it.
  7. Activate the task and let DeepAgent handle the rest.

Once your task is active, you can monitor results, edit instructions, or create additional tasks to automate all aspects of your workflow and life.
