What Ready AI does (in real terms)

Ready AI is an AI website builder designed around a simple idea: build the site quickly, then give you a real editor to adjust everything after the clone is generated.

In this workflow, you get three major capabilities:

  • Clone a website using a link, so the tool analyzes the site and recreates the structure and content.
  • Edit like a normal designer, including regenerating elements and applying changes (example: making text a different colour).
  • Manage the whole stack inside one place, including domain, SEO, analytics, databases, payments, booking, and communication tools.

That last part matters. Many AI site tools stop at “generate pages.” Ready AI goes further by offering built-in tools and integrations so you can actually run the business side of the website, not just display it.

How to clone a website in minutes using Ready AI

The fastest route starts with a website link. Here is the exact process, simplified.

Step 1: Choose how you want to start

When you begin in Ready AI, you can typically start in multiple ways:

  • From scratch by describing what you want to build
  • From a website link to clone an existing website
  • From a business card upload to extract basic details
  • From a template and then customize it

The important lesson from the builder is simple: your prompt (and what you provide as input) strongly influences the final output. If you care about accuracy, put extra time into the prompt you choose, and be specific about the site type, audience, and goal.

Step 2: Paste the URL to analyze and recreate

To clone a site, you:

  1. Grab the URL of the website you want to recreate.
  2. Return to Ready AI and choose the option to start with a website link.
  3. Paste the link and let Ready AI analyze it.

After you submit the link, the tool runs an analysis pass. It reads what is on the site, including content and structure, then prepares a version you can edit.

One example shown was a dental clinic site. Once the link was analyzed, Ready AI generated a plan and then built the website layout and content so it resembled the source closely.

Step 3: Pick a template path (optional, but powerful)

If cloning is not the goal, you can also start from templates. Ready AI includes many pre-built templates across categories such as:

  • Consulting services
  • Education
  • Finance and insurance
  • Local business
  • Online business

Templates are a shortcut when you want speed and structure, but cloning is often the best option when you want to replicate what is already working.

AI SEO: generate SEO settings and publish faster

After editing the design, Ready AI makes SEO setup feel like another step in the workflow, not a separate project.

Use AI to generate SEO automatically

In the site settings, you can trigger an AI automatically generate flow. The builder creates SEO-focused output such as:

  • SEO meta tags

Then, once your domain is set, publishing becomes straightforward. You can publish using the default domain first, or connect a custom domain for the “real” launch.

Appointments and lead capture that actually sync to your site

A website is only valuable if it turns into actions. Ready AI includes built-in flows for appointments and forms.

Configure appointments and enable request appointment

In the platform, you can set up an appointments section. Once it is configured, the website includes a request appointment entry point.

When someone requests an appointment, they fill in details on the website form. Then the lead appears in a backend contact form feed automatically, so you have a record of what was submitted.

This is a big deal for small businesses, because it removes the gap between “design a form” and “actually receive and manage the leads.”
