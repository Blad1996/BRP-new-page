🚀 How to Access Gemini 2.5 Pro for Free

The first question on everyone’s mind is: How do I get my hands on this new Gemini 2.5 Pro model? The process is straightforward and accessible to anyone:

  1. Go to AI Studio by Google at ai.studio.google.com. This platform provides direct access to Gemini’s latest models.
  2. Look at the top right corner of the page, where you’ll find a dropdown menu to select different models.
  3. Choose Gemini 2.5 Pro Preview, specifically the build labeled 0605, which is the most recent update as of June 5, 2024.

What’s truly remarkable is that this model is 100% free. You don’t need any special subscriptions or paid plans to use it, making it accessible for everyone—from hobbyists to professionals.

⚙️ New Features and Settings in Gemini 2.5 Pro

Once inside AI Studio, you’ll notice several new features that make Gemini 2.5 Pro stand out from previous versions:

  • Thinking Budget: You can now allocate how much “thinking time” the AI should spend on your queries, balancing speed and depth.
  • Tool Toggles: Enable or disable powerful tools like structured output, code execution, function calling, grounding with Google Search, and URL context integration.
  • Knowledge Cutoff: Gemini 2.5 Pro has a knowledge cutoff of January 2025, meaning it’s up to date with the latest information and trends.

One of the most exciting tools is grounding with Google Search, which allows Gemini to reference live Google Search results. This means it can access current information, making its responses more accurate and up-to-date.

Gemini 2.5 Pro is Google’s latest large language model (LLM) that excels at writing, coding, reasoning, and multimodal tasks. It’s the most advanced version of Gemini available publicly and is free to use via AI Studio.

How can I access Gemini 2.5 Pro for free?

You can access Gemini 2.5 Pro for free by visiting ai.studio.google.com and selecting the Gemini 2.5 Pro Preview (build 0605) from the model dropdown menu.

This model is faster, produces more human-like and nuanced writing, codes more accurately, and can analyze videos and other media content with greater understanding. It also has a knowledge cutoff of January 2025 and can ground answers in live Google Search results.
