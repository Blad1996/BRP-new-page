🚀 Feature 1: ChatGPT’s New Meeting Recording and Transcription

Imagine having ChatGPT automatically record your meetings, transcribe conversations, and even take actionable notes on your behalf. This feature is now available directly within the ChatGPT app on your computer, and it’s a game-changer for professionals who want to save time and increase efficiency.

Here’s how it works:

  1. Open the ChatGPT app on your desktop. You’ll notice a new record button available.
  2. Click the record button to start capturing your meeting or conversation. The app will transcribe everything in real time.
  3. Pause, resume, or send the recording to ChatGPT for analysis. Once sent, ChatGPT generates a comprehensive summary, highlights key points, and even suggests open-ended questions to explore further.
  4. Interact with the transcription. You can chat with ChatGPT based on the recorded content, and it will remember the context for future conversations.

Important note: Always ask for permission before recording someone else’s voice or meeting to avoid legal issues.

This feature is invaluable for professionals in meetings, interviews, or brainstorming sessions, allowing you to focus on the conversation instead of frantic note-taking. You’ll never miss a detail again, and follow-ups become seamless.

🔍 Feature 2: The Explore Feed – Discover and Remix AI Creations

Have you ever wanted to see what prompts create stunning images or videos with AI? Now, ChatGPT’s companion tool, Sora, offers an Explore feed that acts like a social media hub for your AI creations.

Here’s what makes the Explore feed exciting:

  • Curated content based on your likes and interactions. The feed shows images, videos, and other media generated by AI that you’ve engaged with.
  • Prompt transparency. Click on any image or video to see the exact prompt used to create it, helping you learn and replicate the effects.
  • Remix capabilities. Want to tweak an image or turn a still into a video? The Explore feed lets you do just that with a few clicks.
  • Discover new creative ideas. The feed is a constant stream of inspiration, showcasing the best AI-generated content from your network and beyond.

For content creators, marketers, and digital artists, this feature opens up an exciting new way to harness AI for creativity and collaboration. Instead of starting from scratch, you can build on existing ideas and prompts, accelerating your creative workflow.

🎙️ Feature 3: ChatGPT Voice Mode Takes Action Inside Apps

Voice interaction with AI has taken a huge leap forward. ChatGPT’s voice mode is no longer just for chatting — it can now take real actions inside other apps on your behalf. This is currently available on the ChatGPT desktop app, not the web app.

Here’s what you need to know:

  • Access voice mode in the ChatGPT desktop app. When you activate voice mode, you can link ChatGPT to apps like Notes, Script Editor, Terminal, and Text Edit.
  • Dictate commands or notes, and ChatGPT executes them directly. For instance, you can have ChatGPT save notes, edit scripts, or even run commands in your terminal just by speaking.
  • Get real-time feedback on your spoken content. If you’re rehearsing a script or preparing a presentation, ChatGPT can listen to your tone and suggest improvements.

This feature is perfect for multitaskers who want to stay hands-free or those who prefer speaking over typing. It also opens the door for future integrations where voice commands could automate complex workflows across many apps.

🔌 Feature 4: Connect ChatGPT to Your Favorite Apps with Connectors

Integrating AI with the tools you already use can multiply your productivity. ChatGPT now supports connectors that let you link your favorite apps directly to the AI, creating a seamless workflow.

Currently available connectors include:

  • Dropbox
  • GitHub
  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Drive
  • HubSpot
  • Outlook

Additionally, a new Developer Mode allows tech-savvy users to build custom connectors. By specifying app details, authentication methods, and server links, you can connect virtually any app to ChatGPT.

This feature is particularly exciting because it transforms ChatGPT from a standalone assistant into a central hub that can pull data, send emails, schedule meetings, or execute tasks across multiple platforms—all through natural language commands.

Note that right now, these connectors mainly work with deep research features, but future updates will expand their capabilities to handle emails, task automation, and more.

🧠 Feature 5: Enhanced Memory and Task Management in ChatGPT

ChatGPT’s memory and task management capabilities have been revamped to offer more personalized and efficient experiences.

Here’s what’s new:

  • Personalization settings. You can control whether ChatGPT references all previous conversations when responding. Keeping this on helps maintain context and continuity.
  • Saved memories management. ChatGPT stores details about you, which you can review and edit. This helps the AI tailor responses based on your preferences and history.
  • Task creation and notifications. Tasks no longer clutter your main chat interface. Instead, you manage them via the notifications panel, where you can create, edit, or delete tasks and schedule reminders.
  • Recurring tasks automation. If you regularly use ChatGPT for business leads, content ideas, or news updates, setting up recurring tasks can automate these workflows.
  • Temporary chats. You can open a private chat that doesn’t save history or update memory, ensuring your conversations remain confidential and aren’t used to train AI models.

These memory and task improvements make ChatGPT a more powerful personal assistant, capable of managing your workload, remembering important details, and respecting your privacy preferences.

📱 Feature 6: Advanced Voice Mode and Screen Sharing on Mobile

The ChatGPT mobile app has received exciting updates that make AI support even more interactive and versatile.

Key mobile features include:

  • Advanced voice mode. Beyond simple voice chatting, you can upload photos or take pictures directly within the chat and share your screen with ChatGPT. This is excellent for troubleshooting tech issues or explaining complex problems visually.
  • Camera integration. You can show ChatGPT what you see in real life, and it can identify objects, people, or even different voices. For example, it can help determine if a plant is poisonous for your pets or estimate the price of an item.
  • Voice differentiation and memory. ChatGPT can distinguish between speakers and remember the context related to each voice for future reference.

These mobile capabilities make ChatGPT a powerful companion for on-the-go assistance, visual problem-solving, and hands-free interactions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 🤖

Q1: Is the meeting recording feature available on all devices?

The recording feature is currently available on the ChatGPT desktop app. It requires explicit permission from all participants before recording to comply with legal standards.

Q2: Can I use voice mode on the ChatGPT web app?

Voice mode with app integrations is only available on the ChatGPT desktop app. The web version supports basic voice chat but lacks advanced app connections.

Q3: How do I manage my ChatGPT memory and privacy?

Within ChatGPT’s personalization settings, you can control what information the AI remembers, delete saved memories, and use temporary chats that don’t save history or update memory.

Q4: What kinds of apps can I connect to ChatGPT using connectors?

Currently, popular apps like Dropbox, Gmail, Google Drive, GitHub, and calendar apps can be connected. Developer mode allows custom app connections as well.

Q5: How secure is sharing my screen or camera with ChatGPT?

OpenAI maintains strict privacy and security protocols. However, always be cautious about sharing sensitive information, and review app permissions before enabling camera or screen sharing.
