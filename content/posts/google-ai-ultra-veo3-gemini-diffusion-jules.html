🚀 Introducing AI Ultra: The New VIP Tier of Google AI Subscriptions

Google has revamped its AI subscription plans, introducing a new premium tier called AI Ultra. This plan is designed for users who want to be at the forefront of AI innovation, offering exclusive access to the latest and most advanced tools Google has to offer.

The AI Ultra subscription is priced at $250 per month, but early adopters can start at $125 per month for the first three months. This tier includes an impressive array of features:

  • Veo 3: The latest AI video generation model with sound, dialogue, and sound effects.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro Deep Think: An advanced AI system for deep research and creative tasks.
  • Flow: An AI filmmaking tool that leverages Veo 3 for premium video creation.
  • WISK: An image-to-video creation tool based on Veo 2, with the highest usage limits.
  • NotebookLM: A powerful AI notebook with new video overview capabilities.
  • Project Mariner: Early access to Google’s agentic research assistant.
  • YouTube Premium Individual Plan and 30TB of storage across Google Photos, Drive, and Gmail.

The AI Ultra plan is clearly aimed at professionals, creators, and researchers who want the best tools for AI-driven content creation, coding, and productivity.

🎥 Veo 3: Revolutionizing AI Video Creation with Audio and Dialogue

One of the most exciting announcements is Veo 3, Google’s latest AI video model. Unlike previous versions, Veo 3 can generate videos complete with sound effects, background noises, and even dialogue. This opens up a whole new world of possibilities for creators, storytellers, and marketers who want to produce engaging video content quickly and easily.

Imagine AI-generated videos where characters can speak with different accents, sing, or even behave like cartoons. The narrative potential is enormous. For example, you could create a video of a “live tiger made out of snow sneaking through snow,” and Veo 3 can generate this scene with realistic movement and audio.

Veo 3 is integrated into the Flow AI filmmaking tool, which allows users to cycle through various AI-generated video channels or create custom projects. The Ultra subscription unlocks access to Veo 3’s full capabilities, including upscaling videos to 1080p resolution for high-quality output.

🧠 Gemini Diffusion & Jules: Next-Level AI Coding and Text Generation

Google also introduced Gemini Diffusion, a new text diffusion model that excels in generating code and text rapidly and accurately. Diffusion models have traditionally been used for image generation, so seeing one applied to working code is a remarkable development. Users have reported that Gemini Diffusion can generate functional code snippets in just seconds, making it a powerful tool for developers.

Building on this, Jules is an AI coding agent powered by Gemini 2.5 Pro. Jules works asynchronously across repositories, handling tasks like bug fixes, refactoring, and multi-task management. It integrates with GitHub and helps developers stay updated through CodeCasts, a daily podcast summarizing recent commits in a repository.

Unlike traditional coding assistants, Jules allows you to queue tasks that it processes in the background, freeing developers to focus on higher-level work. Although Jules is currently under heavy load due to high demand, it promises to be a game-changer for software development workflows.

📓 NotebookLM: Bringing Video Overviews to AI-Powered Research

Google’s NotebookLM has been a popular AI notebook tool for summarizing and organizing information. The newest update introduces video overviews in addition to audio summaries, enhancing the way users can interact with their notes and research.

While this feature is still rolling out and not widely available yet, leaked previews show NotebookLM generating video summaries that provide a dynamic and engaging alternative to static text or audio-only content. This could revolutionize how researchers, students, and professionals review complex information.

🤖 Project Mariner: The Agentic AI Research Assistant

Project Mariner is Google’s ambitious AI agent designed to autonomously perform research tasks on behalf of users. It can navigate websites, gather information, and organize findings into structured formats like notebooks or reports.

For example, you can instruct Mariner to find all the announcements from Google IO 2025, add them to your NotebookLM as a project, and generate an audio overview. During testing, Mariner demonstrated the ability to handle cookie consent banners and navigate complex web pages, although it occasionally asks for user input for such interactions.

Mariner’s capabilities are still in the research preview phase, with some bugs and limitations expected. However, it shows great promise as an assistant that can save users time by performing multi-step research tasks independently.

📱 Gemini Live: AI Assistant with Camera and Screen Sharing for Android

The Gemini Live feature brings live interaction with AI assistants to Android devices, allowing users to share their camera view or screen with the AI in real time. This enables the assistant to see what you’re looking at and provide contextual help, whether it’s reading a document, navigating an app, or answering questions about your environment.

Gemini Live is also set to integrate deeply with Google apps such as Calendar, Keep, Tasks, and Maps, making it a versatile productivity companion. Activating Gemini Live involves tapping a magical button in the bottom right corner of the Gemini app, unlocking camera and screen sharing options.

🔍 Deep Research Enhancements: Upload Your Own Files and Create Web Pages

Google’s Deep Research platform has received significant updates, including the ability to upload personal files and images directly for AI analysis. This means you can connect your Google Drive and Gmail for richer, personalized research experiences.

Another exciting feature is the ability to transform deep research reports into custom web pages with a single click. This bridges the gap between AI-generated insights and shareable, interactive web content, ideal for professionals who want to present research findings in an engaging format.

🎨 Imagen 4 and Flow: AI-Powered Image and Video Creation

Google’s Imagen 4 (sometimes pronounced “Imagine 4”) is the latest text-to-image AI, now available for free through the Gemini app. Users can generate high-quality images from text prompts, enhancing creative workflows.

Paired with Flow, the AI video editing tool, Imagen 4 feeds into a seamless ecosystem for creating both images and videos. Flow’s “TV” feature cycles through AI-generated video channels, demonstrating the breadth of creative possibilities with AI-powered media generation.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the AI Ultra subscription and who is it for?

The AI Ultra subscription is Google’s premium AI plan, priced at $250 per month (with an introductory price of $125 for the first three months). It is designed for professionals, creators, and researchers who want access to the latest AI tools, including advanced video generation, coding assistants, and agentic AI research capabilities.

How does Veo 3 improve upon previous AI video models?

Veo 3 adds the ability to generate videos with synchronized sound effects, dialogue, and background noises. This creates more immersive and realistic videos compared to earlier versions that lacked audio or had limited sound capabilities.

What makes Gemini Diffusion special for coding?

Gemini Diffusion is a text diffusion model that can generate working code snippets extremely quickly and accurately. This is notable because diffusion models were traditionally used for images, making this a novel application in AI coding assistance.

What is Jules and how can it help developers?

Jules is an AI coding agent that works asynchronously across code repositories. It can handle multiple tasks like fixing bugs and refactoring code, allowing developers to delegate routine work and focus on complex problems.

How does Project Mariner assist with research?

Project Mariner is an agentic AI that can autonomously browse the web, gather information, and organize it into structured formats like notebooks or reports. It reduces manual effort in research by performing multi-step tasks independently.

What is Gemini Live and how does it work?

Gemini Live is an AI assistant feature for Android that allows users to share their camera or screen in real time. The AI can then analyze what it sees and provide contextual help, making it a powerful tool for productivity and troubleshooting.

Can I use Google Deep Research with my own files?

Yes, recent updates to Deep Research allow users to upload their own files and images and connect services like Google Drive and Gmail for personalized research and AI analysis.

Are these AI tools available to everyone?

Many of the tools are currently in research preview or available through the AI Ultra subscription tier. Some features, like video overviews in NotebookLM, are still rolling out and may not be widely accessible yet.
