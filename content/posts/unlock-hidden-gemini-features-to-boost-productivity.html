🔍 Feature #1: Share Your Screen with Gemini for Real-Time Support

One of the most underrated and powerful features of Gemini is the ability to share your screen directly with the AI. Imagine having a 24/7 technical support agent who can see exactly what you see and provide instant help. Whether you’re stuck editing a video, confused about an Excel formula, or need guidance on paying a bill online, screen sharing with Gemini can save you hours of frustration.

Here’s how it works: Simply go to AI Studio at studio.google.com, navigate to the Stream section, and select Share Screen. You can also share your webcam or talk to Gemini live, making it an interactive and immersive support experience. This feature is completely free and works both on desktop and the Gemini mobile app, where you can open the app, click the live interaction button, and share your screen or camera feed.

Whether you’re troubleshooting software issues or learning a new tool like CapCut, Gemini’s screen sharing acts like an expert assistant right beside you. This feature is a game changer for anyone who frequently needs help with digital tasks.

📅 Feature #2: Automate Your Day with the Productivity Planner

Stop wasting time manually organizing your tasks and emails. Gemini’s Productivity Planner is designed to boost your efficiency by automating daily and weekly work briefs. By connecting Gemini with your Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, and other productivity apps, you can receive a personalized summary every morning outlining your meetings, action items, and important emails.

Setting this up is easy. In AI Studio, click on the Productivity Planner and select “Create Brief for Me Every Morning.” Gemini will analyze your calendar, emails, documents, and tasks to generate a comprehensive overview of your day. This daily briefing can be scheduled to arrive automatically so you never miss a critical update.

For example, I use this feature to get a morning snapshot of my schedule, top priorities, and even YouTube script reminders. It’s like having a personal assistant who preps you for the day ahead without you lifting a finger. You can also export your briefs to Google Sheets for further customization and tracking.

🎞️ Feature #3: Turn Photos into Animated Videos

This feature is nothing short of magical. Gemini can transform static photos into lively videos where the subject in the image talks, tells stories, or even jokes. Imagine taking a photo of a grizzly bear and having it narrate a funny story or share an anecdote.

To try this, upload a photo in the Video section of AI Studio, then prompt Gemini to create a video based on that image. It takes a few minutes for Gemini to process and animate the photo, subtly altering it to bring it to life. The results are impressive and have endless creative applications—from marketing content and social media posts to personal storytelling.

This technology is rapidly evolving, and what you see today is just the beginning of immersive AI-driven video experiences.

⏰ Feature #5: Schedule Recurring Tasks to Automate Your Workflow

Gemini isn’t just for answering questions and creating content—it’s a powerful automation engine. By enabling various apps like GitHub, Google Workspace, travel tools, and media apps within Gemini, you can schedule tasks to run daily, weekly, or monthly.

For instance, you can set Gemini to research the latest AI tools every day, draft a script in Google Docs, add tasks to your Google Tasks list, and even create calendar invites to review your work—all automatically. Scheduling these actions saves tremendous time and keeps your workflow seamless.

In AI Studio, navigate to Scheduled Actions, describe the task you want Gemini to perform, set the frequency and time, and let the AI handle the rest. This level of integration turns Gemini into a personal productivity powerhouse.

🛠️ Feature #6: Build Custom Apps Powered by Gemini’s API

For developers and creators, Gemini offers an incredible Build feature where you can create custom apps directly within the AI Studio environment. What makes this truly powerful is the ability to connect these apps to Google’s API and leverage Gemini’s capabilities in unique ways.

For example, I built a YouTube video feedback app that allows me to upload videos and receive detailed, actionable feedback on how to improve them. This is possible because Gemini can analyze YouTube videos natively, thanks to Google’s ownership of the platform.

Besides video feedback, there are apps for text-to-speech, image generation, URL context tools, music creation, and more—all powered by Gemini’s API. You can browse existing apps for inspiration or create your own tailored to your specific needs. This opens up a world of possibilities for automating complex tasks and building AI-powered solutions.

⚖️ Feature #7: Compare Gemini Models Side-by-Side

Gemini lets you compare different AI models to see which one performs best for your use case. In AI Studio, you can select any two Gemini models and run the same query against both, comparing speed, accuracy, and depth of response.

For example, I asked both a “flashlight” model and the “Gemini 2.5 Pro” model about the best cryptocurrency to buy right now. The flashlight model responded much faster but with less depth, while Gemini 2.5 Pro provided a detailed, thoughtful analysis, albeit more slowly.

This feature is invaluable when you want to optimize for speed versus thoroughness, depending on your task. It also gives you insight into how different AI architectures handle complex questions.

📝 Feature #8: Create Dynamic Content with Canvas Integration

Gemini’s integration with Canvas allows you to transform AI-generated research and answers into rich, editable content formats. For example, after asking Gemini a question like “What is the average car payment in the US?” you can instruct Gemini to turn the response into a Canvas document.

Once in Canvas, you can adjust the length, tone, and style of the content, suggest edits, or even create web pages, infographics, quizzes, or podcasts from the same information. This feature streamlines content creation and helps you repurpose AI output across multiple formats effortlessly.

📧 Feature #9: Gemini Embedded Across Google Workspace

Gemini is no longer confined to AI Studio—it’s integrated across Google’s suite of apps, making it accessible wherever you work. In Gmail, Drive, YouTube, Excel, Sheets, and Docs, you can find Gemini-powered tools that assist with writing, summarizing, researching, and more.

This seamless integration means you don’t have to switch apps or platforms to leverage Gemini’s AI power. Whether you’re drafting emails, analyzing data, or creating presentations, Gemini is right there to help you work smarter.

🔎 Feature #10: Use Google’s AI Mode for Smarter Searches

Google’s new AI Mode search is powered by Gemini and offers a revolutionary way to find answers online. Instead of sifting through multiple links, AI Mode performs the searches for you, synthesizes the information, and delivers concise, actionable responses.

You can even interact with AI Mode using voice commands or typed questions to refine your search. For example, you can ask follow-up questions like “Which option is the cheapest?” and get direct answers without visiting every website.

This feature changes the way we consume information online, making research faster and more efficient.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ❓

What is Gemini, and how is it different from other AI assistants?

Gemini is Google’s advanced AI assistant integrated across multiple platforms. Unlike other AI tools, it offers unique features like screen sharing, app building, and deep integration with Google Workspace, making it highly versatile and productivity-focused.

How can I start using Gemini’s hidden features?

Most features are accessible through AI Studio at studio.google.com or directly within Google apps like Gmail and Drive. To unlock automation and app-building capabilities, enable relevant apps within AI Studio and explore the scheduled actions and build sections.

Is screen sharing with Gemini secure?

Yes, screen sharing is private and secure, designed to help you get personalized assistance without compromising your data. Always ensure you trust the device and network you’re using for screen sharing.

Can I use Gemini to automate tasks outside of Google apps?

While Gemini integrates deeply with Google Workspace, it also supports connections to other platforms like GitHub and various media and travel apps, allowing for broad automation possibilities.

Does Gemini support voice interaction?

Yes, Gemini supports live voice interaction, letting you talk to the AI directly via the AI Studio or mobile app for a more natural and hands-free experience.
