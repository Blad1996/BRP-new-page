Tips & Tricks 🎨

To get the most out of OpenAI's image generation model, consider these tips:

  • Be Descriptive: The more specific your prompt, the better the result. Instead of saying "a dog," try "a golden retriever wearing sunglasses on a beach."
  • Experiment with Styles: Don’t hesitate to specify artistic styles. Phrases like "in the style of Van Gogh" or "as a comic book illustration" can yield fascinating results.
  • Iterate on Your Creations: Use the editing features to refine your images. Adjust colors, add elements, or change backgrounds to enhance your final product.
  • Combine Elements: Merge multiple ideas in one prompt. For instance, "a cat riding a skateboard in a futuristic city" can result in a unique and engaging image.
  • Use Context: Leverage the model's ability to remember context. If you're working on a series of images, keep your prompts connected for cohesive results.
