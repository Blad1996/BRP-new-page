Toronto businesses are primary targets for ransomware and phishing scams. Agri-businesses are also increasingly targeted as hackers look for vulnerabilities in food supply chains.

  • The Risk: An entry-level antivirus is not enough to stop a coordinated cyberattack.

4. You Are Struggling to Integrate New Technology

As businesses grow, they often end up with a "Frankenstein" system—different softwares and hardwares that don't talk to each other.

With Canadian privacy laws (like PIPEDA) becoming more stringent, how you handle client and financial data is under a microscope.

  • The Sign: If you can't confidently answer where your data is stored, who has access to it, or how quickly you could recover it after a disaster, you are at risk of heavy fines and a ruined reputation.
