What Is Pollo Agent 2.0?

Pollo Agent 2.0 is an AI-powered workflow inside Pollo AI that helps create videos through predefined skills and natural-language instructions. Instead of starting with an empty editing timeline, you start with a goal.

That goal might be:

  • Turn a product URL into a video ad.
  • Turn a product photo into a product showcase.
  • Create a UGC-style testimonial video.
  • Build a story-driven short drama.
  • Recreate the format and pacing of an existing ad using a different product.
  • Import a website and build a reusable brand kit.
  • Create a virtual try-on image or video.

The platform’s skills are essentially standard operating procedures. They provide a structured creative process for a specific outcome, which is important because repeatability matters. You do not want every video to feel random. You want a consistent output that matches the type of asset you are trying to create.

There are skills for creative categories such as romance, adventure, anime, marketing, e-commerce, virtual try-ons, and more. You can also browse inspiration, choose something you like, click recreate, and swap in your own product, model, image, or visual vibe.

One Important Setting: Specify Seedance 2.5

If you want to use Seedance 2.5 inside Pollo Agent, make that request explicitly. The agent may otherwise choose another available model, such as Hailuo or SD 2 Mini, because Seedance 2.5 uses more credits.

A simple instruction like the one below can make the preference clear:

“Use Seedance 2.5 for this video.”

This matters most when visual consistency is critical. Product ads, story content, and videos with recurring characters all benefit from stronger consistency between scenes. The goal is to avoid the common AI video problem where an item, outfit, character, or product changes from one shot to the next.

Four Core Ways to Create Content

Pollo Agent gives you multiple starting points, depending on what assets you already have.

1. Photo to Video

Upload a photo and turn it into a video. This can be a product image, a character image, or another visual asset. It is a fast option when you do not have a full product shoot or a stack of creative materials ready to go.

For a product, the agent can create a visual showcase around the image. For a character, it can use that asset as the basis for a more cinematic or social-first piece of content.

2. URL to Video Ad

This is one of the biggest features for e-commerce. Paste a URL from Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, or TikTok Shop, and the agent can pull product information and images from the listing before building the ad.

From one link, it can develop:

  • A creative brief
  • Product-focused scenes
  • Script direction
  • Visual concepts
  • Video pacing
  • A finished ad asset

You can then choose whether to use all product images pulled from the listing, use only selected images, or have the system choose the strongest assets.

3. Script to Video Ad

If you already have a concept or a script direction, you can turn that into an ad. This gives you more control over the message while still avoiding the manual labor of creating every scene, finding footage, recording voiceovers, and editing clips together.

4. UGC Video Ads

UGC-style video ads are built around the feel of a spokesperson review, testimonial, or creator recommendation. You can use product information alongside a character asset or avatar to generate the style of content that often feels more native to social platforms than a polished traditional commercial.

The key is that you can create the structure of a UGC creative without needing to coordinate with creators, wait for deliveries, review raw footage, or pay for every new variation.

Use Case #1: Create Short Story Content and AI Dramas

Short-form dramas have become a major content category on TikTok, with examples reaching millions and even tens of millions of views. There is a huge appetite for compact, emotional, cinematic stories that create curiosity fast and give people a reason to keep following the story.

Pollo Agent can help generate this type of content through its story video tools. You can start from an existing inspiration template, recreate the structure, and adapt elements such as the characters, visual style, identity, and storyline.

A romantic cinematic story, for example, can be planned out with production parameters before generation begins. The agent can prepare:

  • The visual style
  • Character identities
  • A screenplay structure
  • Scene-by-scene direction
  • Generation settings
  • The selected video model

You can review the plan, make changes where needed, and approve it before the scenes are produced. That approval step is useful because it gives you visibility into the direction before credits are spent generating final clips.

When using Seedance 2.5, the major advantage is character consistency. Expressions, voices, smiles, timing, and the overall appearance of the characters can remain more coherent through the different shots. That is exactly what story content needs. A drama falls apart quickly if the lead character looks completely different every time the scene changes.

The other big advantage is speed. Instead of handling casting, scripting, scene planning, production, voice work, and editing one piece at a time, you can create multiple story concepts far more quickly. That makes it possible to test different emotional hooks and build a volume of short-form content without a full studio setup.

Use Case #2: Turn a Product URL Into a Video Ad

For e-commerce brands, URL to video ads may be the most immediately useful feature. A product page already contains the raw materials needed for a basic ad: images, product details, branding, and an offer or positioning angle.

With Pollo Agent, you can paste a product URL, ask for Seedance 2.5 if that is your preference, select the product ad preset, and let the system build the creative direction.

In the ice cream shorts example, the agent pulled information directly from the Amazon listing, identified the available images, and created a creative brief. From there, it could select the strongest product images and generate an ad around the product.

This is useful for product launches, seasonal campaigns, new colourways, limited releases, and ad testing. A clothing brand could create a teaser for a new line without organizing a new shoot for every concept.

What stands out is the product consistency. Starting from a single image of the shorts, the generated creative was able to represent the item convincingly across different views. The front, back, and branding remained aligned with the source product, rather than turning into a completely different garment.

That is a big deal. Many AI video tools can create interesting movement, but the real challenge is preserving the product. If an ad changes the logo, colour, shape, or details of what you are selling, it is not useful. The product needs to remain recognizable.

Once a product showcase is created, you can make variations by adding instructions in the chat. You might change the product movement, visual styling, background colours, or the details designed to grab attention. You are not forced to restart from scratch every time you want a new creative angle.

Use Case #3: Clone the Style of a Proven Video Ad

This feature is honestly the one that can really break your brain.

A clone video ad lets you upload your product image and use a reference ad or video as the creative foundation. The purpose is not merely to swap an image. The system can recreate the style, hook, pacing, composition, character direction, and overall format of the reference creative with your own product inserted into it.

This is incredibly useful when you already have a format that works. Maybe one of your own ads performed well. Maybe there is a creative format that inspires you. Instead of guessing what kind of video structure could work, you can work from a proven direction and adapt it.

In the Bloom Energy example, a reference video featuring Starbucks products was recreated using a Bloom Energy drink image. The resulting version retained the reference video’s overall setup while replacing the original product and branding with Bloom Energy.

That means you can potentially change:

  • The product
  • The packaging and brand identity
  • The characters
  • The visual mood
  • The offer or messaging

All without rebuilding the whole production process, re-recording scenes, or hiring another crew. For anyone who has spent years analysing winning ads, hooks, and creative patterns, the speed here is hard to ignore. A process that once required extensive research and production can be approached in seconds through a structured AI workflow.

Of course, the best use of this feature is to build on creative inspiration and formats you have the right to use. Your own winning ads are a great place to start because you already know the structure resonates with your audience.

Use Case #4: Import Your Website and Create a Brand Kit

Video generation gets much more useful when the creative actually looks like your brand. That is where the brand kit feature comes in.

You can enter your website URL and import your brand information. The agent can detect and pull assets such as:

  • Brand colours
  • Images
  • Logos
  • Taglines
  • Brand values

If you do not have a website, these details can also be entered manually. Once the kit is created, you can use it as the foundation for future creative requests.

For example, after importing the ContentBuddy AI website, the system identified the branding and then created a 50% off coupon ad. The resulting ad reflected the site’s red and blue visual identity, included the logo, and even incorporated social media icons.

This is a huge upgrade from creating disconnected one-off AI videos. With a reusable brand kit, your campaigns can maintain visual alignment across product ads, promotional offers, social content, and future experiments.

Virtual Try-On Videos and Photos

Virtual try-on is another strong option for fashion, accessories, and product-led brands. You can select a model, upload a product image, and have the tool place the product into the scene.

A bucket hat example shows how simple this can be. Start from a virtual try-on inspiration video, click recreate, remove the existing product image, upload a bucket hat, and let the skill rebuild the concept around the new item.

The result can include a generated model wearing the product, with the product logo and visual details represented clearly. No model booking. No product shoot. No camera setup. No editing timeline.

You still have the ability to customize the vibe, model, styling, and direction. But you are no longer stuck doing every technical step yourself.

Why This Changes the AI Video Workflow

The real shift is not that AI can generate a clip. Plenty of tools can do that. The shift is that an agent can take a business goal and manage the workflow around it.

Instead of needing separate tools for writing, image generation, avatars, voice, motion, editing, and creative planning, Pollo Agent brings those steps into one place.

That means:

  • No prompt engineering required for basic workflows
  • No need to write every scene from scratch
  • No need to manually assemble clips on a timeline
  • No need for a camera or actor for every new concept
  • No need to pay thousands of dollars for every test ad
  • No need to stop at one version of a creative

If you have been sitting on a product, a photo, or an idea because you cannot edit, cannot film, or do not want to spend agency-level money on a single ad, that roadblock is getting a lot smaller.
