🚀 Automate Your Workflow with Scheduled Actions in Gemini

One of the most exciting new capabilities in Google Gemini is the ability to schedule tasks that run automatically at specific times. This feature is a game changer for anyone who wants to streamline repetitive workflows and stay on top of their daily priorities without manual intervention.

Here’s how it works:

  • From the Gemini web app, navigate to the Settings menu located in the bottom-left corner.
  • Click on Scheduled Actions to view and manage all your automated tasks.
  • To create a new scheduled action, simply provide a prompt like: "Please create me a scheduled action to send me a daily update of my calendar, to-dos, and important unread emails at 8 AM."

Gemini then leverages its internal scheduling tool (called scheduler.schedule) to set up this action, which you can easily pause, edit, or delete at any time. You can customize how often it runs—daily, weekly, monthly—and even specify increments as fine as every 15 minutes.

This automation is incredibly powerful because Gemini integrates seamlessly with a wide range of Google apps, including:

  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Docs
  • Google Drive
  • Google Keep
  • Google Tasks
  • Google Flights
  • Google Hotels
  • Google Maps
  • YouTube and YouTube Music
  • GitHub
  • OpenStack

Imagine waking up every morning with a neatly compiled summary of your day’s events, your to-do list, and unread emails delivered automatically. Or picture Gemini tracking your competitors’ latest YouTube content and sending you regular insights to help you stay ahead. You can even automate travel planning by having Gemini monitor flight and hotel prices for your upcoming trips.

For example, linking Google Maps with your Google Calendar lets Gemini provide directions and travel time updates for your meetings, saving you time and hassle.

Pro Tip: When creating scheduled actions, always start your prompt with "Please create me a scheduled action" to ensure Gemini understands your request properly. Plan your scheduled tasks around the apps Gemini can access to unlock its full potential.

🖼️ Create Stunning Images Instantly with ImageGen 4 in AI Studio

Google has also upgraded its image generation capabilities by launching ImageGen 4 inside AI Studio—an entirely free platform that lets you generate high-quality images using AI.

Here’s how to get started:

  1. Head over to AI Studio and navigate to the Generate Media section.
  2. Select ImageGen, where you’ll find options for ImageGen 4 Preview and ImageGen 4 Ultra Preview.
  3. Enter your image prompt—for example, "Please generate a hyper-realistic image of a beautiful modern home in Montana."
  4. Watch as ImageGen 4 produces your requested image in seconds.

When compared to other AI models like ChatGPT’s image generation, ImageGen 4 Ultra Preview is significantly faster and more efficient. In real-time tests, ImageGen completed image creation well before ChatGPT even started processing.

The interface offers handy features such as:

  • Downloading or copying images
  • Exporting directly to Google Drive
  • Adjusting aspect ratios
  • Providing feedback on image quality

While ImageGen 4 only generates one image per prompt, older versions like ImageGen 3 allow you to create multiple variations—though the quality and speed of ImageGen 4 make it the recommended choice.

This tool is perfect for marketers, designers, or content creators who want to generate custom visuals quickly without the need for expensive software or design skills.

💻 Gemini CLI: Your AI Agent in the Terminal

Developers and tech enthusiasts will be thrilled by the release of Gemini CLI, a lightweight, open-source AI agent that brings Gemini’s power directly into your terminal environment.

This means you can now automate complex coding and operational tasks without leaving your command line interface.

Here’s what Gemini CLI offers:

  • Query and edit large codebases that exceed Gemini’s one million token context window.
  • Generate new applications from PDFs or sketches using Gemini’s advanced multimodal capabilities.
  • Automate operational tasks such as pulling requests, performing complex rebases, or running security and performance audits.
  • Integrate with other tools and servers to extend Gemini’s functionality—for example, media generation with ImageGen or music creation with Lyra.
  • Ground queries with the built-in Google Search tool for real-time information.

To get started, visit the Gemini CLI GitHub repository, where you’ll find a detailed README with quick start instructions, advanced usage tips, and example commands.

For those new to coding, the documentation breaks down everything step-by-step, making it accessible even if you’re not a developer.

Some popular use cases for Gemini CLI include:

  • Writing custom Discord bots that answer FAQs
  • Cloning GitHub projects and summarizing recent changes
  • Generating comprehensive documentation
  • Conducting dependency analysis and modernization of codebases
  • Streamlining development workflows

By combining Gemini CLI with large language models (LLMs) like GPT, Claude, or Grock, you can expand your automation capabilities even further. For example, you might ask Gemini to generate ideas tailored specifically to your role—whether you’re a marketing manager, software engineer, or product owner.

Pro Tip: Use Gemini 2.5 Flash for faster, more responsive interactions, as the Pro version tends to overthink and slow down the process.

🧠 New AI Models in Google AI Studio: Gamma 3 NE 2B and 4B

Google has also introduced two new open-source AI models in AI Studio under the “Gamma” series: Gamma 3 NE 2B and Gamma 3 NE 4B. These models are designed to be hosted locally, but Google allows you to experiment with them directly in AI Studio for rapid prototyping and testing.

While these models may not be as polished or user-friendly as Gemini 2.5 Pro or Flash, they represent a significant step towards more customizable AI solutions that developers can fine-tune and deploy on their own machines.

For most users, continuing to use Gemini 2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash, or the Flash Light Preview (released June 17) will provide the best balance of performance and usability.
