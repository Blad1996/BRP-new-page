🚀 Auto-Suggest Follow-Up Prompts: Keep the Conversation Flowing

One of the most overlooked but incredibly productive features in ChatGPT is the ability to automatically generate follow-up suggestions during your conversations. By enabling auto-suggest in your settings, ChatGPT can propose next steps or related questions every time you interact with it, helping you dig deeper and explore new ideas without losing momentum.

To activate this, simply navigate to the Suggestions section and ensure the following are turned on:

  • Auto Suggest
  • Generate Suggestion Edits
  • Auto Apply (optional – can be annoying if enabled)

For example, if you ask ChatGPT how to automate responses to social media comments and DMs using AI, it won’t just give you the answer. At the end, it might say, “If you want, I can help you build a working prototype agent with browser automation to do this. Just say the word, and I'll walk you through it.” This proactive approach keeps the conversation alive and pushes you toward actionable results.

This feature is perfect if you want to brainstorm, plan projects, or even develop AI-powered tools without having to think of every follow-up question yourself. It’s like having an AI partner that’s always thinking ahead for you.

🤖 Agent Mode: Let ChatGPT Take Action on Your Behalf

Agent Mode is a revolutionary addition to ChatGPT that takes automation to the next level. Instead of just providing answers, you can now have ChatGPT perform tasks for you across various platforms and applications. This means your AI assistant can handle reports, manage spreadsheets, create presentations, and much more without you lifting a finger.

Here’s how it works:

  • Open the Tools section and select Agent Mode.
  • Choose from a list of suggested actions tailored to your needs, such as generating reports or managing data.
  • Watch as ChatGPT executes these tasks step-by-step, or review the prompts it uses to understand the process.

What’s even more impressive is that you can schedule these actions to run automatically at specific times. For example, you could have ChatGPT review your emails every morning or update your calendar without manual input.

To enable this, connect your favorite tools through the Connectors settings—whether it’s Gmail, Google Drive, HubSpot, Notion, or other platforms. Once connected, Agent Mode can seamlessly interact with these services to automate your workflow.

This feature is a game-changer for busy professionals who want to delegate repetitive tasks and focus on higher-value work. Imagine running a business where AI handles customer follow-ups, data entry, and scheduling autonomously—that’s the power of Agent Mode.

📅 Scheduling Tasks: Automate Your Workflow Like a Pro

Pairing Agent Mode with ChatGPT’s built-in scheduler allows you to fully automate your daily routine. Instead of manually prompting ChatGPT every time you need a task done, you can set up recurring schedules that keep your projects moving forward without interruption.

For example, you might schedule ChatGPT to:

  • Review and summarize your emails each morning.
  • Generate weekly reports or presentations.
  • Follow up on pending tasks or meetings.

To manage schedules, go to Settings > Schedules > Manage. Here, you can create, edit, and track all your automated workflows. This level of automation frees up your time and ensures nothing slips through the cracks.

Businesses that have embraced these AI scheduling capabilities report significant growth and efficiency improvements. With AI handling routine tasks, teams can focus on creativity, strategy, and innovation.

🎤 Meeting Recording & Summarization: Capture Every Detail Effortlessly

Another standout new feature is the ability to record meetings directly through the ChatGPT desktop app. Instead of relying on separate meeting tools, you can now talk to ChatGPT during your calls or presentations, and it will record, transcribe, and summarize everything for you.

Once you stop recording, ChatGPT generates:

  • A concise summary of the meeting
  • Key points discussed
  • Action items to follow up on
  • Open questions that remain

Because this feature integrates with ChatGPT’s suggestion engine, you’ll also receive follow-up prompts to keep projects moving. For example, it can remind you of pending action items from previous meetings or help you draft emails to stakeholders based on discussion points.

This functionality is invaluable for professionals who attend frequent meetings, ensuring nothing is missed and boosting accountability across teams. With memory enabled, ChatGPT can reference past meetings to provide context-aware assistance.

📚 Study & Learn Mode: Your 24/7 AI Tutor

Learning just got a major upgrade thanks to ChatGPT’s new Study and Learn mode. This feature uses the Socratic method to teach you by asking thought-provoking questions, helping you deepen your understanding of complex topics.

Here’s how it works:

  • Open a new chat and select Study and Learn from the tools menu.
  • Enter a topic you want to learn about (e.g., “Building AI agents with Nanonets”).
  • ChatGPT will ask you questions about your experience and understanding to tailor the lesson.
  • You engage in an interactive Q&A that reinforces learning and clarifies confusion.

This mode is perfect for students, professionals, or hobbyists who want a personalized tutor available anytime. You can even study while walking, driving, or doing chores—no need to wait for scheduled lessons.

Contrary to fears that AI will replace teachers, this tool serves as an augmentation, providing continuous support alongside traditional education methods. It empowers learners to explore topics at their own pace and with deeper engagement.

🔒 Personalization & Connectors: Unlock ChatGPT’s Full Potential

Two foundational features you must enable to truly leverage ChatGPT in 2025 are Memories and Connectors.

Memories allow ChatGPT to remember your preferences, past conversations, and context, enabling it to provide more personalized and relevant responses. This feature sets ChatGPT apart from other AI models like Claude, Gemini, or Grok, which lack persistent memory capabilities.

Connectors let you link ChatGPT to external services such as Gmail, Google Drive, HubSpot, and Notion. When combined with Agent Mode, this integration enables powerful automation workflows that can run your entire business or personal projects with minimal manual input.

For instance, a friend of Rob’s has connected his Gmail, HubSpot, and Google Drive accounts to ChatGPT. Using Agent Mode and these connectors, he has automated his whole business, generating close to a million dollars a year passively by delivering AI-powered services.

Additionally, you can add custom API servers to extend ChatGPT’s functionality even further, with detailed guides available to help you set this up.
