💻 AI for Coding: From Simple Scripts to Complex Apps

Coding is arguably one of the most powerful applications of AI right now. Whether you want to automate repetitive tasks or build full-fledged applications, AI can speed up development and help you overcome knowledge gaps.

For example, I recently asked ChatGPT to create a drawing app that uses hand tracking via my camera as the user interface. Using the OpenAI GPT-4 API (you’ll need a paid account for this level of access), it generated the code in under three minutes. The app lets me select colors by making a peace sign with my hand and draw freely on the screen. This simple project took just 30 seconds to create once I had the code—showcasing how AI can accelerate prototyping and development.

My colleague Alex uses AI to generate scripts that manage file organization for his video editing workflow. He simply describes his folder structure and what he wants to delete or keep, and AI creates custom scripts that save him hours of manual work. For example, Alex asked AI to delete raw footage folders older than one month while preserving recent files. AI generated a script that he runs safely on his system.

These examples scratch the surface of AI’s coding potential. Whether you need simple automation, productivity tools, or complex applications, AI can be your coding partner.

📄 Contract and Legal Document Review: Save Time and Money

Contracts can be dense and intimidating, especially if you’re not a legal expert. I use ChatGPT to review NDAs, job offers, and other agreements before consulting a lawyer. This approach saves me time and reduces unnecessary legal fees.

For instance, when I receive an NDA, I upload the PDF and ask ChatGPT to identify any nonstandard or problematic clauses. It points out issues like indefinite confidentiality, vague use restrictions, or missing governing law clauses. AI even suggests how to negotiate these terms with the counterparty, providing language you can use to redline the contract. This empowers you to negotiate confidently without legal jargon holding you back.

Negotiation is another area where AI excels. Many people find negotiating difficult or uncomfortable, and AI can help draft polite yet firm negotiation emails. For example, if you receive a job offer but want a higher salary or better equity, AI can generate a professional response tailored to your experience and market benchmarks. This makes negotiation accessible and less stressful.

🌱 Life Advice and Decision Making with AI

One of the most underutilized AI use cases is life advice and personal decision-making. Despite accounting for only about 2% of AI usage according to Anthropic, I find this to be incredibly valuable. AI can help you think through complex life choices by asking the right questions and presenting options you might not have considered.

For example, I recently debated whether to move from Los Angeles to the Bay Area. I uploaded a detailed prompt explaining my situation: ten years in the Bay, currently building an AI-focused media company, a new house in LA with an ADU for my parents, cost of living concerns, and more. AI responded with thoughtful questions about networking needs, housing budgets, school priorities, commute times, and family logistics.

By answering these questions, I received a nuanced analysis weighing pros and cons, including commute times to AI events, cost implications, and social factors. This helped me clarify what matters most and explore alternatives like quarterly trips instead of a full relocation. You can even ask AI to role-play different perspectives—like a pessimist or optimist—to get balanced viewpoints.

📸 Image Analysis: Identify, Diagnose, and Learn

AI’s ability to analyze images is another powerful tool. I often take pictures of plants to diagnose issues or broken household items to get repair suggestions. For instance, I uploaded a photo of an apple and asked ChatGPT to identify its type. It correctly identified it as a Gala apple, describing its color and striping.

Image analysis also extends to geolocation. I tested this by uploading a Google Maps photo of Catalina Island. AI accurately identified the location as Little Harbor on Santa Catalina Island, California, even providing exact coordinates. This geo-guessing feature can be fun or practical when you need to identify unknown places from photos.

Transcription is another invaluable feature. I use a digital writing tablet called reMarkable to jot down notes, then upload PDFs of my handwritten notes to ChatGPT for transcription. AI accurately converts them into readable text, saving me time on manual typing.

FAQ 🤖

Q: Do I need a paid ChatGPT account to use these AI features?

A: Some advanced features, like coding with GPT-4 or deep research, require a paid subscription. However, many use cases such as basic search, writing assistance, and image analysis can be done with free models or open source alternatives.

Q: Can AI replace professionals like lawyers or doctors?

A: AI is a helpful tool for initial understanding and advice but should never replace professional consultation. Always verify legal documents and medical diagnoses with qualified experts.

Q: How accurate is AI for translation and transcription?

A: AI translations and transcriptions are generally accurate and directionally correct but may not capture every nuance. For critical documents, professional human translation is recommended.

Q: Is AI safe to use for personal data and sensitive information?

A: Always be cautious when uploading sensitive data. Use trusted platforms and understand their privacy policies. For highly confidential information, consider on-premise or private AI models.

Q: How can I improve my AI prompt writing skills?

A: Start simple and use AI itself to help craft better prompts. Specify your goals clearly, provide context, and iterate based on AI responses. Many online resources and communities offer prompt engineering tips.
