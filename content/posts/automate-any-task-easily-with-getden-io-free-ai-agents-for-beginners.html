How Does GetDen.io Work? 🛠️

When you log into your getden.io homepage, you’ll see all your active AI agents running in different workspaces. You can organize these workspaces by department or function—such as content creation, customer service, email management, onboarding, and more. If you’re working with a team, you can invite collaborators to your workspace for full transparency and joint development of AI agents.

Creating a new AI agent is straightforward. You click “New Agent,” give it a name, and then describe its goal in plain English. You can specify the integrations it should connect with and outline the steps it needs to follow. For example, you might want an agent that rewrites blog posts, summarizes calendar events, or monitors customer metrics.

GetDen.io also lets you configure the AI model it uses—currently featuring the latest GPT-4 and Claude models—and set schedules or triggers for when the automation should run. You can even add custom knowledge or context to tailor the agent’s behavior to your specific needs.

Building an AI Agent: A Step-by-Step Example 📝

Let’s say you want to create an agent that takes existing blog posts and rewrites them into fresh versions. Here’s how you’d do it:

  1. Name the agent “Blog Post Agent.”
  2. Describe the goal: “Analyze blog posts and create new rewritten versions.”
  3. Specify how the blog post will be provided (e.g., via URL to scrape).
  4. Decide what to do with the rewritten version (e.g., email it to yourself).
  5. Answer a few clarifying questions the AI asks to ensure it understands your needs.

Within seconds, the platform generates a complete agent workflow, including detailed instructions, integrations, and notes. The agent scrapes the blog post content from the URL, analyzes the tone and message, rewrites it with fresh wording while maintaining SEO elements, and emails the final version to you.

You can run the agent manually or schedule it to run automatically, for example, whenever a new blog post is published on your website.

Top 5 Insane Automations You Need to Set Up Now ⚡

Rob highlights five incredible AI automation use cases that can transform how you work. These automations not only save time but also unlock new opportunities to leverage AI’s power in your daily tasks.

1. Daily Calendar Summary with External Research 📅

Imagine waking up to a personalized summary of your entire day’s meetings—complete with background research on every external participant. This automation pulls all your Google Calendar events for the day, then scours the internet to gather relevant information about the people you’re meeting.

The agent creates a concise briefing that includes details like their roles, companies, recent news, or any other pertinent info. You can receive this summary via email, Slack, or any communication tool you prefer.

For even deeper preparation, the agent can integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive, or your email to pull relevant documents and past interactions, ensuring you’re fully prepared without lifting a finger.

2. Automated Metrics Gathering and Reporting 📊

If you’re running an online business or subscription service, tracking key metrics is crucial. This automation connects with Stripe (or other platforms like HubSpot, Jira, Facebook Ads, Shopify) to automatically gather and summarize your sales and performance data.

The agent can pull stats such as total revenue, new customers, churn rate, monthly recurring revenue (MRR), refund rates, and more. It can even compare these figures to previous periods and generate detailed reports.

Instead of manually compiling spreadsheets or dashboards, this AI agent delivers ready-to-use insights on your schedule—daily, weekly, or monthly.

3. Find Expansion Opportunities Among Customers 💼

For sales and marketing professionals, identifying upsell or expansion opportunities is a goldmine. This automation scans through your customer databases (e.g., Snowflake, HubSpot, Shopify) to flag clients who show signs of growth or increased usage.

The agent looks for signals like:

  • Rapid growth in usage or purchases
  • Consistent high engagement
  • Package limits nearing (e.g., storage, API calls)

Once identified, the agent notifies the appropriate sales reps or channels so they can proactively reach out. You can even configure the agent to automatically initiate contact and schedule calls, accelerating your revenue growth.

4. Automated Onboarding Document Creation 📋

Onboarding new team members can be time-consuming and repetitive. This AI agent simplifies the process by asking for the role description of the new hire, then combing through your uploaded documentation to gather all relevant information.

The agent organizes everything into a structured onboarding document covering:

  • Welcome and introduction
  • Company overview
  • Role-specific information
  • Key processes and workflows
  • Important contacts and resources
  • First-week checklist

You can customize this document with any additional details you want, ensuring every new hire gets a comprehensive and tailored introduction to their role.

5. Leverage Pre-Built Templates for Instant Value 📂

GetDen.io offers a rich library of templates tailored to various departments and functions, including operations, customer success, product engineering, sales, marketing, and design. These templates provide ready-made AI agents that you can customize and deploy quickly.

By exploring these templates, you can immediately start automating tasks within your existing tool ecosystem, saving hours of manual work and increasing efficiency.
