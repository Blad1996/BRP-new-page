How to Access GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT

Getting started with Astra is pretty straightforward. Inside ChatGPT, go to the Work area and open the model selector. GPT-6 Astra should appear as an available model option.

Once selected, Astra lets you choose the level of effort it should apply to a task:

  • Medium
  • High
  • Extra High
  • Max
  • Ultra

Medium effort is already extremely capable for many practical use cases. Before assuming every task needs the highest setting, test your workflow at Medium and evaluate the result. You may find that it produces what you need without burning through usage unnecessarily.

Astra may also appear inside the ChatGPT app under Codex. If it is missing, make sure the app is updated. An app update can be the difference between not seeing Astra at all and having it appear immediately in the model options.

The positioning is clear: Astra is meant to take on hard problems, formulated ideas, and projects that have been sitting in the back of your mind because building them felt too complicated, expensive, or time-consuming.

AI Video Editing: Creating Vertical Videos From Audio and an Avatar

One of the wildest Astra workflows is AI video creation. A simple request can combine an uploaded audio file with a HeyGen avatar and produce a vertical video format suitable for short form social content.

This matters because vertical video creation normally involves a lot of moving pieces:

  • Matching the voiceover to an on-screen presenter
  • Choosing the right dimensions and framing
  • Adding B-roll at relevant moments
  • Editing pacing and transitions
  • Exporting the finished asset in the right format

With Astra, the goal is no longer simply asking an AI to write a video script. You can use the actual audio and give it the ingredients needed to create the visual asset itself.

That is an enormous shift for creators, agencies, and businesses trying to produce more content without expanding every part of the production team.

Using Reference Videos to Guide the Edit

A more advanced version of this workflow involves uploading a reference video, the original content asset, and the relevant script. The reference establishes the style, pacing, B-roll pattern, and creative direction you want Astra to emulate.

From there, Astra can create an edited version that uses B-roll and visual pacing similar to the reference format. It can take a piece of content and help transform it into something that feels structured for a specific publishing style.

This is where things get especially crazy. Video editing has always required someone to make creative decisions around timing, visuals, emphasis, and flow. Astra can now assist with exactly those decisions at speed.

Extracting B-Roll in Seconds

Another useful AI video editing workflow is B-roll extraction. Upload a video and ask Astra to pull out the B-roll. It can organize the material as individual clips or combine the clips into one file.

What could easily take an editor an hour can potentially be handled in a fraction of that time. The value is not just speed. It is removing a repetitive, tedious task from a workflow so creative people can spend more time on the decisions that actually need their attention.

For content teams, this creates a practical asset library. Old videos can become a source of reusable visuals, clips, screenshots, social posts, and new creative variations.

Prompts Matter More Than Ever With GPT-6 Astra

Here is the part people cannot ignore: even with a model this powerful, the prompt matters massively.

A small change in instructions can completely change the result. In visual tasks, one prompt can produce an excellent output while a slightly different version can introduce bad framing, incorrect zoom levels, or strange visual artifacts.

That is why good prompts should not live in random old chats. When you get a result that works, save the prompt and turn it into a repeatable system.

My Prompt Buddy is useful for this because it lets you optimize, save, organize, and reuse your strongest prompts across tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

Instead of rewriting instructions from memory every time, create shortcuts that include:

  • A clear name for the workflow
  • The full prompt
  • A brief description of what it does
  • Any formatting, quality, or style requirements

This is how you get consistency. You are not relying on memory or hoping that you phrase something perfectly again. You are building a prompt library that compounds over time.

More than ever, prompts are operational assets.

Powerful ChatGPT Astra Prompts to Test

If you are wondering where to start, do not begin with easy work. Give Astra a difficult task that has a clear outcome, multiple steps, and enough value that saving time would actually matter.

1. Bill Negotiator

Ask Astra to audit recurring expenses such as internet, phone, insurance, software, and similar bills. The objective is to identify opportunities to lower costs and find potential discounts or savings.

This is a simple example of using AI as a financial efficiency tool rather than just a writing assistant.

2. Turn an Agency Into Software

Choose a service business or agency niche where customers pay for a defined result. Ask Astra to reverse engineer what happens between the moment a client pays and the moment the client gets the result.

Then have it design an MVP around that workflow. This can help identify where software, automation, templates, or AI agents could replace manual service delivery.

3. Garage Sale Flipper

Use Astra to search places such as Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and local marketplaces for items that may be worth buying and reselling. The focus is on turning broad, repetitive searching into a more systematic sourcing process.

4. One-Person Company Operating System

Ask Astra to audit the ways you use ChatGPT and identify where your existing processes could be improved. This is especially powerful for solo operators who are doing content, research, operations, sales, product work, and customer support all at once.

5. Find AI Agents to Hire Before Humans

Have Astra audit company operations and identify recurring tasks where an AI agent should be considered before hiring another employee.

This is not about removing people from work for the sake of it. It is about recognizing repetitive tasks that can be automated so human time is focused on high-leverage work.

6. Browser Operator

Give Astra a browser-based workflow and ask it to complete the process from beginning to end. This could involve research, collecting information, locating visual assets, or navigating online tools.

Browser access is a huge deal because much of modern work happens inside websites. If AI can search, click, gather, and organize information, it can take meaningful operational weight off your plate.

7. Automated QA Testing

Ask Astra to test a product each night as if it were a brand-new customer, across both mobile and desktop experiences. It can go through the sign-up process and document issues with screenshots, videos, and exact reproduction steps.

This gives founders and product teams a way to make quality assurance more consistent. Bugs do not have to wait until someone remembers to test the flow manually.

8. Competitor Intelligence Agent

Give Astra a list of competitors and have it monitor websites, pricing pages, onboarding flows, products, changelogs, and other relevant updates.

Competitive research often gets delayed because it is not urgent until suddenly it is. A recurring monitoring workflow makes it easier to spot changes before they become surprises.

Browser Automation Is Where This Starts Getting Really Serious

Astra can open its own browser, search online, and gather assets such as screenshots. For something like finding B-roll, collecting reference material, or researching a topic, this can remove a huge amount of clicking and tab switching.

It is important to use common sense with access and credentials. Storing sensitive information for high-risk accounts such as banking or retirement accounts is a very different decision from using browser functionality to gather public screenshots for a creative project.

The larger takeaway is simple: ChatGPT Astra is no longer limited to telling you what to do. It can help do the work itself.
