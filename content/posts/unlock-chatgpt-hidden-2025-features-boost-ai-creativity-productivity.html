🎨 Revolutionize Your Image Creation with New ChatGPT Styles

One of the most exciting updates is the overhaul in how ChatGPT generates images. Now, when you go to the “Create an Image” tool, you’ll find nine distinct style options to choose from. These range from cyberpunk aesthetics to retro 1950s cartoon styles, minimal vector art, art deco inspiration, and more.

Here’s why this is a game-changer: instead of struggling to craft complex prompts from scratch, you can simply select a style, and ChatGPT will automatically adjust the prompt to fit that visual theme. For example, if you want a grizzly bear illustrated in a retro 1950s cartoon style, you just toggle the style on and ChatGPT fills in the descriptive details to match that vibe.

But there’s more to it than just picking a style. This update also serves as a guide on how you should be prompting AI for images. When you select a style, ChatGPT populates the prompt with key descriptors such as composition, lighting, color palette, mood, and artistic details. This is a perfect learning tool for mastering the art of prompt engineering.

To get consistent, high-quality images, Rob emphasizes using the eight-element prompting framework:

  • Subject: What is the main focus of the image?
  • Composition: How are elements arranged?
  • Style: What artistic style or genre?
  • Lighting: Mood and source of light
  • Color: Palette and tones
  • Mood: Emotional atmosphere
  • Details: Fine elements and textures
  • Context: Background or environment

Additionally, negative prompting is crucial—you specify what you don’t want in the image to avoid unwanted elements cluttering your results.

Another tip is to use ChatGPT’s memory feature to create a brand board—saving your preferred colors, fonts, design styles, and tone so you don’t have to repeat these details each time you generate an image. This makes your creative process faster and more consistent.

Finally, you can upload any image to ChatGPT and ask it to reverse engineer the prompt that would recreate that image. This is an invaluable tool for understanding how certain visual styles are constructed and learning to replicate them in your own projects.

🌐 Why You Should Avoid Using a VPN with ChatGPT

Rob highlights an increasingly common issue that users face when accessing ChatGPT through VPNs. While VPNs are great for privacy and security, they can cause several problems when interacting with AI services like ChatGPT. Here are five reasons to avoid VPNs when using ChatGPT:

  1. Inaccurate Location-Specific Responses: ChatGPT tailors some features and settings based on your region. VPNs can mask your location, leading to responses that don’t fit your actual context.
  2. Account Security and Login Issues: Frequent location changes can trigger security flags, causing login difficulties or account locks.
  3. Latency and Performance Problems: VPNs often slow down your connection, leading to laggy or delayed responses.
  4. Policy Violations and Suspicious Activity Flags: Using VPNs may trigger automated systems to flag your usage as suspicious, potentially limiting access.
  5. Billing and Regional Access Problems: Some features or updates roll out regionally. VPNs can block access to these or cause billing mismatches.

The most impactful problem Rob notes is that AI platforms like OpenAI roll out new features regionally. Using a VPN can mean you don’t get the latest updates or have the right settings enabled, which can seriously hamper your experience.

If you’ve noticed strange issues or missing features on ChatGPT recently, check if you’re connected through a VPN and try disabling it. This simple step can resolve many performance and access glitches.

⏰ How to Schedule Tasks and Reminders Using ChatGPT GPT-4 Mini

Scheduling tasks and reminders directly through ChatGPT just got a major upgrade. However, there’s a catch: the classic GPT-4 (GPT-4.0) model no longer supports task scheduling natively. Instead, you need to switch to GPT-4 Mini to set up reminders and automate task notifications.

Here’s how to make the most of this feature:

  • Go to Settings > Personalization > Notifications to manage how you receive alerts—push notifications on your phone or computer, email, or both.
  • Click on Manage Tasks to view, edit, pause, or delete your scheduled tasks.
  • Use GPT-4 Mini by selecting it in the model dropdown before asking ChatGPT to set a reminder or task.

For example, if you want a daily reminder to drink water, GPT-4 Mini will actually create and manage that task for you, sending notifications based on your preferences. If you hit the maximum number of tasks allowed, simply delete old or unused tasks to make room for new ones.

This feature is perfect for anyone who needs to organize their day, track habits, or schedule recurring work tasks. Rob recommends setting these reminders for important activities like stock monitoring, project deadlines, or personal wellness routines.

🧠 Unlocking the Power of ChatGPT Memory and Personalization

One of the most underrated but powerful new settings is ChatGPT’s memory and personalization feature. This is what sets ChatGPT apart from other AI models like Gemini, Grok, or Claude. Here’s why it matters:

  • Memory Saves Your Preferences: ChatGPT can remember your favorite colors, style preferences, frequently used phrases, and other personal details to provide more tailored responses.
  • References All Chat History: It can build on previous conversations, creating a continuous, evolving dialogue that feels more natural and context-aware.
  • Custom Instructions: You can tell ChatGPT what to call you, what you do, and what personality traits or tone it should adopt when responding.

To get started, go to Settings > Personalization and enable Save memories and Reference chat history. Then, review your saved memories by managing them—delete anything that doesn’t align with your actual preferences or past interactions.

It’s also important to make sure these features are turned on properly and not interfered with by VPN usage or account issues. If you feel like your ChatGPT isn’t “remembering” things correctly, double-check these settings.

🔗 Connectors and GPTs: Expanding ChatGPT’s Integration Power

ChatGPT now supports connectors and specialized GPTs that integrate with external services and apps, dramatically expanding what you can do without leaving the chat interface. This is a huge leap toward making ChatGPT your centralized AI assistant for research, content creation, video editing, presentations, and more.

Here’s what you need to know:

  • Connectors link ChatGPT to apps like video editors, presentation tools, or research databases.
  • GPTs are custom AI models built for specific tasks, such as VideoAI for video creation or Veed for editing.
  • You can search for GPTs that fit your needs, like those that help build PowerPoint presentations, automate content generation, or perform deep web research.

Rob predicts that the number of connectors and GPTs will explode soon, making ChatGPT even more versatile. Currently, they’re best for specialized tasks like research or content creation, but expect these integrations to become as seamless and powerful as tools like Claude in the near future.

🤖 Choosing the Right GPT Model for Your Task

Not all GPT models are created equal, and picking the right one can make a huge difference in the quality and speed of your results. Rob breaks down the best use cases for each model:

  • GPT-4.5: Best for writing, creative brainstorming, and general conversations.
  • GPT-4.1: Ideal for quick coding, fast analysis, and web development.
  • GPT-4 Mini: Perfect for everyday tasks where speed is more important than depth.
  • GPT-4.0: Multimodal tasks, general purpose use.
  • GPT-3: Advanced reasoning and complex problem solving.
  • GPT-4 Mini High: Specialized for coding and visual reasoning.

If you’re ever unsure which model to use, simply ask ChatGPT itself by showing it the list and describing your task. It can recommend the best model for your needs, ensuring you get the most accurate and efficient output possible.
