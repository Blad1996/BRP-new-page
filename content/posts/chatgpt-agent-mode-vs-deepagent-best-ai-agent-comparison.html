🔍 The AI Agent Showdown: ChatGPT Agent Mode vs. DeepAgent

When evaluating AI agents, it’s crucial to test them on diverse, real-world use cases to understand their strengths and limitations. I chose four distinct prompts to challenge both ChatGPT Agent Mode and DeepAgent:

  • Research and generate a crypto market recap report
  • Find business leads and create outreach emails
  • Conduct market research and build a presentation pitch
  • Analyze social media content and create improved tweets

Each prompt tested the agents’ ability to gather live data, generate actionable insights, create content, and perform follow-up actions like scheduling posts or repeating reports.

📈 Test 1: Crypto Market Recap Research

The first challenge was to create a concise yet insightful crypto market recap for the past week. The prompt included key elements such as:

  • Top performing coins
  • Top losers
  • Major crypto news
  • Whale wallet movements (transactions over $10 million)
  • Upcoming token unlock events
  • All data should be live and sources cited with links

ChatGPT Agent Mode: Immediately began processing the request, but did not ask any clarifying questions about the timeframe or specific cryptocurrencies. It tried to gather live data but ran into multiple 404 errors and took almost ten minutes without delivering a complete report.

DeepAgent: Took a more methodical approach by asking follow-up questions to refine the task, such as whether this was a one-time report or recurring, the timeframe (past seven days), and whale movement thresholds. This ensured the output was relevant and tailored.

Within minutes, DeepAgent completed the task, producing a well-structured weekly crypto recap that included:

  • Top performing and losing coins with source citations
  • Summaries of major crypto news events
  • Details on whale wallet movements
  • A list of upcoming token unlocks

Moreover, DeepAgent efficiently repeated the report generation task on demand, costing just 288 credits per run — an impressively economical solution.

Verdict: DeepAgent clearly outperformed ChatGPT Agent Mode in accuracy, speed, and usability for crypto market research. The lack of clarifying questions and persistent errors significantly hampered ChatGPT’s effectiveness.

📞 Test 2: Lead Generation and Outreach Email Creation

Next, I asked the AI agents to help find leads for an AI consulting agency. The task was to:

  • Find 20 potential leads based on an ideal customer profile (ICP)
  • Provide detailed company info including industry, location, employee count, and revenue
  • Explain why each lead is a good fit

ChatGPT Agent Mode: Started by setting up its workspace and quickly generated five leads with relevant company details and one outreach email template. While it completed the task, the output was limited in scope and depth.

DeepAgent: Asked several clarifying questions about lead quantity, contact preferences, industry focus, and geographic scope (US only). It then spun up multiple parallel sub-agents to scour the web and databases for leads.

DeepAgent produced a detailed list of 20 companies with comprehensive profiles including:

  • Company name, industry, size, revenue, and location
  • Website links and rationale for selection
  • Three different outreach email templates
  • Strategies for finding contacts and verifying emails
  • Recommended timing and next steps for outreach

This holistic approach makes DeepAgent a powerful tool for sales and marketing teams looking to automate lead generation and outreach at scale.

Verdict: While ChatGPT Agent Mode completed the basic task, DeepAgent’s thoroughness, multiple email templates, and strategic advice make it the superior choice for lead generation.

ChatGPT Agent Mode: Despite running for over 20 minutes, it failed to deliver any usable research or presentation slides. It struggled to complete the task and produced no results.

DeepAgent: Quickly gathered comprehensive data on city growth metrics and demographics, then automatically generated a 14-slide presentation with detailed charts and graphs. The presentation was immediately usable and could be exported as a PDF or PowerPoint.

🐦 Test 4: Social Media Content Creation and Automation

Finally, I tested how well the agents could analyze a Twitter account’s latest 10 tweets and generate improved tweets on the same topics. The prompt also asked if the AI could automate scheduling posts twice daily.

ChatGPT Agent Mode: Completed the task in six minutes, producing enhanced tweets with topic citations and answering questions about automation possibilities. It suggested logging into platforms to schedule posts but did not automate posting directly.

DeepAgent: Delivered a similar output with multiple tweet concepts and key insights from its research. It also prompted for login credentials to facilitate scheduling and automation.

Both agents successfully completed this task, demonstrating strong content creation and social media management capabilities.

Verdict: Both ChatGPT Agent Mode and DeepAgent performed well here, with minor differences in how they approached automation and scheduling.
