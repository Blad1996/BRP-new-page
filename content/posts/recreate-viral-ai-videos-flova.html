How to recreate a viral video with Flova

The workflow is surprisingly straightforward.

1. Find a viral format worth modeling

Start by looking for content that already has traction. A useful example is the wave of dramatic animated fruit stories that have been circulating on TikTok. Some of these videos pull in over a million views in a day, which makes them perfect source material for reverse engineering.

You are not copying the original piece frame for frame. You are identifying a format that clearly resonates: the character style, emotional tone, pacing, and storytelling structure.

2. Upload the reference video

Once you have your source clip, upload it into Flova using the reference remix skill. Then add a simple instruction that tells Flova what to do with it.

For example, you might ask it to recreate the same type of fruit video but with a new scene and plot.

You do not need a giant prompt. One of the main points of this system is that the uploaded video carries much of the creative context.

3. Let Flova analyze the original

After that, Flova starts doing the heavy lifting.

It analyzes the uploaded clip using a multimodal process, which means it studies both the visuals and the audio together. It looks at how scenes are arranged, how the story moves, how tension is built, what the atmosphere feels like, and how all the parts support each other.

At this stage, Flova is essentially learning why the original worked.

4. Review the video spec and storyboard

Once the analysis is done, Flova creates a production plan. This often includes a video specification and storyboard.

That is where the platform starts feeling less like a simple generator and more like a mini production team. Instead of handing you a random output, it maps out:

  • The story length
  • The characters involved
  • The scene sequence
  • The audio approach
  • The visual references needed

You can accept that plan as is, or change the story, character setup, or other details before generation begins.

5. Generate character and scene references

Next, Flova creates the visual anchors for the project. That includes character references, background scenes, and different variations for each important image element.

This step is critical because it helps maintain consistency across the final video. Rather than generating each clip in isolation, Flova has a clear visual foundation to follow.

In practical terms, this can save an enormous amount of work. Building all of these references manually could easily take days. Flova can produce them in seconds.

6. Generate the video clips

Once the visual references are approved, Flova uses them to create the individual video shots. In the example workflow, it generates multiple clips that later get assembled into the full piece.

These clips can include dialogue, emotional reactions, movement, and sound. You can inspect them one by one before they are combined.

7. Direct the result like an editor

This is one of the best parts of the process. You are not locked into the first draft.

If a shot feels off, a character expression needs changing, or a scene should be regenerated, you can step in and adjust it. Flova gives you room to direct the project instead of forcing you to accept whatever comes out.

After the clips are finalized, the platform can assemble the audio track and combine everything into the finished video on a timeline.

Best practices when recreating viral videos with AI

If you want better results, keep these principles in mind:

  • Choose strong source material. Start with videos that clearly have momentum and a recognizable structure.
  • Change the theme, not just the surface. Give the remake a distinct story, setting, or audience angle.
  • Review the storyboard carefully. This is where you catch weak logic before generation.
  • Use visual references well. Consistency improves when character and scene anchors are solid.
  • Create multiple versions. Often the second or third variation becomes the strongest one.
  • Treat Flova like a collaborator. Ask for revisions, new ideas, or alternative approaches inside the chat.
