🚀 Introduction to VEO 3: The Future of AI Video Generation

VEO 3 is the latest iteration of Google’s AI-driven video generation tool, powered by the Flow platform. Unlike previous versions, VEO 3 integrates audio with video, producing remarkably realistic clips that blur the lines between AI-generated content and real footage. Matthew Berman’s experiments reveal that VEO 3 can generate complex scenes, dialogue, music, and even simulate physics, all within mere seconds or minutes.

This new technology has shocked the industry, raising questions about the future of video content creation and the role of human creators. Could VEO 3 eventually replace traditional video production? Matthew’s video hints at this possibility, but also highlights areas where the AI still struggles, ensuring a healthy discussion on the present and future state of artificial intelligence in media.

🎥 Hyper-Realistic Street Style Interviews

One of the standout examples Matthew showcased was VEO 3’s ability to create hyper-realistic street interviews. These clips mimic the style of casual, spontaneous conversations with people on the street, often capturing a vibe that feels raw and authentic. In Matthew’s demonstration, two Gen Z girls, seemingly just out of a bar and a bit tipsy, engage in a playful dialogue about their own “reality” as AI-generated beings.

Interviewer: "Low key, can you believe we aren’t real?"

Girl One: "I don't know about you, honey. I'm a hundred percent real." (said sassily)

Girl Two: "Yeah. Bet. We're as real as they come."

The dialogue, though not perfectly matching Matthew’s prompts, was compelling enough to fool casual viewers—his wife even mistook the AI-generated clip for a real video. This speaks volumes about how far AI-generated human interaction has come, especially in terms of facial expressions, voice modulation, and natural pauses, even if there are occasional awkward moments.

💥 Action Scenes and Physics Simulations: Trains and Rubik’s Cubes

Matthew also experimented with more dynamic, physics-heavy scenes, such as two massive bullet trains heading toward each other on the same track with a spectacular explosion. Despite detailed prompting, VEO 3 struggled to get the trains on the same track or moving in the right directions, and the explosion, while visually impressive, lacked a believable cause-effect relationship.

Similarly, Matthew tested VEO 3’s ability to simulate a Rubik’s Cube being solved in 3D. The AI generated visually stunning images of the cube and its internal gears, but the movement was inaccurate, with pieces falling off or changing unexpectedly. The hand manipulating the cube sometimes looked unrealistic, and the overall simulation didn’t fully emulate a proper Rubik’s Cube solve.

These examples illustrate that while VEO 3 excels at generating visuals and audio, complex physical interactions and precise object manipulation remain challenging areas for the AI.

🍤 Creative and Fun AI Experiments: Shrimp Fried Rice and Horror Aliens

Alex, Matthew’s co-creator, also shared some hilarious and imaginative VEO 3 experiments. One of his first attempts was generating a video of shrimp fried rice, which amusingly resembled a lobster more than a shrimp. Another clip featured a character tossing a wok using “force,” showcasing VEO 3’s ability to blend humor and action.

Alex pushed the envelope further by creating horror-themed scenes with aliens lurking in dark alleys. The AI managed to capture the eerie vibe and lighting effects, although the sound effects were hit-or-miss. Interestingly, Alex accidentally generated one video with VEO 2, which was noticeably less impressive, highlighting the significant improvements in VEO 3.

🤠 Wild West Meets Futuristic AI: Cowboy on a Half-Motorcycle, Half-Horse

One of the most American-themed prompts Alex tried was a cowboy riding a hybrid motorcycle-horse, jumping through a ring of fire. The results were a mixed bag: some clips showed the cowboy performing the stunt with a fiery ring, but others had odd visual glitches like the motorcycle having a horse tail or the cowboy jumping off into nothingness. Despite these flaws, the creativity of the prompt and the AI’s attempt to bring it to life were entertaining and impressive.

🦖 AI-Generated Jurassic Park: Dinosaurs and Tourists

Matthew took VEO 3 into the realm of classic cinema by recreating a Jurassic Park-like scene. The AI generated realistic crowds, the iconic park gate, and a variety of dinosaurs. While the humans looked flawless, the dinosaurs appeared more animatronic than lifelike, lacking the hyper-realism seen in the human subjects.

Interestingly, one dinosaur looked more like a bird, complete with feathers and a beak, aligning with recent scientific discoveries about dinosaur appearances. The AI even created scenes of a T-Rex chasing tourists, complete with cinematic camera movements and gate animations. Though sometimes awkward (like the giant T-Rex being only seven feet tall), these clips demonstrated VEO 3’s potential for storytelling and complex scene generation.

📱 Fictional Product Reviews and AI-Generated Dialogue

Another fascinating use case shown by Ethan Malek was creating fictional product reviews using VEO 3. The AI flawlessly mimicked YouTube review styles, complete with product demonstrations and dialogue. However, subtle giveaways like awkward pauses in human speech still revealed the AI nature of the videos.

Matthew noted that these minor glitches are the primary giveaway that a video is AI-generated, but overall, the dialogue and presentation were highly convincing. This opens up exciting possibilities for AI-generated marketing, reviews, and educational content.

🎶 Songs and Captions: VEO 3’s Audio Capabilities

One of VEO 3’s killer features is its integration of audio, including the ability to generate songs with synchronized captions. Matthew showcased a catchy AI-generated song where the captions matched the lyrics perfectly, demonstrating the precision of the AI’s audio-visual synchronization.

Even iconic characters like Darth Vader appeared in these AI-generated musical clips, showing that VEO 3 can blend pop culture references with original content seamlessly.

🕺 Boy Bands, Rube Goldberg Machines, and Physics Challenges

Matthew and Alex also explored VEO 3’s ability to generate dancing boy bands and complex Rube Goldberg machines. While the boy band videos had some unexpected extras showing up in the background, the overall performance and choreography were impressive.

The Rube Goldberg machines looked visually intricate, with marbles, dominoes, and gears, but lacked continuous motion or functional physics. Some clips even showed objects moving in impossible directions or defying gravity, highlighting the current limitations of AI in simulating real-world physics accurately.

🏙️ Time-Lapse Builds and Abstract Concepts

Alex experimented with time-lapse videos of New York City being built out of Legos. Although the AI simply spawned Lego blocks rather than showing people building the skyline, the resulting visuals were captivating. Another abstract experiment involved visualizing the fourth dimension, with portals and otherworldly effects that captured the imagination.

🍔 Food Videos and Cel-Shaded Realism

Food is one of VEO 3’s strongest suits. Alex created videos showing hamburger ingredients slowly falling onto a bun, with impressive detail and realistic textures. Although some clips didn’t fully complete the assembly, the visuals were mouthwatering and highlighted the AI’s prowess in rendering food.

In a different style, Alex also generated cel-shaded realistic humans, which looked like a blend between animation and hyper-realism. These visuals could inspire new artistic directions for AI-generated content.

🤣 Stand-Up Comedy and One-Liners

VEO 3 even dabbled in humor, generating stand-up comedy clips featuring one-liner puns. One such joke about a band named “ten twenty three megabytes” failing to get a gig was genuinely funny and even made Alex laugh out loud. This shows the AI’s potential to create entertaining scripted content beyond just visuals.

🪐 Space and Science Visualizations

Matthew tested VEO 3’s ability to depict the solar system, with planets and celestial bodies rendered in detail. While some camera movements resulted in awkward object placements, the overall visualization was impressive and educational, showing promise for AI in science communication.

🔫 The Game Portal Reimagined in Hyper-Realism

One of Matthew’s standout projects was recreating the iconic game Portal in hyper-realistic detail. His prompt described a man in a futuristic tactical suit holding a glowing portal gun in a dimly lit industrial room. VEO 3 generated stunning visuals of the portal opening, energy crackling, and the man stepping forward. Although the man didn’t actually walk through the portal, and reflections were minimal, the scene was visually striking and demonstrated the AI’s ability to interpret complex prompts.

Matthew also highlighted the use of prompt expansion — a technique that enhances AI output by elaborating on initial prompts — and encouraged viewers to explore tutorials on forwardfuture.ai for mastering AI prompting.

♻️ Environmental Storytelling: The “This is Plastic” Video

Meta Puppet created a nearly three-minute video titled “This is Plastic” using VEO 3, which humanized plastic pollution by narrating the life of a plastic baby. The video portrayed microplastics’ impact on human health with a mix of humor and pathos, using AI-generated characters and dialogue.

The video was both hilarious and unsettling, showing plastic growing softer as it warms, being bullied in school, and even starring in a commercial. This creative approach to environmental storytelling demonstrated VEO 3’s versatility in combining education, entertainment, and social commentary.

👽 Alien Invasion: Complex Narratives and Interactions

Matthew’s final example was a three-minute alien invasion movie generated entirely by VEO 3. The video featured panic spreading worldwide, state emergencies, government conspiracies, and civilians reacting to the invasion. The AI even mimicked live TikTok streams with emojis floating up and viewer counts, creating layers of realism and interactivity.

This clip was arguably the most complex and detailed AI-generated video Matthew had ever seen, combining multiple characters, scenes, and audio elements to tell a coherent story. The addition of synchronized audio was a game-changer, elevating VEO 3’s content far beyond silent or captioned animations.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is VEO 3 and how is it different from previous versions?

VEO 3 is Google’s latest AI video generation tool, powered by Flow AI. Unlike earlier versions, it integrates synchronized audio with video, producing more realistic and dynamic clips. It can generate human dialogue, music, complex scenes, and even simulate physics to some extent.

Can VEO 3 replace human video creators?

While VEO 3 is incredibly advanced, it still has limitations, such as unnatural pauses, physics inaccuracies, and occasional visual glitches. However, it is a powerful tool that can augment human creativity and may eventually automate certain types of video production.

What types of videos can VEO 3 generate?

VEO 3 can create a wide range of videos, including street interviews, action scenes, horror clips, product reviews, music videos, environmental stories, sci-fi narratives, and more. It excels at realistic human faces, audio synchronization, and visual effects.

How can I start using AI tools like VEO 3?

Currently, VEO 3 is part of Google’s Flow AI suite. Additionally, platforms like Hostinger Horizons allow users to launch AI-powered web applications easily. For learning AI prompting and generation techniques, resources like forwardfuture.ai offer tutorials and newsletters.

What are the main challenges VEO 3 faces?

Key challenges include accurately simulating physics and object interactions, eliminating awkward speech pauses, improving reflections and lighting effects, and ensuring coherent multi-object scenes. These are active areas of research and development.
