⚙️ How to Create Your First AI Workflow in Plain English

One of the coolest features of DeepAgent is how simple it is to get started. When you create a new AI workflow, DeepAgent prompts you with a few straightforward questions to understand your goal. Here’s the process I followed to build a workflow that scrapes a website and summarizes recent blog posts:

  1. Define the main purpose: I told DeepAgent I wanted to scrape a website and create posts based on updates from the last 24 hours.
  2. Specify inputs: The workflow would receive the website URL to scrape.
  3. Define outputs: I wanted a summarization of news articles, including updates, actionable insights, and use cases.
  4. Outline specific steps: I detailed the process step-by-step, such as scraping posts, fetching full content, analyzing content, and generating a report.

After providing these instructions, DeepAgent took over. It generated a massive amount of code automatically, connecting multiple nodes like a website scraper, content fetcher, post analyzer, and report generator. Within seconds, the entire workflow was ready to deploy — no coding from me at all.

Here’s what the final workflow looked like:

  • Website scraper: Extracts recent blog posts.
  • Content fetcher: Retrieves full content of each post.
  • Post analyzer: Summarizes posts, highlights key updates, extracts actionable insights, and identifies use cases.
  • Report generator: Combines all data into a comprehensive final report.

What’s more, creating this workflow used just 200 credits — a tiny fraction of what you might expect for such a powerful automation.
