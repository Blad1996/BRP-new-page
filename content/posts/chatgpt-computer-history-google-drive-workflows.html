ChatGPT computer history, Google Drive access, automated work, interactive quizzes, restaurant reservations and smarter permissions are turning ChatGPT into something much bigger than a chat box. These upgrades make it possible to give ChatGPT context around what you are doing, let it work across documents and apps, and build workflows that actually save time.

The big shift is simple: instead of opening a new conversation and explaining everything from scratch, ChatGPT can increasingly understand the work around it. It can help with files, browser activity, schedules, study material, documents and tasks, all from one place.

That is incredibly powerful. It also means permissions and prompt quality matter more than ever

1. ChatGPT Computer History Gives Your Work Context

The biggest upgrade is ChatGPT computer history. This feature allows ChatGPT to maintain text summaries of activity across apps and websites so it has context for the work you have been doing.

Think about how often you stop in the middle of something, go to lunch, return later and have to retrace your steps. With computer history enabled, you can ask questions such as:

  • What was I working on before lunch?
  • What were the key tasks I was completing this morning?
  • What should I be doing next based on the work I have already done?
  • Can you explain what I was doing in this app?

That is a major change from the usual experience of manually pasting context into a prompt. ChatGPT can use the work history it has permission to reference to help answer questions, explain what is happening, and identify possible next actions.

How to Enable Computer History

Inside the ChatGPT desktop app, open Settings and look for the integrations area. From there, you can enable the setting that lets ChatGPT follow your work across supported apps and websites.

When enabled, the feature is designed to create text summaries of activity rather than record your screen or audio. You can pause history, resume it later, remove individual records or clear the entire history when needed.

It works much like memory in the sense that you control what ChatGPT can retain and reference.

Privacy Controls You Should Actually Use

Computer history can be useful, but this is not something to turn on blindly. You should actively manage what apps and websites are included.

For example, sensitive areas such as banking, health information, private messaging and communication platforms should be handled carefully. You can exclude specific apps and websites from being included in computer history.

  • Pause computer history whenever you need a private workspace.
  • Exclude financial, health and sensitive communication apps.
  • Exclude specific websites, such as online banking portals.
  • Clear individual activity records when they are no longer useful.
  • Pause history during video calls, messaging and other communications unless everyone involved has consented.

The goal is not to hand over unlimited access without thinking. The goal is to give ChatGPT the context it needs for useful work while keeping boundaries around the information that should remain private.

App Shots Add Visual Context

Another useful option is app shots. This gives ChatGPT access to visual and text content from an application, including material that may have been scrolled off screen.

With a configured hotkey, you can capture the relevant information from the task you are doing and send it to ChatGPT for help. That can be useful when an interface is confusing, an error message appears, or you need help understanding what is on screen.

Instead of trying to explain every menu, button and setting manually, you can provide direct context and ask ChatGPT to help solve the issue.

2. ChatGPT Can Work With Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides

One of the most practical upgrades is the Google Drive integration. ChatGPT can now be used to create, edit and work with files inside Google Drive without constantly switching between separate tabs.

This includes work across:

  • Google Docs for drafting and updating written documents
  • Google Sheets for working with spreadsheets
  • Google Slides for presentation content
  • Google Drive for selecting and managing specific files

A simple request can be enough to get started. For example, you could ask ChatGPT to draft release notes in a Google Doc. From there, it can create the document, provide a link to it and let you open it directly from within the ChatGPT workflow.

This matters because the workflow becomes much smoother. You are no longer treating ChatGPT as a place to generate text that you then manually move somewhere else. It can become part of the actual production process.

Setting Up Google Drive Access

To use these capabilities, open the plugins or integrations area, search for Google Drive and install the relevant connection. It is also worth connecting the individual tools you expect to use, including Docs, Sheets and Slides.

Once connected, use the ChatGPT work environment and reference Google Drive directly in your request. You can choose specific files when needed, which is useful if you want ChatGPT to work from one document, spreadsheet or presentation rather than your entire Drive.

For anyone creating content, organizing operational information or handling recurring documents, this is a massive upgrade.

3. Schedule Work in Plain English

ChatGPT can also help set up scheduled work. Rather than figuring out every setting up front, you can explain what you need in plain English and have ChatGPT ask questions to clarify the schedule.

For example, you could ask it to set up a scheduled task, explain the goal, and let it interview you about timing, frequency and desired output. This is useful because people often know what they want done but do not know how to translate it into a detailed automation workflow.

The important part is being clear about the desired outcome. Describe:

  1. What you want the task to accomplish.
  2. What information or files the task should use.
  3. When it should run.
  4. What the finished result should look like.

That gives ChatGPT enough context to help turn an idea into a scheduled process.

4. Stop Using Basic Prompts

One of the biggest mistakes people make with ChatGPT is using prompts that are way too basic. A request such as create a blog post about AI agents may produce something decent, but it will not reliably produce the result you actually want.

The issue is not that ChatGPT cannot write. The issue is that it cannot read your mind.

A strong prompt gives the model structure. It explains the situation, the task, the objective, the knowledge it should consider and the exact type of output you need.

What a Better Blog Prompt Includes

Instead of making a vague request, define the assignment. For a blog post about AI agents, you might specify:

  • The target length, such as 1,000 words.
  • The intended audience and level of expertise.
  • The core angle or argument.
  • The desired format, including headings, examples and a conclusion.
  • Whether research should be included.
  • The need for a header image concept and image prompts throughout the article.

When you provide that level of detail, the output changes completely. You get the written article, but you may also get supporting image prompts, a stronger structure and a much clearer path to a publishable final piece.

For prompt optimization, MyPromptBuddy can help turn a rough request into a structured prompt. It also makes it possible to save repeatable shortcuts for prompts you use all the time.

The point is not to make prompting complicated. It is to stop settling for a generic first draft when a little more context can produce a significantly better result.

5. Use ChatGPT to Find Restaurant Reservations

ChatGPT now has integrations with OpenTable and Resy that can help find reservations directly inside the conversation.

You can make a natural request such as asking for an Italian or sushi restaurant for four people in a particular city next week. ChatGPT can search for options, compare restaurants and surface available reservation times.

In the Bozeman example, ChatGPT surfaced restaurants including Blackbird, Tutepane, Fresco Cafe and Seven Sushi, then ranked options and displayed reservation availability. That makes the experience more useful than simply receiving a list of restaurant names.

It combines recommendation, availability and booking access in one workflow.

6. Build Interactive Quizzes for Any Subject

Another seriously useful feature is inline quiz creation. You can ask ChatGPT to generate a quiz on virtually any topic, answer questions directly in the conversation and get immediate feedback.

For example, someone studying AI agents can request a quiz designed to build expertise. ChatGPT can ask a multiple choice question, evaluate the answer, explain why it is correct or incorrect, and continue to the next question.

If the quiz is too easy, simply tell it to make the questions harder. It can shift to a more advanced level and generate a new set of questions.

Turn Your Existing Notes Into Study Material

The quiz functionality becomes even more powerful when you upload your own files. You can provide documents, spreadsheets, slide decks, study guides or notes and ask ChatGPT to create questions based on that material.

This can support a wide range of study workflows:

  • Create a diagnostic quiz to identify weak areas.
  • Generate increasingly difficult questions as you improve.
  • Request hints before revealing answers.
  • Switch subjects, such as moving from AI agents to SAT math preparation.
  • Ask for an assessment that adapts to your performance.

For test preparation and skill building, this is a huge step up from static flashcards. The experience can become interactive, adaptive and focused on the areas where you need the most help.

7. Choose How Much Access ChatGPT Has

These new capabilities come down to access. Inside a new chat, you can select the plugins and integrations ChatGPT can use, then choose how it should handle actions involving your computer and the internet.

There are three main permission approaches:

  • Ask for approval: ChatGPT asks before editing files, using the internet or taking other actions.
  • Approve for me: ChatGPT acts automatically in most cases but asks when it detects a potentially unsafe action.
  • Full access: ChatGPT is given broader freedom to use the computer, apps and connected tools.

Approval mode provides the most control, but it can slow the workflow down. The middle option offers a practical balance by allowing ordinary tasks to move quickly while flagging actions that appear risky.

Full access can be incredibly convenient, especially for repetitive work, but it should be used with intention. Review your connected apps, files, websites and permissions before enabling it.

Run Tasks Locally or in the Cloud

You can also choose where a ChatGPT task runs. When it runs on your local computer, it can read and edit local files. When it runs in the cloud, it cannot access those local files.

Cloud based work can be especially useful when you need to continue a task from a mobile device. There is also an option to allow ChatGPT to access a computer even when it is locked, allowing work to be initiated remotely from a phone.

That is wild, but again, permissions are everything. The more access you grant, the more carefully you should configure exclusions and safety controls.

The Real Upgrade Is a More Connected Workflow

None of these features matter much if ChatGPT is still being used only for one sentence requests and copied text. The real opportunity is combining context, integrations and strong prompts into a workflow that helps you get actual work done.

Use computer history to regain context. Use Google Drive to create and update documents. Use schedules to automate recurring work. Use interactive quizzes to learn faster. Use restaurant integrations for real world planning. Most importantly, use better prompts so the output matches the quality of the task.

ChatGPT is becoming more capable, but capability alone is not the advantage. The advantage comes from setting it up properly, protecting sensitive information and giving it clear instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ChatGPT computer history?

ChatGPT computer history is a feature that creates text summaries of activity across selected apps and websites, allowing ChatGPT to reference prior work and provide better context when helping with tasks.

Can I exclude sensitive apps and websites from ChatGPT computer history?

Yes. You can pause history, clear records and exclude specific apps or websites. Sensitive areas such as banking, health and private communications should be reviewed carefully before enabling access.

What can ChatGPT do with Google Drive?

With Google Drive integrations enabled, ChatGPT can work with Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and selected Drive files. It can help create, edit and organize content without requiring constant tab switching.

Why are detailed prompts better than basic ChatGPT prompts?

Detailed prompts define the situation, task, objective and desired output. This gives ChatGPT the context needed to create a more relevant and complete result, including items such as structure, research direction and image prompts.

Can ChatGPT create quizzes from my notes?

Yes. You can upload documents, spreadsheets, slides or study guides and ask ChatGPT to create quizzes, diagnostic assessments, hints and increasingly difficult questions based on the material.
