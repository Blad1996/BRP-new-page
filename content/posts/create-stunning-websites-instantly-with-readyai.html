🛠 How to Use ReadyAI: Step-by-Step Website Creation

Getting started with ReadyAI is incredibly straightforward. Here’s a breakdown of the process:

  1. Choose Your Device Format: Decide whether to design for desktop or mobile.
  2. Customize Visual Settings: Adjust corner radius, primary colors, themes, and upload your own images if desired.
  3. Enter Your Prompt: Write a simple description of what you want. For example, “Create a modern website for Benjamin’s Lawn Services.”
  4. Enhance Your Prompt: Use ReadyAI’s feature to select an existing page as a reference to enrich your prompt automatically.
  5. Generate Website Plan: The AI breaks down the site structure including navigation bars, hero sections, service grids, color palettes, typography, and contact bars.
  6. Approve and Generate: Review the plan and click “Generate” to create your website instantly.
  7. Edit Elements: Use a selector tool to click on any element you want to change, such as business hours or text, and provide new instructions.
  8. Build Subpages: Click buttons like “Get a Quote” to generate additional pages or forms. This allows you to build a fully interactive multi-page website.
  9. Publish: Connect your custom domain and publish your site live in seconds.

For instance, after generating a lawn care website, if you want to update the hours from “9 AM to 5 PM daily,” simply select the hours section, input the new text, and watch ReadyAI update the page instantly. The platform also supports adding interactive forms that collect client data, which you can access and export later.
