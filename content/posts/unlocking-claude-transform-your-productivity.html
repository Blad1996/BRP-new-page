🔧 Setting Up Claude: A Step-by-Step Guide

Setting up Claude is incredibly straightforward. Here’s a step-by-step guide to get you started:

  1. Visit the Claude Website: Go to claude.ai.
  2. Access Connected Apps: Once on the homepage, look for the "Connected Apps" section.
  3. Connect Your Accounts: Click on the "Connect" button next to Google Calendar, Gmail, and Google Drive. Follow the prompts to grant necessary permissions.
  4. Verify Connections: Ensure that each service is connected by checking for any updates or notifications.
  5. Start Using Claude: With everything set up, you can begin exploring the various prompts and functionalities Claude offers.

This simple setup process ensures that you can leverage the full power of Claude’s new features without any technical hassle. Once connected, you’ll be able to visualize your time management, optimize your inbox, and much more.
