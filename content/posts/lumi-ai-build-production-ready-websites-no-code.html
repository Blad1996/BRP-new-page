🔧 What is Lumi and why I’m excited about it

Lumi is an AI-powered website builder that generates production-ready sites from plain-English prompts. I tested it hands-on and was impressed not only by how quickly it builds pages but also by the level of polish, back-end features, and customization it provides.

Key highlights that stood out during my testing:

  • Prompt-driven creation: Type a description of what you want (style, pages, tone) and Lumi builds the site.
  • Remix community: You can browse community-built sites, open one you like, then “remix” it to make it yours instantly.
  • Built-in toolbox: Unlike many no-code AI builders, Lumi includes database, email service, file storage, authentication, and analytics.
  • Workspace & backend access: You can view data, code, domains, and security settings through a central workspace.
  • Daily free credits: You get five free credits every day so you can iterate without immediate cost.

In short: Lumi is not just a pretty front-end generator — it delivers functionality you can ship with and connect to real services.

📝 How Lumi works — a walkthrough of the builder

Here’s a short walkthrough of the workflow I used and the features I tested. Follow these steps to replicate what I did and get a high-quality site fast.

  1. Open the Lumi homepage: Browse featured community sites to find a layout or idea that inspires you.
  2. Remix or start from prompt: Click remix on a community site to adapt it, or input a custom prompt describing your business, pages, and tone.
  3. Choose a style: Lumi offers ready-made style presets (I used “Nordic minimalist” for a sleek, professional look).
  4. Use the toolbox: Add database, email, file storage, authentication, or analytics if your project needs them.
  5. Enhance the prompt: If your prompt is short, click “enhance prompt” to let Lumi expand and refine your request automatically.
  6. Upload assets: Upload logos, images, screenshots, or reference sites so Lumi can match visuals and layout.
  7. Build & publish: Click build to generate the site. Preview on desktop or mobile, then publish to Lumi’s domain, community, or your own custom domain.
  8. Iterate using Discuss mode: Toggle between build and discuss modes to ask Lumi questions and refine design choices without consuming credits or making immediate changes.

During my demo I asked Lumi to build “a sleek and modern professional website for an AI agent and automation agency that helps businesses save time and scale using AI tools and workflows.” I specified pages, sections, style, and tone — and Lumi delivered an editable site in minutes.

🎛️ The toolbox: five back-end features that matter

What often differentiates hobby-site generators from production-ready platforms is back-end capability. Lumi offers:

  • Database: Store user data, content, and dynamic records to power dashboards and interactive features.
  • Email service: Manage transactional emails or lead capture follow-ups without external integrations.
  • File storage: Host images, videos, and assets used by your site directly in Lumi.
  • Authentication: Add sign-up / login flows to build member areas or client portals.
  • Analytics: Track user behavior and measure conversions — many AI builders skip this, but Lumi includes it.

These features turn a landing page into a functional product: a booking system, dashboard, or client portal can all live in the same environment without writing a backend from scratch.

✍️ Prompting best practices: how to get the site you actually want

Writing a good prompt makes a massive difference. Here are practical tips I used that you can copy:

  • Be specific about pages and sections: Instead of “build a website,” write “Create a home page, about page, services page with pricing tiers, case studies, and a contact form.”
  • Define the tone: “Professional, confident, friendly” or “minimalist and authoritative” helps the copy and design match your brand voice.
  • Pick a style preset: Choose a preset like “Nordic minimalist” to align layout and typography with your vision.
  • Upload assets: Add logo files, example websites, or screenshots of layouts you prefer — Lumi will use them as guides.
  • Use “enhance prompt” if stuck: If your prompt is short, let Lumi expand and add recommended sections or copy.
  • Iterate in Discuss mode: Ask questions like “Are there other fonts that would make this look more modern?” without using credits, then apply changes when you’re ready.

Example prompt I used (copy and adapt):

Create a sleek and modern professional website for an AI agent and automation agency that helps businesses save time and scale using AI tools and workflows. Include homepage, about, services with pricing tiers, case studies, contact form, and a booking CTA for a free audit. Tone: confident and helpful. Style: Nordic minimalist.

⚙️ Editing, remixing and the power of Discuss mode

Once Lumi builds a site, editing is straightforward. You can click on any component and tell Lumi to change it. For instance, I clicked an icon and told Lumi: “Please change this to Instagram instead of Twitter.” Lumi responded, “Great, I will update the component and change the Twitter icon to Instagram,” then refreshed the site with the updated icon — simple and fast.

Discuss mode is incredibly useful: toggle from build to discuss to have a conversational back-and-forth with Lumi without consuming credits or applying changes. Use it to brainstorm font pairings, color variations, or structural choices before committing edits. Treat this like a design critique session — ask about accessibility, readability, or conversion suggestions, then apply the best ideas.

🛠️ Practical tips, pitfalls to avoid, and optimization strategies

Here are lessons I learned while testing Lumi and tips to make your process smoother:

  • Start with a clear brief: Treat the prompt like a creative brief — list pages, CTAs, target audience, and tone.
  • Use Discuss mode first: Ask layout and font questions in Discuss mode before spending credits on builds.
  • Upload references: Screenshots of pages you like help Lumi match spacing and composition.
  • Test mobile: Always preview mobile and desktop; Lumi lets you toggle both in the editor.
  • Hook up analytics and email early: If you’re testing conversions, connect tracking and email capture before publishing.
  • Avoid over-customizing at first: Launch a minimal viable site, validate demand, then iterate.

🧾 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 😊

How much coding knowledge do I need to use Lumi?

None. Lumi is designed for non-developers. You describe the site you want in plain English, select style presets, and Lumi generates editable, production-ready code. If you do know code, you can still inspect and tweak it in the workspace.

Can I connect a custom domain and email provider?

Yes. Lumi supports publishing to custom domains and integrates with email services available in its toolbox. You can also buy domains directly from the workspace. Verify email deliverability settings and SPF/DKIM records if sending transactional emails.

Does Lumi support authentication and user accounts?

Yes. The authentication tool in the toolbox lets you add sign-up and login flows, which is perfect for membership areas, client portals, or SaaS prototypes.

What are daily credits and how many do I need?

Lumi gives you five free credits every day to build and iterate. The number of credits needed per site varies depending on the complexity and how many rebuilds or major edits you request. For many basic and medium-complexity sites, the free credits are enough to prototype and refine.

Is the generated code exportable?

Yes — Lumi allows you to export code and host outside of Lumi if you prefer. The workspace gives access to code and data entities, so advanced users can take full control.

Can I use Lumi to accept payments?

Stripe integration is launching soon. Once available, you’ll be able to add payment flows directly. Until then, you can integrate external payment providers or use built-in email and forms to capture orders and invoice externally.

Is it suitable for eCommerce?

For simple eCommerce or order-form based sales, yes. For complex marketplaces or large inventories, you’ll need to evaluate whether the current features meet your requirements, though the upcoming Stripe support will extend Lumi’s capabilities substantially.

How secure is my data on Lumi?

Lumi exposes security settings in the workspace, including data entities and storage controls. Always follow best practices — limit access, secure authentication, and review privacy policies before storing sensitive user information.
