🚀 Getting Started with Grok 4 and Grok 4 Heavy

Using Grok 4 and Grok 4 Heavy is surprisingly straightforward. Simply visit grok.com, and you’ll see options to select between Grok 3 (the free model), Grok 4, and Grok 4 Heavy. Here’s a quick rundown of the differences:

  • Grok 3: The free model, but it requires you to manually enable “Thinking Mode” and “Deep Search” for reasoning capabilities.
  • Grok 4: A strict reasoning model that automatically reasons through every query without needing toggles. It performs searches and cross-checks its answers internally.
  • Grok 4 Heavy: The premium, $300/month subscription that runs queries through four different AI “agents” using varied methodologies. These agents then compare their results and consolidate the most accurate output, ideal for factually precise or complex problems.

This multi-agent approach is particularly powerful for tasks requiring up-to-the-minute information or deep factual accuracy. Imagine asking four experts and synthesizing their insights—that’s essentially what Grok 4 Heavy does. However, this thoroughness comes at a cost: it typically takes over three minutes to complete a single query, compared to Grok 4’s much faster processing.

While Grok 4 Heavy promises future enhancements like video and coding tools, its current main advantage lies in this multi-agent reasoning system. But does this justify the price? Let’s explore.

📸 Use Case 1: Image Analysis and Geolocation

One of the biggest advertised improvements in Grok 4 is its enhanced ability to analyze images. To test this, I uploaded a photo of a house and asked the AI to identify the location and even guess the exact address. This test also included comparisons with OpenAI’s GPT-3, ChatGPT, and Claude.

The challenge: The photo showed a house with a visible number, architectural style, and surrounding environment. Ideally, the AI would zoom in on the house number and recognize regional architectural clues to pinpoint the location.

Here’s what happened:

  • Grok 4: Struggled to correctly interpret the house number, guessing “6386,” which was inaccurate. It did place the location roughly in Georgia, but didn’t nail the specifics.
  • ChatGPT: Performed better by identifying the house as located in Huntersville, Mecklenburg County—very close to the actual location.
  • Other models: ChatGPT also provided a faster response time, about seven seconds, compared to Grok 4’s longer processing.

Interestingly, while Grok 4 is touted for image recognition, this test revealed it’s not yet superior to ChatGPT for geolocation based on images. Grok 4 Heavy didn’t significantly improve on this either, indicating that the current image analysis capabilities might be overhyped.

This test highlights a key point: AI benchmarks can be gamed or tuned to produce impressive results on paper, but real-world performance can tell a different story.

📊 Use Case 2: Solving Mathematical and Business Problems

Next, I evaluated Grok 4’s reasoning and problem-solving skills with a practical business question involving budget allocation between two marketing channels. The problem required calculating the optimal number of units to invest in each channel to maximize leads, given cost and lead generation rates.

The problem:

  • Channel A costs $200 per unit and produces 150 leads per unit.
  • Channel B costs $300 per unit and produces 250 leads per unit.
  • The company has a $10,000 budget and wants at least 20 units from Channel A.
  • Goal: Maximize total leads within budget constraints.

Here’s how the AI models performed:

  • Grok 4: Quickly formulated the problem as a linear programming model, identified the feasible solution space, and correctly calculated the optimal allocation: 20 units for Channel A and 20 units for Channel B, yielding 8,000 total leads.
  • Claude: Reached the same conclusion but took longer to process the query.

This test clearly demonstrated Grok 4’s strength in structured reasoning and business problem solving. It not only arrived at the right answer but also provided a step-by-step explanation of its thought process, which is invaluable for transparency and trust in AI decisions.

💻 Use Case 3: Coding Challenge – Rotating Hexagon with Realistic Physics

To test Grok 4’s coding capabilities, I asked it to create an interactive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript program where balls bounce realistically inside a rotating hexagon. The balls should be affected by gravity and friction from the hexagon’s walls. This is a complex task combining physics simulation with graphical rendering.

The prompt was given to Grok 4, OpenAI’s GPT-4 (in its “mini high” coding variant), Claude, and Gemini 2.5 Pro.

Results:

  • Grok 4: Took the longest time to generate code and ultimately failed to produce a functional or previewable result. The output did not simulate gravity or friction as requested.
  • ChatGPT (GPT-4 mini high): Completed the task quickly, but the preview in chat was limited. Still, the code was functional and demonstrated basic physics.
  • Claude: Provided editable code with adjustable parameters like rotational speed, gravity, and friction, showcasing a flexible and user-friendly approach.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: Passed the test with a basic but working version of the simulation.

This test exposed a significant weakness in Grok 4’s coding abilities, especially compared to competitors. Despite the hype, Grok 4 struggled with practical coding challenges that require intricate logic and physics simulation. This is an important consideration for developers looking for AI-assisted coding tools.

📰 Use Case 4: Analyzing Real-Time Social Media and News Trends

Where Grok 4 Heavy truly shines is in integrating real-time information from social media, particularly from X (formerly Twitter). I tested its ability to analyze the latest public reactions and developments on a major, ongoing news event — specifically, the “Epstein list” controversy, including key influencer posts.

Unlike traditional AI models that rely on static knowledge bases with cut-off dates, Grok 4 Heavy accesses live data streams from X, spinning up four agents to cross-check and synthesize the freshest information.

Comparisons:

  • Grok 4 Heavy: Provided a detailed, up-to-date analysis citing posts directly from X, with accurate timestamps and influencer insights. However, the response took longer and was surprisingly similar in content to ChatGPT’s summary.
  • Claude and ChatGPT: Delivered solid summaries based on their training data, but lacked live social media citations, limiting their timeliness.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: Struggled to filter relevant information, sometimes citing outdated or irrelevant data.

Although Grok 4 Heavy’s ability to tap into real-time social media is a game changer, in practice, the benefit over ChatGPT’s well-trained knowledge base wasn’t as dramatic as expected. The longer processing time and high cost may deter casual users.

💸 Is Grok 4 Heavy Worth the $300/Month Upgrade?

After extensive testing, here’s my candid take on Grok 4 Heavy’s value proposition:

  • Pros:
    • Exceptional at delivering factually accurate, up-to-date information by leveraging multiple agents and live social media data.
    • Ideal for complex, nuanced problems where cross-verification is critical.
    • Potential for future expansion with video and coding tools.
  • Cons:
    • Extremely slow response times (often over three minutes per query).
    • High subscription cost with limited current features beyond multi-agent reasoning.
    • Doesn’t outperform competitors in image analysis or coding tasks.
    • Real-time social media insights, while impressive, aren’t always significantly better than existing models.

For most users, especially those needing fast, versatile AI support, Grok 4 Heavy may not justify the expense yet. However, for professionals requiring top-tier factual accuracy and real-time updates, it could be a valuable tool.

📚 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Grok 4 and Grok 4 Heavy?

Grok 4 is xAI’s latest AI model focused on strict reasoning and search-based answers. Grok 4 Heavy is a premium version that uses four different AI agents to cross-verify answers, offering higher accuracy and access to real-time social media data.

How does Grok 4 compare to ChatGPT and Claude?

Grok 4 excels in business reasoning and multi-agent fact-checking (especially Grok 4 Heavy), but it falls short in image analysis and coding tasks compared to ChatGPT and Claude. Grok 4 Heavy’s real-time social media integration is unique but comes with slower response times.

Is it worth paying $300/month for Grok 4 Heavy?

For casual users or developers, probably not. But if you require cutting-edge factual accuracy, up-to-date social media insights, or complex problem solving, Grok 4 Heavy could offer value despite the cost.

Can Grok 4 analyze images accurately?

While Grok 4 has improved image analysis capabilities, it currently doesn’t outperform models like ChatGPT in practical tests involving geolocation or detailed image interpretation.

Does Grok 4 support coding tasks?

Grok 4 struggles with complex coding challenges and is not yet competitive with GPT-4 or Claude in this area.
