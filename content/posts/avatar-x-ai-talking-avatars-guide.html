Core features explained

Below is a walkthrough of the major features I covered and demonstrated.

1. Create from photo or choose premade avatars

You can either upload a photo (or a short video clip) to create a custom avatar, or select from JoggAI’s library of premade avatars. Custom avatars can be generated from 10 seconds of footage or a few images — you give consent, submit, and the system creates your AI twin that moves and emotes like the real person.

This is perfect for creators who want an avatar version of themselves (to scale content) or for brands that want different spokespeople without hiring multiple actors.

2. Emotion control and custom emotions

One of Avatar X’s standout features is the emotion control panel. You can set standard emotions — neutral, angry, confident, disgusted, happy, nervous, sad, surprised, excited — or create a custom emotion. To make a custom emotion you:

  1. Name the emotion (e.g., "jolly").
  2. Provide an emotion prompt describing how the avatar should feel and behave.
  3. Apply that emotion to your selected avatar and the system will render those micro-expressions.

This is how I made the Santa avatar feel truly "jolly" — smiling with slightly squinted eyes, a warm posture, and the tiny facial twitches that sell authenticity.

3. Script generation and controls

Instead of writing every line yourself, JoggAI can generate scripts for you based on a few keywords or a content brief. You can edit, regenerate, or randomize scripts as needed. There are built-in options like creative hook, soft sell, expert advice, and emotional appeal scripts which are great for ads, UGC, and social content variations.

Additional script controls include adding pauses (for natural delivery), randomizing lines to create multiple variants, and specifying the maximum video length (up to 15 minutes for documentary/tutorial style segments in some workflows).

4. Voice options and 11 Labs integration

JoggAI offers an extensive voice library by provider, gender, language, accent, and age. If you want even more control, you can integrate a third-party voice provider such as 11 Labs by pasting your API key into the integrations panel. That allows you to use a voice clone of yourself or a highly customized TTS voice for maximum consistency and branding.

Want to use your exact speaking voice? Record the necessary training data in 11 Labs, paste your API key into JoggAI, and your avatar will speak with that cloned voice. It’s an essential step if you want your avatar to truly represent you.

5. Singing avatars

Avatar X can even produce singing avatars. The trick is to pick a model that fits a singing performance visually (for example, an avatar positioned in front of a microphone). You upload singing audio — either pre-recorded by you or synthesized elsewhere — and Avatar X synchronizes lip movement, subtle breathing, and facial expressions so the avatar performs the song convincingly.

In my demo, the lip sync and subtle facial changes while singing were surprisingly accurate — a rare capability among current avatar generators.

6. Templates, VFX, and advanced editing

JoggAI includes templates for social ads, memes, UGC, long videos, promo content, and green screen layouts. Avatar VFX adds attitude and visual effects to an avatar, and templates streamline everything from promotional videos to personalized outreach clips.

Other tools in the suite include face swap, image-to-video, video-to-text, video extender (to lengthen an asset), and a brand kit area for consistent fonts, colors, and logos.

Technical tips & best practices

To get the best output from Avatar X, follow these practical tips I use in demos and client work.

  • Use high-quality reference footage: 10 seconds of clear frontal video with natural lighting gives the best avatar results when creating a custom avatar.
  • Refine custom emotions: Write detailed emotion prompts (body posture, eye behavior, mouth tension) for the most realistic outcome.
  • Voice matching: Integrate 11 Labs if you need voice cloning. Paste your API key into JoggAI’s integrations and assign the cloned voice to your avatar.
  • Short scripts for social: For Reels and TikTok, aim for 15–30 seconds. Use strong hooks in the first 2–3 seconds.
  • Batch creation: Randomize script lines and create multiple variants to test copy and visuals quickly.
  • Choose appropriate avatars for singing: Pick avatars shown with microphones or in a studio pose for better perceived authenticity.
  • Brand consistency: Use the brand kit (logos, fonts, colors) to keep videos on-brand across formats.

Ethics, legal considerations, and disclosure

Powerful tools like Avatar X come with responsibility. When using AI-generated avatars you should consider:

  • Consent: Only create avatars of people who have given explicit consent. If you generate an avatar of yourself, document that consent is yours. For others, always get permission.
  • Deepfake concerns: Avoid creating avatars that could misrepresent or defame real people. Do not impersonate public figures in a way that could be misleading or harmful.
  • Transparency: Label content that is AI generated where relevant, especially in advertising or in contexts where authenticity matters (e.g., testimonials, political content).
  • Copyright: Ensure you have the right to use any photos, audio, or scripts you supply to the system.
  • Platform rules: Comply with the terms of social platforms you post to; many have or are developing policies on synthetic media.
