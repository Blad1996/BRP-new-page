🚀 Manus AI Playbooks: Automate Complex Tasks with Ready-Made Templates

Manus AI has taken automation to a whole new level with its innovative Playbook feature. Think of playbooks as pre-built AI-powered templates designed to perform specific, complex tasks with just a few inputs from you. Whether it’s generating presentation slides, building websites, or even identifying influencers for your product, Manus AI does the heavy lifting.

How Manus AI Playbooks Work

Let me give you a real-world example. I grabbed a product URL and set the region to the United States. After clicking “Start Finding,” Manus AI went through eleven pages of data, analyzing brand portfolios, keywords, and social media channels. It provided an extensive report including:

  • Product analysis and portfolio overview
  • Influencer recommendations across platforms
  • Implementation roadmap for marketing campaigns
  • Budget recommendations

This is the kind of detailed work a marketing director might spend days on, but Manus AI did it in minutes. The playbook even breaks down “brain characteristics” of the target audience, helping you tailor your messaging for maximum impact.

Beyond Influencer Finding: Other Playbooks to Explore

Manus AI offers a treasure trove of templates including:

  • Sentiment Analysis — Gauge public opinion about your brand or product
  • Chrome Extension Builder — Describe what you want your extension to do, and Manus AI builds it for you
  • Video Generation — Create videos based on your input data
  • Website Builders — Automate website creation without writing a line of code

One particularly exciting playbook is the Chrome Extension Builder. You just describe the extension’s function, user interface style, and display method, and Manus AI handles the entire build process. Imagine being able to create custom browser tools on demand without needing technical skills!

Scheduled Tasks for Continuous Automation

Manus AI recently added a powerful new feature: Scheduled Tasks. You can now set up recurring AI jobs that run automatically on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis. For example, you could schedule a daily prompt to receive a summary of all the latest AI news, delivered directly to you without lifting a finger.

This automation saves tremendous time and ensures you never miss out on important updates or repetitive tasks that can be systematized. With scheduled tasks, Manus AI becomes your AI-powered assistant working in the background 24/7.

⚡ Genspark Personalized Tools: Supercharge Your Browser and Workflow Automation

Genspark is a game-changer when it comes to personalized AI tools that integrate deeply with your existing digital ecosystem. Unlike generic AI assistants, Genspark allows you to connect with over 600 third-party MCP (Modular Chat Plugin) tools created by the community, giving you unprecedented customization and control.

Seamless Integrations with Your Favorite Platforms

Genspark supports native integration with popular platforms like Google Suite and Notion. You can also add custom MCP servers by simply providing the server name, URL, and request headers. This means you can tailor Genspark to your exact workflow needs without waiting for official API support.

Among the many MCP tools, you’ll find connectors for:

  • X (formerly Twitter)
  • Reddit
  • DeepWiki
  • Hacker News
  • Databases, code execution environments, storage solutions, and more

This vast library of tools empowers you to automate complex workflows, extract data, and interact with multiple platforms all from one AI-powered hub.

Lightning Fast Browsing with Genspark Browser

One of the standout features is the Genspark Browser, an AI-powered browser that’s lightning fast, blocks ads, and operates in a fully agentic autopilot mode. This browser includes an MCP store and a “super agent” capable of performing virtually any task you throw at it.

Imagine browsing the web with an assistant that can automatically research topics, summarize content, download files for you, and even create slides or spreadsheets based on the information it finds — all without you having to manually intervene.

Use Cases That Will Blow Your Mind

Some practical examples of what Genspark can do include:

  • Creating presentation slides from raw data
  • Generating AI sheets for data analysis
  • Downloading files from URLs based on your instructions
  • Running complex multi-step workflows across different web platforms

With Genspark, your browser is no longer just a tool to view content; it becomes an intelligent assistant actively working to boost your productivity.

🧠 Grok AI: The Deep Research and Task Automation Powerhouse

Grok AI is setting a new standard for deep research and task management in AI. Unlike other platforms, Grok combines powerful task scheduling with the ability to perform deep, contextual research within the same workspace.

Deep Research Within Scheduled Tasks

When you create a task in Grok, you can embed detailed research elements directly into it. This means Grok doesn’t just follow simple instructions — it thinks, analyzes, and synthesizes information deeply before delivering results. This feature is unique and not available on ChatGPT or many other AI platforms.

Workspaces for Organized AI Collaboration

Grok’s workspaces allow you to maintain context across multiple conversations and tasks. You can upload attachments such as PDFs, CSVs, photos, or even sketches for the AI to reference. Integrations with Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive make it easy to pull in relevant files.

Each workspace acts like a project hub where you can:

  • Store and manage AI interactions
  • Share instructions and context for specific tasks
  • Keep all relevant data and conversations in one place

This structure is perfect for teams or individuals handling complex projects that require ongoing AI assistance.

Why Grok Stands Out

While many AI tools focus on single interactions, Grok is designed for sustained, intelligent collaboration. Its ability to conduct deep research and maintain context across tasks makes it ideal for professionals who demand more than surface-level answers.

🤖 Retriever AI: Your AI-Powered Chrome Extension to Automate Everything

Retriever AI is a Chrome extension that takes automation to the next level by literally taking over your browser and completing a wide array of tasks for you. No coding required. Just install the extension, give it instructions, and watch it perform.

Capabilities That Will Blow Your Mind

Retriever AI can:

  • Extract data from multiple websites automatically
  • Build complex workflows that span several browser tabs
  • Automate repetitive tasks with precision and speed
  • Create documents, web pages, and even graphs
  • Fill out PDFs and send messages on Slack

You can schedule these tasks to run at specific times or repeat the last task with a single click, making it a powerful assistant for daily work.

Example: Automated Price Extraction

Here’s a practical use case I tested: I asked Retriever AI to go to a website (bl101.com) and extract the prices of their latest Fourth of July drop. Without me touching the keyboard, Retriever AI navigated the site, gathered the product names, prices, and links, and compiled everything into a neatly organized sheet.

This is exactly the kind of automation that can save hours of manual work, especially for e-commerce managers, marketers, and data analysts.
