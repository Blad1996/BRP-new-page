How Durable Builds a Business Website in Minutes

The setup process is ridiculously straightforward. Instead of choosing a template and staring at an empty page, Durable starts by asking a few questions about the business.

To test it, I created a sample roofing business called Rob’s Roofing in Denver. The initial information included the business type, current monthly revenue, location, website language, and company name.

Then came the important part: a simple description of the business. For this example, Rob’s Roofing was positioned as a trusted Denver roofing company offering roof repairs, replacements, and new installations for residential and commercial customers. The description also emphasized quality workmanship, reliable service, and long-lasting roofing solutions.

Businesses can also add files, photos, and branding details at this stage. That gives the AI more context to work from before it begins creating the site.

After submitting the details, Durable builds the business foundation and assembles the website. It generates the core structure, professional copy, images, and relevant sections based on the type of company and local market.

The result is not a wireframe or a vague design concept. It is an actual working website with relevant messaging, clear calls to action, images, business sections, and a layout that already makes sense for the service being offered.

For the roofing example, the site included locally relevant messaging around Denver roofs, protection from the elements, customer estimates, service information, and proof-oriented content. That is a huge difference from starting with generic placeholder text and stock sections that have nothing to do with the business.
