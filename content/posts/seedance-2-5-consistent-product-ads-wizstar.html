How to Get Better Results From Wizstar AI

AI tools work best when you give them a strong creative brief. Seedance 2.5 can do a lot, but the output becomes more useful when the input is specific.

Use clear reference materials

When product accuracy matters, add high-quality references. Show the actual product, logo, packaging, colours, and details that should remain stable. This is especially important for items with distinctive patterns, branding, or shapes.

Describe the marketing objective

Do not only explain what should appear. Explain why the content exists. Is the goal to create a product review, announce a sale, demonstrate a feature, make a lifestyle ad, or introduce a brand?

A prompt built around a marketing objective gives the generation more direction than a simple request for a person holding a product.

Match the format to the placement

Choose the aspect ratio before generating. A horizontal ad and a vertical social asset need different framing. If you are planning to test creative in several placements, create variations designed for each format rather than cropping one version after the fact.

Generate variations for testing

AI makes it practical to test more angles. Keep the product and offer the same, then vary one important factor at a time:

  • The opening hook
  • The creator or athlete persona
  • The setting
  • The product benefit being emphasized
  • The voiceover style
  • The call to action

That gives you a more organized way to compare concepts instead of randomly producing content and hoping one version lands.
