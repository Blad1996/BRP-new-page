What Is Google Gemini Omni?

Google describes Gemini Omni as a multimodal world model. In plain English, that means it can work across multiple types of media and use real-world understanding to generate or edit video in a more coherent way.

Instead of being limited to a single prompt box where you only type text, Omni can work with:

  • Text prompts
  • Images
  • Audio
  • Video

From there, it can create video content or modify existing content. That includes things like:

  • Adding something into a video
  • Changing the format or aspect ratio
  • Starting from prebuilt templates
  • Generating short-form clips from a prompt and an uploaded image

According to the release information, Gemini Omni Flash is live in places like YouTube Shorts Remix, the Create app, Google Flow, and the Gemini app. It is also being positioned as available for AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers.

How to Access Gemini Omni in Gemini

The workflow shown is refreshingly straightforward.

  1. Open Gemini.
  2. Click on Videos.
  3. Inside that section, you should see access to the new Omni tools.

From there, you can start experimenting with different generation and editing options.

This is one of the reasons the feature stands out so much. You are not jumping through some complicated production pipeline. The setup appears designed for speed, which is exactly what people want from AI video generation right now.

The Core Things You Can Do With Gemini Omni

1. Add elements into a video

One of the first capabilities highlighted is the ability to add something into the video. The flow appears simple: click the relevant option, then click on the image you want to work with.

That points to one of Omni’s most practical use cases: targeted video editing through natural interaction. Instead of manually compositing assets in traditional editing software, the system handles the heavy lifting.

For creators, marketers, and meme-makers, this kind of feature matters because it saves time and lowers the barrier to experimentation.

2. Change the aspect ratio

Gemini Omni also includes controls for changing the aspect ratio of a video.

That might sound like a small feature, but it is actually huge in practice. Different platforms want different formats:

  • Vertical for Shorts and Reels
  • Square for some social posts
  • Horizontal for traditional video layouts

If Gemini can help adapt the same generated concept to multiple layouts, that makes the tool more useful for real publishing workflows, not just one-off demos.

3. Start from templates

Another major convenience feature is the availability of different templates you can start with.

Templates matter because they reduce blank-page syndrome. Instead of staring at an empty interface and wondering what to make, you can begin from a format that already suggests a creative direction. That is especially useful for short-form content where style and speed often matter more than perfection.

For anyone testing AI video for the first time, templates can be the difference between curiosity and actual output.
