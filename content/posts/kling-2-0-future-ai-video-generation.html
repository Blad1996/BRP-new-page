Despite its impressive capabilities, Kling 2.0 is not without limitations. One of the notable challenges is its handling of complex actions, particularly in fight scenes. Additionally, while Kling 2.0 excels in generating realistic characters, it can falter with more stylized animations, such as anime. Users seeking to create anime-style content may find better results with specialized models.
