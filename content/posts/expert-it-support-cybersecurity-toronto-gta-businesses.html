Technical Insights: Simplifying IT for Toronto Businesses 💡

Many Toronto business owners aren’t IT specialists, which makes understanding technical concepts challenging. Here’s a simplified explanation of key IT service components:

  • Firewall: Think of it as a security guard that monitors incoming and outgoing network traffic to block unauthorized access.
  • Endpoint Protection: Software installed on devices like laptops and phones to detect and block malware.
  • Cloud Backup: Automatically saving copies of your data to secure servers accessed via the internet, so you don’t lose information if something breaks.
  • Managed IT Services: Outsourcing your IT maintenance and support to experts who ensure your systems are healthy and secure.

Understanding these basics helps business owners make informed decisions about their IT investments.
