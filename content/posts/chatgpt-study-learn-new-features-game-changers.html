🔍 Unlocking the Study & Learn Feature: Your 24/7 AI Tutor

The first and arguably most exciting feature is the new Study & Learn mode. This isn’t just another ChatGPT update—it fundamentally changes how the AI interacts with you by making conversations more interactive and educational.

Unlike traditional ChatGPT, which primarily responds to your prompts, the Study & Learn mode enables ChatGPT to actively ask you questions, quiz you, and guide your learning process using Socratic questioning and scaffolded responses. This is akin to having a patient tutor available around the clock, tailored to your learning style.

How Study & Learn Works

Imagine you want to master a complex topic like creating AI agents with Make.com. Instead of passively reading explanations, you can ask ChatGPT to create a practice quiz for you. Here’s a quick walkthrough:

  • Activate the Study & Learn mode in ChatGPT.
  • Request a practice quiz on your specific topic (e.g., “Create a practice quiz about AI agents with Make.com”).
  • Choose your preferred question type, such as multiple choice.
  • Answer the questions and immediately receive feedback on what you got right or wrong.
  • Ask ChatGPT to explain concepts you missed or didn’t fully understand.

This interactive back-and-forth turns rote memorization into an engaging learning experience. You’re not just consuming information—you’re actively participating in your education.

Why Study & Learn Is a Game Changer

This feature is designed to mimic effective teaching techniques like scaffolding, where the tutor breaks down complex topics into manageable chunks, and Socratic questioning, which encourages critical thinking by asking guiding questions. This means ChatGPT is no longer a passive tool but a dynamic learning partner.

Some key benefits include:

  • Personalized Learning: ChatGPT adapts to your level of understanding and learning pace.
  • Instant Feedback: You get real-time corrections and explanations to reinforce concepts.
  • 24/7 Availability: Unlike human tutors, ChatGPT is available anytime you want to study.
  • Voice and Visual Integration: Using voice mode or sharing your screen enhances interactivity and immersion.

While this won’t replace teachers, it serves as a powerful augmentation tool, giving learners access to personalized tutoring whenever they need it.

Example Use Case: Learning About Yellowstone Geysers

To illustrate, you can ask ChatGPT in Study & Learn mode to explain why geysers happen in Yellowstone. Instead of just a one-way explanation, ChatGPT will ask you questions first, gauge your current knowledge, then tailor its teaching accordingly. This interactive approach helps reinforce understanding and retention.

Currently, this feature is available to logged-in free, plus, pro, and team users, with further enhancements expected soon in the GPT EDU version.

💡 Enhanced Suggestions & Auto-Complete in the ChatGPT Desktop App

The second major update comes with the ChatGPT desktop app, introducing smarter auto-complete suggestions, trending searches, and follow-up question prompts. These improvements make your ChatGPT sessions smoother, quicker, and more insightful.

How to Enable and Use These Features

Once you download the ChatGPT desktop app, head over to the settings panel and ensure the following options are turned on:

  • Auto-complete
  • Trending searches
  • Follow-up questions

When you start a new chat or type a query, ChatGPT will now suggest relevant follow-ups, trending topics, and even auto-fill your next question. For example, if you ask about “things to do in Yellowstone, Wyoming,” ChatGPT might answer your query and then suggest, “If you want tailored ideas or a guided tour, just let me know.”

Why These Suggestions Matter

This feature acts like a conversational guide, helping you explore topics more deeply without having to think about the next best question. It essentially builds a more natural, flowing dialogue that can uncover insights you didn’t initially consider.

Additionally, on mobile, these suggestions appear as prompts that evolve based on your interactions, creating a curated experience tailored to your interests and needs.

In essence, this makes ChatGPT not just a reactive tool but a proactive assistant helping you navigate complex topics, brainstorm ideas, or discover new angles effortlessly.

🎙️ Recording and Integration Features: Supercharging Productivity

The third cluster of new features focuses on productivity enhancements through recording, app integrations, and better task management within ChatGPT.

Recording Meetings and Conversations

The desktop app now includes a recording feature that allows you to capture meetings or any ongoing ChatGPT session. After recording, you can send the transcript into ChatGPT for detailed analysis.

Once processed, ChatGPT generates:

  • An overall summary
  • Key points highlighted
  • Action items to follow up on
  • Open questions to address

This is a huge time saver for professionals who want to quickly distill and act on meeting content without manually taking notes.

App Integrations: Notes, Script Editor, Terminal, and More

ChatGPT now supports direct access to several apps within the desktop environment, including:

  • Notes
  • Script Editor
  • Terminal
  • TextEdit

This integration allows you to perform tasks like editing scripts, running terminal commands, or taking notes without leaving ChatGPT, streamlining your workflow significantly.

Connectors and Expanded Access

Connectors have been upgraded, enabling ChatGPT’s agent mode and deep research capabilities to access your Gmail, Google Drive, calendar, and more. This means ChatGPT can interact with your personal data (with permission) to provide tailored assistance such as:

  • Reading and managing emails
  • Accessing and summarizing Google Drive documents
  • Scheduling and managing calendar events

These connectors open the door to powerful automation and personalized AI support, making ChatGPT an even more valuable productivity partner.

Scheduling Reminders with ChatGPT

Scheduling reminders has also been refined. Simply asking ChatGPT to schedule a daily reminder (e.g., “Remind me to check email at 9 AM every day”) can now be delegated to the GPT-4 Mini model, which handles task creation more reliably than the default ChatGPT interface.

Note that there are task limits in place, so managing and deleting old tasks might be necessary to keep your schedule organized.
