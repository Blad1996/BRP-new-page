⚡ What is Claude’s New AI-Powered Artifacts Feature?

Claude’s latest update centers around its Artifacts platform—an environment where you can build and customize apps, documents, prototypes, and websites that actually use Claude’s AI capabilities inside them. The magic here is that you don’t need to be a coder or have technical expertise to tap into Claude’s API. You simply start by telling Claude what you want to build, and it guides you through creating a fully functional AI-powered artifact.

To get started, you just need to enable the feature in your settings under Feature Preview by toggling on "Create AI powered artifacts." Once activated, you can browse existing artifacts for inspiration or build one from scratch.

This integration means your custom-built tools can perform complex AI tasks like writing, analyzing, generating, and interacting with users—all powered by Claude, but without any backend hassle on your part.

🛠️ How to Customize and Build Your Own AI Artifacts

One of the most exciting parts about this feature is the ability to customize existing artifacts or create entirely new ones tailored to your specific needs. Here’s how the process works:

  1. Find an Artifact to Remix: Explore the pre-built artifacts gallery. For example, you might find a writing editor that’s already set up with a prompt and style.
  2. Customize the Prompt: Click “Customize this artifact” and tweak the instructions. For instance, you could transform a generic writing assistant into an Alex Hormozi-style copywriting expert or a Taylor Swift-inspired songwriting assistant.
  3. Update the Style: Refresh the visual design to make it more personal or aligned with your brand.
  4. Train the Artifact: Claude will analyze your changes and update the artifact’s behavior accordingly, focusing on specific goals like clarity, urgency, emotional impact, or value-driven messaging.
  5. Publish and Share: Once ready, publish your artifact and share the link. The best part? When others use your artifact, it doesn’t consume your Claude credits, so you can share freely without extra cost.

For example, if you create an Alex Hormozi copywriting assistant, others can use your tool to generate high-converting sales copy, and you can even monetize it or use it internally for your team.
