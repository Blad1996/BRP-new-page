🛸 Drone Flying Simulator: A Stunning One-Shot Creation

One of the first tests of GPT-5’s capabilities was to create a sophisticated 3D drone flying simulation using Three.js, a popular 3D JavaScript library. The prompt was straightforward: build a drone flying simulation with textured roads, cars, buildings, smooth flying mechanics controllable with WASD keys, and multiple camera views.

The results were nothing short of incredible. In a single shot, GPT-5 generated a simulation that featured:

  • Realistic cars that follow the roads and turn at intersections
  • Fluid, intuitive drone flying controls with smooth acceleration and deceleration
  • Multiple camera views including trailing, overhead, chase, and behind-the-drone perspectives
  • Dynamic shadows that realistically follow the drone and objects

While collision detection wasn’t included in the initial build, it was clear that this could be easily added in subsequent iterations. The level of detail and the smooth mechanics demonstrated in this one-shot creation represent the best AI-generated drone simulation of its kind seen so far. This example highlights GPT-5’s ability to understand complex spatial and motion dynamics from a simple prompt and produce a working, interactive 3D environment.

🏙️ Procedural 3D City Builder with Brutalist Architecture

Inspired by a desire to create a procedural city builder featuring brutalist architectural designs, GPT-5 was tasked with generating a system where users can drag, edit, and place buildings dynamically. Brutalist architecture is characterized by stark, geometric, and blocky concrete structures, and the AI captured this aesthetic with remarkable fidelity.

Within just under a minute, GPT-5 produced code that allowed:

  • Generation of customizable buildings with adjustable parameters such as number of floors, base width and depth, floor height, and tapering
  • Dragging and precise positioning of buildings within the scene using directional arrows
  • Randomization of building features based on seeds, allowing for diverse, unique structures
  • Duplicating buildings to quickly fill out city blocks

Though the initial version was somewhat monochrome — fitting the brutalist style but lacking vibrancy — the AI’s foundational work provided a solid platform for further development. The grid system could be improved for better visibility, and adding color or lighting effects would enhance realism. Nevertheless, the fact that GPT-5 understood and implemented a procedural building generator with interactive editing in one shot is a testament to its advanced comprehension and coding skills.

🚂 3D Train and Cargo Management Simulation

Next, GPT-5 tackled a more complex simulation involving trains traveling on railroads, picking up and delivering cargo such as wood, metal, and oil. The prompt included requirements for:

  • Resource tracking at various stations (Wood Bay, Steel Yard, Oil Depot)
  • Train control with adjustable track switching
  • Dynamic cargo loading and unloading based on train preferences
  • Visual indicators showing train cargo status

The AI delivered a fully functional system where trains moved along tracks, stopped at stations to pick up or drop off materials, and switched tracks automatically based on user settings. Each train’s cargo was tracked in real-time, and users could control the trains’ behavior to prioritize different resources.

While the track layout was basic with sharp 90-degree turns—something that could be enhanced in future iterations—the core mechanics were well implemented. This demonstration highlighted GPT-5’s exceptional ability to follow detailed instructions, manage stateful game elements, and produce a working resource logistics simulation in a single prompt.

🌕 Moon Landing Game: Realistic Low-Gravity Simulation

To showcase GPT-5’s versatility in game development, it was assigned the task of creating a 3D moon landing game using Three.js. The game needed to simulate low gravity, realistic textures, a heads-up display, and mouse-controlled camera movement. The player would use the spacebar to control thrust and safely land a lunar module on the moon’s surface.

GPT-5 produced an impressive game that included:

  • Textured moon surface with realistic lighting and shadow effects
  • Visible thrust flames activated by the spacebar
  • Low gravity physics allowing slow, controlled descents
  • Camera controls for looking around (though with some limitations, such as inability to look underneath the module)
  • A landing mechanic that rewarded smooth touchdowns and penalized crashes

The game captured the essence of a lunar landing experience well, with smooth physics and immersive visuals. Minor improvements could make it even better, such as enhanced starfield backgrounds and more flexible camera angles. Still, this project exemplifies GPT-5’s capability to combine physics simulation, graphics rendering, and user interaction flawlessly in a single attempt.

🪓 Near One-Shot Minecraft Clone: Pushing AI Boundaries

Perhaps the most ambitious project tested was a full 3D Minecraft clone with core functionalities like block digging and placement, crafting, armor, weapons, and day-night cycles. The prompt was complex and even slightly jumbled during input, but GPT-5 rose to the challenge and delivered a working prototype that included:

  • 3D world navigation with smooth movement, jumping, and camera control
  • Collision detection preventing the player from passing through blocks like trees
  • Mining and placing blocks with a functional crafting system
  • Basic game mechanics resembling early Minecraft beta versions

While some bugs appeared—such as errors related to pointer locking that interfered with digging mechanics—the overall functionality was remarkably close to a full Minecraft experience. GPT-5 managed to include many complex systems in one go, with the main limitations stemming from browser security restrictions rather than lack of AI capability.

This demonstration is a clear sign that GPT-5 is capable of tackling large, multifaceted projects with minimal prompting. It also underscores the AI’s impressive instruction-following skills and its ability to generate intricate codebases quickly.
