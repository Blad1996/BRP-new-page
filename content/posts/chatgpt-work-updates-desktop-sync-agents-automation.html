ChatGPT 5.6 and ChatGPT Work have brought a ridiculous number of new features into one place. We are talking about desktop and web syncing properly, computer control, smarter agent settings, plugins, custom skills, website creation, schedules, and much more.

The big shift is simple: ChatGPT is moving beyond being a place to ask questions. It is becoming a workspace that can connect to your tools, understand your files, perform complex tasks in parallel, automate recurring work, and even build things for you.

That also means your settings matter more than ever. If you have not updated your desktop app and reviewed the new controls, you may be missing some of ChatGPT's most capable functionality.

Start by Updating the ChatGPT Desktop App

The first thing to do is make sure your ChatGPT desktop app is fully updated. On the Mac desktop app, click ChatGPT in the upper-left corner and select Check for Updates.

This update matters because the desktop experience is now much more aligned with the web and mobile versions of ChatGPT. Previously, people ran into frustrating gaps where projects, chat history, and memory did not carry cleanly from one environment to another. The updated app is designed to bring those experiences together.

After updating, you should be able to access the following from the desktop sidebar:

  • Conversation history
  • Projects
  • Synced chat and work history across web, mobile, and desktop
  • Quick switching between Chat and Work modes

Local tasks remain on your computer, which makes sense. But the larger workflow, including your conversations and projects, is much more unified now.

Chat Mode vs. ChatGPT Work Mode

ChatGPT now gives you a much clearer choice between two ways of working.

Chat Mode

Chat mode is what most people already know. You ask questions, brainstorm ideas, write content, analyze information, and explore topics. It is perfect when you need a fast answer, a second opinion, or help thinking through something.

Work Mode

ChatGPT Work is where things get seriously powerful. It is designed for completing real tasks using files, apps, connected tools, research, and agent-style workflows. Rather than simply giving you a text response, Work can handle a multi-step assignment and produce multiple deliverables.

Think about the difference between asking, “Can you help me plan a video?” and asking ChatGPT to research a topic, generate title ideas, write an SEO description, create tags, produce a script in your normal format, and create a thumbnail concept.

That used to be a multi-hour process involving research, writing, editing, and multiple tools. ChatGPT Work is built to complete much of that process in one workflow.

Desktop Settings to Enable for Computer Use

Once the desktop app is updated, open Settings from the lower-left corner. There are several areas worth reviewing, especially if you want ChatGPT to do more than basic chatting.

Computer Use and App Connections

Under computer use settings, you can install and connect supported apps and extensions. Examples shown include a Google Chrome extension and Microsoft Excel.

These connections are important because they allow ChatGPT to work with the software you use. Depending on the permissions you provide, that can mean interacting with browser tasks, working with spreadsheets, and carrying out actions in apps rather than only explaining how to do them.

You may also see an option to let ChatGPT use your Mac when it is locked. This is a powerful setting, but it is not one to turn on blindly. Enable it only if it aligns with your workflow and your comfort level around access.

Browser Controls

In the browser settings, ChatGPT can be allowed to control its built-in browser. You can choose whether browser actions open in ChatGPT or in your default browser. Using your default browser can make the process feel more natural when you need to review or take over a task.

There are also separate destination settings for local URLs. Take a few minutes to review browser history, access management, and permissions.

Be especially intentional about granting access to sensitive information such as:

  • Passwords
  • Contact details
  • Location data
  • Browsing information

More access can make ChatGPT more helpful, but it also means more responsibility on your side. Give it only what is genuinely necessary for the work you want done.

AppShots for Better Screen Context

Another useful setting is AppShots. When enabled, it gives ChatGPT a clearer view of what is happening on your screen so it has more context about your current task.

This can be extremely useful when you are troubleshooting something, working across multiple applications, or need help understanding what is on screen. Context makes the AI better, faster, and far less likely to guess incorrectly.

Important ChatGPT Web Settings: Higher Intelligence and Ultra Effort

The desktop and web apps do not have identical settings, so you should check the web version too. In the web app settings, two options stand out immediately: Higher Intelligence and Enable Ultra Effort.

Higher Intelligence

Higher Intelligence allows ChatGPT to automatically choose a stronger intelligence level when you ask it a more complicated question. Instead of manually trying to decide which model or capability level to use for every task, ChatGPT can make a smarter choice based on what you are asking.

This is particularly helpful for difficult planning tasks, deep research, multi-step analysis, and complex work that needs more than a quick answer.

Enable Ultra Effort

Ultra Effort allows ChatGPT to use multiple agents in parallel for ambitious tasks. This is a major deal.

Without parallel work, a system might complete one step, then start the next, then move to the third. With multiple agents working at the same time, different paths can be handled simultaneously. Research can be happening while a script is being structured and outputs are being prepared.

That means more capable work can also get done faster. For bigger assignments, parallelism is what changes ChatGPT from a useful assistant into something that feels much closer to a real production workflow.

Customize ChatGPT Voice, Tone, and Formatting

The new voice controls are far more flexible. You can choose from voice options such as Live, Advanced, and Standard, then adjust the intelligence level between instant, medium, and high.

The best option depends on what you are doing. A quick back-and-forth may not need the highest intelligence setting. A detailed planning session or advanced task may be worth the extra capability.

Personalization also has more depth now. You can adjust ChatGPT's base style and tone, including characteristics such as:

  • How warm or direct the responses feel
  • How enthusiastic the writing is
  • How often it uses headers and lists
  • Whether it prefers concise formatting or fuller paragraphs
  • Whether it uses emojis

This is more important than it sounds. If you use ChatGPT every day, getting the tone and formatting right saves time in almost every conversation. You should not have to keep repeating that you want concise answers, clear headers, less hype, or a certain writing style.

Review Data Controls and Cloud Browser Permissions

As ChatGPT becomes more connected to browsers, apps, files, and automations, data controls matter a lot more. Go through the data settings and choose permissions based on what you are comfortable sharing.

The cloud browser settings may also allow you to choose an always-allow option. If you routinely ask ChatGPT to perform browser-based tasks and are confident in your permissions, this can remove repetitive confirmation requests.

But do not confuse convenience with a reason to approve everything. A better approach is to enable broader permissions only for workflows you understand and trust.

Usage Tracking Means You Need to Value Max, Not Token Max

One major change is that ChatGPT now makes usage more visible. Your activity can be tracked across areas such as Codex, Work, workspace agents, and ChatGPT itself. Both the web and desktop experiences can show your plan, credits, and usage by tool.

That can become frustrating if you use advanced models and agent workflows heavily. Complex tasks, automations, research, and connected app actions can consume usage faster than ordinary chatting.

The answer is not to avoid powerful tools. The answer is to use them for work that creates real value.

Stop token maxing and start value maxing.

How ChatGPT Work Can Replace Hours of Content Production

A practical example makes ChatGPT Work click immediately. Imagine you want to create a YouTube video about how the TikTok algorithm works in 2026.

Instead of doing every part manually, you can give ChatGPT Work a detailed task that asks for:

  1. Three title ideas
  2. An SEO-optimized description
  3. SEO tags based on the titles and description
  4. A full script in your usual format
  5. A thumbnail concept or image

ChatGPT Work can research the topic, pull from sources, create the requested outputs, and package everything together. It can even match your usual call to action and script format when it has the right context.

The completed package can include title options, a recommended title, metadata, a long-form script, a short-form script, research insights, and source links. That is a huge jump from getting a single generic draft.

The key is not to hand over a vague request. Give the system clear deliverables, context, desired style, and the outcome you need. The more specific the assignment, the more useful the final work package becomes.

Plugins, MCPs, and Skills Make ChatGPT More Useful

ChatGPT Work gets much stronger when you connect it to the tools and services you already use.

Plugins

Plugins can connect ChatGPT to external apps for productivity, data analysis, and business tasks. For example, a Stripe connection could help retrieve customer lists, show products, or surface pricing information.

That matters because the AI can work with your actual business environment instead of operating in isolation.

MCP Connections

You can also connect ChatGPT to MCPs, which expand what it can access and do. A YouTube-related MCP, for example, can provide the type of context and capabilities that make a video production task much more effective.

Custom Skills

Skills are instructions that extend ChatGPT's capabilities. You can create them directly in a chat, use an editor, or upload them from your computer.

This is where you start turning repeated workflows into reusable systems. If you have a specific way of writing content, reviewing customer data, preparing reports, or handling recurring tasks, build a skill around it.

Start with the tools and processes you use every day. Connecting random apps just because they are available is not the goal. Build a setup that supports real work you already need to do.

ChatGPT Sites Can Build and Publish Websites

Another wild addition is ChatGPT Sites. From the desktop app, you can open Sites, click create, describe the website you want, and have ChatGPT build it.

Once the site is ready, you can adjust sharing permissions and publish it so it is available online. ChatGPT can design the site, create the structure, and produce a functional published result from a description.

This does not mean you should publish blindly. You still need to review the layout, copy, permissions, calls to action, and overall quality. But for landing pages, simple project sites, early concepts, and fast experiments, the speed is honestly insane.

Schedules Bring Automation Into ChatGPT

Schedules take the platform even further. You can create automations for recurring tasks, including work inside connected plugins, research workflows, and actions involving your computer.

That opens the door to using ChatGPT as an active system rather than a tool you only open when you remember to ask something.

Examples of the type of work this can support include:

  • Recurring research tasks
  • Automated actions inside connected apps
  • Regular workflow checks
  • Scheduled task execution on your computer
  • Repeatable processes powered by custom skills

Add Codex into the bigger picture and ChatGPT becomes even more capable for technical and coding-related tasks. The platform is increasingly built around one idea: connect to what you use, understand the task, and get work done.

The Best Way to Use These New ChatGPT Features

Do not try to turn on every setting and connect every app in one afternoon. That is the fastest way to create an expensive, confusing setup that does not actually improve your work.

Instead, follow this simple process:

  1. Update the desktop app and confirm history and projects are syncing.
  2. Enable Higher Intelligence and Ultra Effort for complex work.
  3. Review computer use, browser, AppShots, and data permissions carefully.
  4. Adjust personalization so the responses match how you like to work.
  5. Connect one or two high-value apps you already use.
  6. Create a custom skill for a repeated workflow.
  7. Use ChatGPT Work on a task that normally takes multiple hours.
  8. Check usage regularly and focus your spend on outcomes with real value.

That is how you get the upside of ChatGPT 5.6 and ChatGPT Work without simply burning through credits. The goal is not more AI activity. The goal is better results, faster execution, and less manual busywork.

FAQ

What is ChatGPT Work?

ChatGPT Work is a task-focused mode built for more complex workflows. It can work with files, apps, research, connected tools, and agent-style processes to produce multiple deliverables from one assignment.

Should I enable Higher Intelligence and Ultra Effort?

These settings are especially useful for difficult, multi-step tasks. Higher Intelligence helps ChatGPT choose stronger capability when needed, while Ultra Effort allows multiple agents to work in parallel. Review your usage, since advanced work can consume more credits.

What does AppShots do in ChatGPT?

AppShots gives ChatGPT more visual context about what is happening on your screen. This can make it more useful for troubleshooting, understanding an active workflow, and helping with tasks across different applications.

Can ChatGPT build a website?

ChatGPT Sites allows you to describe the website you want, generate it, manage sharing permissions, and publish it online. Review the finished site carefully before making it public.
