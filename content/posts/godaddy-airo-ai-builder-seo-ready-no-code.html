Starting a build from a single prompt

The starting point can be as simple as one sentence. For example: “I want to build a website for my AI agency that’s focused on helping creators automate their workflow.”

Click Start Building for Free, and the builder turns that prompt into a guided conversation. The goal is not to make you write an enormous technical specification. The AI simply gathers the context it needs to create something relevant rather than generic.

That makes this especially useful if you know the outcome you want but do not know how to describe the design, site architecture, code, integrations, or hosting requirements behind it.

Answering AI’s guided questions: name, audience, offer, style, tone

After the initial prompt, Airo asks practical questions to shape the website. In the Creator OS example, the business name was set as Creator OS, the audience was social media influencers and content creators, and the offers included automation services or courses.

It also asks for brand direction. The visual style selected was bold and futuristic, while the brand voice was practical and results-driven.

These details matter because a site for content creators should not look or sound like a corporate legal firm, and a course business should not be positioned like a local plumbing company. The more useful context you provide, the better the AI can align the copy, colors, layout, imagery, calls to action, and overall brand personality.

What to give the builder

  • Your business name: A working name is enough.
  • Your ideal customer: Be specific about who you are trying to help.
  • Your main offer: Services, courses, products, consultations, or something else.
  • Your desired style: Bold, minimal, futuristic, premium, playful, or another clear direction.
  • Your tone: Educational, direct, friendly, professional, practical, or results-driven.

Voice prompting: hands-free building

You are not limited to typing. The microphone option lets you use voice prompting, which is honestly useful when you have a lot of thoughts and do not want to organize them perfectly before getting started.

AI tools are often at their best when you give them a real brain dump. Explain what you like, what feels wrong, who the customer is, what the site needs to accomplish, and what you want changed. You do not need to speak in code or design terminology. You can explain it in normal language.

That hands-free workflow means you can communicate the direction while moving around, working from your phone, or simply thinking out loud.

Parallel build: hero, header, footer, logo

One reason the build happens so quickly is that Airo does not work through every component one at a time. It builds key site elements in parallel.

For Creator OS, it generated a custom colour palette, selected fonts, established the hero section and image direction, then worked on the header, footer, and logo at the same time. That is a much faster experience than manually choosing each piece separately.

The result is a site that starts with a more consistent identity from day one. Instead of ending up with a random logo, mismatched fonts, and colours that were chosen one by one, you have a coordinated brand foundation to refine.

Editing text, style, and design with AI chat, zero coding needed

Building the first version is only step one. You still need to make it yours. Airo makes edits conversationally, so you can click an element and tell the AI what you want changed.

You can replace text directly, fix typos and grammar, add a reference, or ask for higher-level improvements. For example, instead of trying to write conversion copy from scratch, you can ask the AI to make a headline more convincing or improve the hook.

In the Creator OS example, a headline was revised to lead with a desired outcome for creators, focusing on more output and less grind rather than opening with a command. That is exactly the kind of small messaging improvement that can make a section feel sharper without requiring copywriting expertise.

You can also preview the design on other device sizes, including mobile, so you are not publishing a desktop-only experience.

Automatic SEO/AEO audit + one-click fixes

Airo can run an SEO and AEO audit after your site has been built or edited. The audit identifies items that may need attention for search visibility and AI search readiness, then gives you a direct way to review the details.

Better still, you can ask it to make the improvements using a plain-language command, such as asking it to fix the SEO and AEO changes identified earlier. The builder can work through multiple files and changes quickly.
