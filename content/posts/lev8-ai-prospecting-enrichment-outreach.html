What Is Lev8 AI?

Lev8 is an AI teammate for sales prospecting, client acquisition, partnerships, sponsorships, recruiting, and any other workflow where you need to find the right companies and start relevant conversations.

The core process is straightforward:

  1. Find prospects based on a plain English description of your ideal customer.
  2. Verify the fit against the criteria you provide.
  3. Enrich lead data with company and contact information.
  4. Score opportunities based on how well they match your service or offer.
  5. Research and draft outreach that reflects your goals, tone, and chosen channel.
  6. Schedule recurring tasks to keep prospect lists fresh over time.

That combination is what makes it useful. Most tools stop at a generic list of companies. The real work begins after the list is created: figuring out whether the prospect is actually qualified, who owns the decision, what is happening in the business right now, and what you can say that does not sound like every other cold message in their inbox.

Start With Your Ideal Customer in Plain English

One of the best parts of the Lev8 workflow is that you can describe the target market normally. You do not need to build a complicated search with filters and Boolean operators before you can get started.

For example, a request could be:

Find 10 SaaS companies in North America or Europe, founded before 2021, with 5 to 200 employees, that are currently hiring for sales leadership roles.

From there, Lev8 asks clarifying questions and presents the search approach. You can specify what must be included, add optional qualities, choose the target number of results, review estimated credit use, and exclude companies already saved to your list.

This matters because good prospecting is not about generating the largest possible spreadsheet. It is about producing a list that is specific enough to act on. A company hiring sales leadership, for example, may be a stronger fit for certain services than a company that merely happens to operate in the right industry.

What the Results Can Include

Once the search runs, the resulting table can include company names, descriptions, social links, match status, job-posting information, and scores against the criteria you gave. You can also adjust which columns are visible, export results, save them to a library, or review the work in full-screen mode.

More importantly, the system does not only surface apparent matches. It can also show companies that were skipped, explain where they missed the criteria, and make it easier to decide whether your original request was too broad or too restrictive.

That feedback loop is valuable. If you only get a few qualified companies, that is not necessarily a failure. It may simply mean your targeting is doing its job. For a high-value service, partnership, or sponsorship, a small list of truly relevant opportunities can be far better than hundreds of weak leads.

Turn Raw Leads Into Prospect Profiles With AI Enrichment

Finding companies is only the first layer. The next step is lead enrichment, where Lev8 turns a basic company list into information you can actually use to make decisions and begin outreach.

You can ask it to add fields such as:

  • Founder names
  • Email addresses
  • Employee count
  • Industry
  • Company website
  • Recent hiring activity
  • Location
  • Revenue range
  • Company type
  • Technology stack
  • Founding year

The practical benefit is simple: instead of researching every company manually, you can ask for the business context that matters to your offer. If you sell AI automations, you may care about company size, business model, hiring activity, and signs that the team has a genuine need for automation support.

After enrichment, you can ask Lev8 to apply a fit score from one to 10. The scoring criteria can include company size, recent activity, business model, and the likelihood that the company needs your service.

Lev8 also asks for details rather than blindly assuming what you sell. When setting up a fit score, you can explain the service, such as AI automations, so the qualification process has a clearer frame of reference. That is a much better approach than allowing an AI tool to invent a definition of your ideal customer.

Use an Existing CSV or CRM List Instead of Starting From Scratch

You do not have to begin with a brand-new prospect search. If you already have a CSV of company data, domains, or LinkedIn URLs, you can upload it and use Lev8 to enrich the list.

This is especially useful for businesses that already have leads sitting in a CRM but lack the context needed to prioritize them. A spreadsheet with company names and web domains is not a pipeline. It becomes a pipeline when you know what the companies do, how large they are, where they are located, what they use, and who is most relevant to contact.

An enrichment request might ask Lev8 to identify each company’s industry, employee count, location, revenue range, company type, tech stack, and founding year. Rather than rebuilding your list elsewhere, you can use the records you already have and make them more useful.

Research-Led Outreach Is the Difference

The outreach workflow goes beyond simply finding a name and attaching a generic template. You can ask Lev8 to conduct deeper research into each company, identify the person worth contacting, locate a relevant campaign or recent activity, and create a personalized message based on that research.

For example, someone searching for a marketing role could ask Lev8 to find the right contact at each company and draft LinkedIn outreach specific to a campaign the company recently ran. The point is not to send more cold messages. The point is to make each message feel connected to something real.

When you set up the outreach, Lev8 asks for inputs such as:

  • Who the sender is
  • The sender’s experience and background
  • The goal of the message
  • The desired tone
  • The channel, such as email or LinkedIn
  • The message format, including LinkedIn InMail
  • The action you want the recipient to take

Those questions are important. A message for a sponsorship proposal should not sound like a message for a job application. A warm, conversational partnership email should not sound like a highly research-led LinkedIn message. Your goal, your credibility, and your call to action shape whether the outreach feels authentic.

Lev8 can create a preview of the drafts before you take the next step. This gives you the opportunity to review the contact selected, inspect the personalized angle, and make sure the copy sounds like it came from you.

Use Case 1: Find Sponsorships and Partnership Opportunities

As a creator, I am constantly looking for companies that could sponsor content, sponsor a newsletter, or become long-term partners. Historically, that work meant either doing the research myself or paying a virtual assistant to do it. Both routes can consume a serious amount of time and money.

A highly specific Lev8 prompt could look for AI and SaaS companies that raised funding in the past six months and are either doing influencer marketing already or could benefit from it. A head of influencers or partnerships is also a strong signal, because it points to a person and function that may already be focused on this kind of collaboration.

From there, the list can be enriched with funding details and key personnel. Lev8 can identify how recently a company raised, pull relevant people and titles, score the opportunity, and then draft personalized sponsorship outreach.

For high-value sponsorships, it is completely normal to have a small number of strong matches. One partnership can be worth a significant amount to a media business, so precision matters much more than volume.

Use Case 2: Find the Right Decision Maker and Create Personalized Outreach

Once you have a qualified company list, the next challenge is reaching the right person. Contacting a random employee is not prospecting. You need the person responsible for the decision, whether that is a founder, partnership lead, hiring manager, or another relevant stakeholder.

Lev8 can enrich a list with the individual to contact and generate outreach for each person. For a sponsorship campaign, the sender profile could explain that Rob is an AI creator with millions of monthly views who is looking to form a brand partnership. The outreach goal could be a sponsorship proposal, with a warm and conversational tone.

The platform does the work in parallel, which means it can research multiple companies and contacts at once rather than forcing you through a one-by-one process. It can also produce a sequence, which matters because many worthwhile opportunities do not respond to the first message.

The available outreach actions include email, LinkedIn messaging, and X, with WhatsApp noted as coming soon. That makes it easier to work from the channel where the relationship is most likely to begin.

Use Case 3: Keep Your Prospecting List Updated Every Week

The final piece is automation. A good prospect list is not static. Companies hire, raise funding, change priorities, and add new leadership roles constantly. A list that was useful a month ago may already be outdated.

Lev8 can turn an existing workflow into a recurring scheduled task. For example, you can ask it to refresh a SaaS prospect list every Sunday at 9 a.m. Eastern. The recurring task can search for new companies, rerun enrichment, update fit scores, and summarize the results.

You can then manage the task from the tasks area, review results, pause the automation, or cancel it when needed. This is where the tool becomes more than a one-time lead generator. It becomes a recurring research system that continues doing the manual work you would otherwise need to do yourself or outsource.

Why Better Cold Outreach Is Not About Sending More Messages

Cold outreach gets a bad reputation because most cold outreach is lazy. It is generic, sent to the wrong person, disconnected from the company’s current priorities, and impossible to distinguish from the dozens of similar messages people receive.

The better approach is research, relevance, and clarity. Identify a company that actually fits. Find the person who has a reason to care. Use a real business signal, such as a funding round, hiring activity, a campaign, or an interview. Then send a message in your own voice with a simple next step.

That is the opportunity with an AI prospecting agent like Lev8. It can handle the heavy lifting of finding, verifying, enriching, and organizing information, while you remain responsible for your offer, positioning, judgment, and relationships.
