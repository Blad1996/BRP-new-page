Google Gemini just rolled out a huge wave of updates across Gemini Notebook, Gemini Spark, Chrome, image generation, video creation, skills, schedules, and more. These are not tiny interface tweaks. They fundamentally change how you can research, write, create content, organize work, and use AI across your day.

The big theme is simple: Gemini is becoming much more than a chatbot. It is turning into a connected AI workspace that can understand your sources, use information from your tabs, create videos, learn your writing style, and proactively help you act on external events.

Here is what matters most and how to use these new Google Gemini updates effectively.

NotebookLM Is Becoming Gemini Notebook

The first major update is the rename of NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook. Google describes it as a new name for the same great product, but there is clearly much more happening beneath the surface.

The biggest upgrade is that every notebook can now have access to a secure cloud computer. That means Gemini Notebook can write and execute code natively while staying grounded in the sources you provide.

This is a big deal for anyone doing work that goes beyond simple summarization. Standard AI models are great at condensing information, but they can struggle when a task involves complex math, detailed data work, or analysis that needs to be checked against source materials.

With the cloud computer capability, Gemini Notebook can help power:

  • More complex data analysis based on the documents and sources inside a notebook
  • New output formats for presenting research and findings
  • Code execution that supports deeper analytical tasks
  • More portable notebooks that can sync across more places

The feature is initially available for Google AI Ultra and Workspace Business customers, with broader availability expected over time. The practical takeaway is that Gemini Notebook is moving closer to being a serious research and analysis environment, not just a place to upload files and ask questions.

Make Gemini Notebooks Easier to Share

Another useful Gemini Notebook update is the ability to customize the presentation of a notebook. You can now add a custom cover, title, and notebook summary.

Think of it like the opening slide of a presentation. Instead of sharing a generic notebook with a vague name, you can make it immediately clear what the project is, what it contains, and why it matters.

This becomes especially valuable when sharing work with a team, clients, classmates, or stakeholders. A polished notebook can include:

  • A custom cover that visually identifies the project
  • A clear title that states the topic or outcome
  • A custom summary that explains the purpose and major findings

That may sound simple, but clean presentation matters. If your notebook is the central place where research, chats, documents, and generated assets live, it should be understandable before anyone even starts exploring its contents.

Turn Research and Blog Posts Into Short-Form AI Videos

One of the coolest features is Gemini Notebook’s ability to generate short video overviews. You can take a source, report, article, or notebook topic and turn it into short-form content complete with visuals and voiceover.

This is powerful because search behavior is changing. Search results increasingly include video results and short-video placements alongside traditional webpages. If you are creating reports, blog posts, or educational resources, it is no longer enough to publish only the written version.

Short-form content gives you another way to distribute the same core idea. A single well-researched article can potentially become:

  • A full blog post
  • A summarized video overview
  • Multiple short-form clips
  • Social media content
  • A visual explainer for a client or team

The impressive part is that Gemini can create the video, produce the voiceover, and assemble the visual presentation. That makes it much easier for someone without editing experience to create content from ideas or materials they have already developed.

For creators and businesses, this is an important visibility play. If people are searching a topic through short-video results, you want your ideas and expertise represented in those placements too.

Create and Organize Notebooks Directly Inside Gemini

Gemini Notebook is also becoming more deeply connected to the main Gemini experience. You can create notebooks from inside Gemini and use them in two different ways.

Use Gemini Notebooks for Learning

You can build a notebook around a subject you want to understand, ask questions, generate custom lessons, and track your progress. For example, if you want to understand how MCP works in AI, you can create a notebook around that topic and ask Gemini to explain it in very simple language.

MCP, or Model Context Protocol, can be thought of as a universal connector that helps AI models interact with data and tools. A notebook gives you a focused space to gather explainers, chats, sources, and notes around a topic like that.

Use Gemini Notebooks to Organize Ideas

You can also use a notebook to collect conversations, group chats by topic, save sources, and ground your work in specific materials. This bridges the gap between Gemini as a conversational AI tool and Gemini Notebook as a source-based workspace.

Instead of losing useful chats in a long history, you can turn relevant conversations into part of an organized knowledge base. That is a much better workflow for research, content creation, planning, and ongoing projects.

New Image and Video Templates Expand Creative Options

Gemini is adding a large range of video and image templates. These templates cover creative formats such as memes, montages, pixelated content, comic-book-style visuals, music videos, moon-themed concepts, talking pets, and more.

The main benefit is speed. Rather than beginning with a blank canvas and trying to figure out how to describe a style from scratch, you can start with a format built for a particular creative direction.

Gemini also gives you different model options, including variations such as Flash and Pro models. Choosing a different model can affect the image model being used in the background, which can change the output style, speed, and overall result.

The important point is not to blindly use one model for every task. Experiment with the available options. A fast model can be useful for ideation and quick drafts, while a more capable model may be better when visual quality or complexity matters.

Better Prompts Create Better Gemini Results

One of the biggest lessons from these updates is that prompting still matters. You can ask Gemini to write a blog post with a quick one-line instruction, but the result may be poorly structured, incomplete, or difficult to publish without a lot of cleanup.

A stronger prompt tells the AI exactly what it needs to know. Instead of writing, “Create a blog post about how MCP works in AI,” provide the context, task, desired objective, required knowledge, length, formatting requirements, and output expectations.

A well-structured prompt should typically clarify:

  1. The situation: What is the broader context for the request?
  2. The task: What specifically should Gemini create?
  3. The objective: What should the final output achieve?
  4. The required knowledge: Which concepts, sources, or details must be included?
  5. The format: How should the result be organized and delivered?

For example, rather than asking for a general article, you might request a 1,000-word post with a title, headings, introduction, conclusion, image suggestions, and a specific explanation level. That changes the quality of the output dramatically.

The difference is huge. Better instructions create a more detailed, better formatted, more usable result. If you are creating content regularly, prompt structure is one of the highest-leverage skills you can develop.

Create AI Videos With Your Own Avatar

Gemini can now create videos using a personal avatar. You can upload a photo in the avatar settings, then ask Gemini to generate a video of your avatar explaining a topic.

For example, you could ask it to create an explainer about MCP in AI. Gemini can generate the script delivery, voice, animations, expressions, and visual elements around the explanation.

The avatar is intended for your own use, and you can retake or update it when needed. That is worth doing. If your hairstyle, beard, clothing, or overall appearance changes, an older avatar may not accurately represent how you currently look.

This opens up a lot of opportunities for content production. You can create multiple clips, combine them into longer pieces, and build short-form or long-form AI-generated content without recording every segment manually.

The technology is not a replacement for being intentional about your message. It is a way to remove friction between having an idea and turning that idea into usable content.

Gemini Skills Can Learn Your Writing Style

Gemini Spark introduces Skills, which can be created with Gemini, made manually, or uploaded. One of the most interesting examples is a skill designed to match your writing style.

This kind of always-on skill can analyze writing across your Workspace apps and build a persistent voice profile. It can learn patterns such as:

  • Sentence length
  • Vocabulary preferences
  • Tone of voice
  • Typical openings and phrasing
  • General writing habits

That means Gemini can use your emails, documents, and Drive content to better understand how you communicate. Once the skill is created, you can invoke it using a slash command and apply it whenever you need help drafting content.

This could be incredibly useful for emails, client communications, social posts, internal documents, and any writing where generic AI language would feel out of place.

Skills are also accessible through Ask Gemini in Chrome, making them available while you work across the web.

Ask Gemini in Chrome Can Understand Your Open Tabs

Ask Gemini in Chrome has received major upgrades. It is powered by Gemini Spark, allows model selection, and can automatically choose the best model for the task when Auto mode is enabled.

One of the most practical features is tab context. By using the at symbol, you can reference a specific open tab and ask Gemini to work with it. For example, you can ask Gemini to summarize a YouTube video or analyze information in another page you have open.

You can also reference multiple tabs. This is where it gets seriously useful.

Imagine having several resources open while researching a topic. Instead of manually copying information from each page into a document, you can ask Gemini to use the context from those tabs to create:

  • A script
  • An email
  • A summary
  • A set of action items
  • A comparison of the sources
  • A research-based draft

You can open new chats quickly, use skills like Explain Simply, Action Items, or Translate Text, browse additional skills, and move useful responses into a new chat. Gemini also includes chat history in this environment.

That is why Gemini in Chrome feels like a major shift. Instead of having to keep a separate AI tab open all the time, AI support can be available directly alongside the work you are already doing.

Event-Based Schedules Make Gemini More Proactive

Schedules inside Google Spark are no longer limited to tasks that run only at set times. You can now create schedules that respond to events or continuously monitor and react to changes.

Previously, you might set up a recurring task for Gemini to check email or provide a regular update. Now you can create a workflow that responds to an external event, such as a new real-estate listing appearing in a specific area.

This completely changes the role of schedules. They are not just time-based reminders anymore. They can become proactive monitors that help you stay on top of changes that matter.

You can create schedules manually or ask Gemini to create and edit event-based schedules for you. The basic process is straightforward:

  1. Give Gemini clear instructions for what to monitor or respond to.
  2. Name the schedule so it is easy to recognize.
  3. Set how often it should run or define the triggering event.
  4. Review usage limits to make sure the workflow can continue running.

Gemini provides usage-limit information in settings so you can see how much of your allowance has been used. Schedules may use more of that limit during peak periods, and they will not run when the available limit has been reached.

Daily Briefs Can Organize Your Entire Day

The final major update is the Daily Brief. Gemini can now provide a personalized overview of your day in one place, drawing from your calendar, Drive, email, chat, and other digital activity.

The purpose is to cut through digital clutter and make it clear what needs your attention first. Instead of opening multiple apps and trying to reconstruct your day, you can start with an organized summary of what is ahead.

A useful Daily Brief can help surface:

  • Calendar commitments and upcoming meetings
  • Important emails that need a response
  • Files or documents connected to current work
  • Relevant chat activity
  • Priority tasks worth acting on immediately

This is one of those features that could become part of a daily workflow very quickly. The more your calendar, Gmail, Drive, and chat tools are connected, the more valuable a proactive overview becomes.

The Bigger Shift: Gemini Is Becoming an AI Operating Layer

When you put all of these Google Gemini updates together, the direction is obvious. Gemini is becoming an AI layer across research, writing, web browsing, content creation, planning, and productivity.

Gemini Notebook helps you ground work in real sources. Skills help make outputs sound more like you. Chrome context lets you work across active tabs. Schedules can react to the world around you. Daily Briefs help organize your attention. AI avatars and creative templates make it easier to turn knowledge into content.

The people who get the most value from these tools will not just use them to ask random questions. They will build repeatable workflows around research, better prompts, source organization, content repurposing, personal skills, and proactive automation.

The upgrade is not just the technology. The upgrade is what you can now build with it.

FAQ

What is Gemini Notebook?

Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM. It is a source-grounded AI workspace that helps organize documents, chats, research, analysis, summaries, and generated outputs in one notebook.

What does the secure cloud computer do in Gemini Notebook?

The secure cloud computer allows Gemini Notebook to write and execute code natively, supporting deeper analysis and more complex data tasks based on the sources inside your notebook.

Can Gemini create short-form videos from written content?

Yes. Gemini Notebook can generate short video overviews from source material, including visuals and voiceover. This can help repurpose articles, reports, and research into short-form content.

How can Gemini help with writing in my own voice?

Gemini Skills can create a persistent writing-style profile using writing across Workspace apps. A skill can learn patterns in your tone, sentence length, vocabulary, and typical phrasing.

Can Ask Gemini in Chrome use information from more than one tab?

Yes. Ask Gemini can reference specific tabs and multiple open tabs, allowing you to create summaries, scripts, emails, and other outputs based on context from several sources.

What are event-based Gemini schedules?

Event-based schedules can respond to external changes or continuously monitor for conditions, rather than running only at a fixed time. For example, a schedule could respond when a new real-estate listing becomes available.
