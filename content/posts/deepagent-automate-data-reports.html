DeepAgent addresses all three. How DeepAgent Works — The Workflow in Plain English ⚙️

At a high level, here’s the flow we use when automating tasks with DeepAgent:

  1. Prompt: Give DeepAgent a clear text instruction describing the task (e.g., "Analyze this booking data and produce a detailed report").
  2. Data input: Upload files from supported cloud drives or your computer (Excel, CSV, PDF, etc.).
  3. Clarifying Qs: DeepAgent will ask follow-up questions (time period, business goals, desired output format).
  4. Processing: It analyzes the data, builds models, creates visuals, and compiles deliverables.
  5. Deliverables: Export as PDF, PowerPoint, spreadsheets, or push to Slack/email—and optionally schedule recurring runs.

That clarifying-questions step is critical. It ensures the agent aligns with your business objective (e.g., increasing revenue vs. cutting costs) and returns results you can act on immediately.

Practical Prompt Examples and Templates ✍️

Below are real prompt templates you can adapt for your organization. Use them as-is or tweak the specifics.

Data analysis & report prompt

Prompt: "Analyze the attached bookings.xlsx file. Please answer: top booking channels, guest origin countries, average spend per night, average length of stay, and top revenue opportunities. Provide a detailed report with an executive summary, charts for seasonality and channel performance, and recommended action items to increase revenue. Use the entire dataset and output as a PDF."

Invoice generator prompt

Prompt: "Create an Excel invoice generator that accepts inputs (client info, invoice items, quantities, unit prices, taxes, discounts). Include a printable invoice tab and validation rules. Provide a user guide document explaining how to use the tool and example output PDF."

DCF model prompt

Prompt: "Build a 5-year DCF model with base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios. Use these assumptions: revenue year 1 = $X, growth rates = A/B/C, margins = M%, capex % of revenue = Z%. Include sensitivity tables and compute value per share. Provide a clear executive summary and charts."

Influencer forecast prompt

Prompt: "Forecast campaign outcomes for the attached influencers.csv. For each influencer, use follower counts, engagement rates, and assumed conversion rates to estimate expected conversions, revenue, and ROI. Provide recommendations for budget allocation and risk notes."

Product listing prompt

Prompt: "For the attached products.csv, generate category, subcategory, SEO-friendly title, concise 150–200-word product description, and product attributes. Use brand voice: [insert brand tone]. Return a CSV-ready file and provide a quality score for each listing."

Integration Tips: Get the Most Out of DeepAgent 🔗

Here are practical tips to ensure reliable, auditable, and secure automations:

  • Start with clean data. The better the input data, the better the output.
  • Provide clear goals (e.g., "objective: increase revenue by 10% in Q3") so analysis is actionable.
  • Use sample outputs in your prompt to force format consistency (e.g., "Return a table with columns: Channel, Bookings, Revenue, Avg Spend").
  • Validate outputs. Use spot-checks and run small pilots before fully automating critical processes.
  • Set up scheduling. Use DeepAgent’s task scheduler to run recurring reports weekly or monthly and route results to Slack or email channels.
  • Maintain security. Use cloud platforms you control (SharePoint, Google Drive) and manage who the agent can notify via Slack/email.

Limitations & Best Practices — What to Watch For ⚠️

DeepAgent is powerful, but it’s not a replacement for judgment. Keep these considerations in mind:

  • Garbage in, garbage out. Bad or incomplete data will produce unreliable outputs.
  • Assumptions matter. In financial modeling, small changes in growth or discount rates can shift valuations dramatically—always include sensitivity analysis.
  • Human review is still necessary for legal documents, compliance-sensitive items, or high-stakes financial decisions.
  • Bias and hallucination: for any generated text (product descriptions, marketing copy), review for brand voice accuracy and factual correctness.
  • Data privacy: confirm that your usage complies with your organization’s data policies—don’t feed sensitive PII unless approved.
