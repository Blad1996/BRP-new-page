PowerPoint has long been the default for presentations, but it often requires hours of manual input, formatting, and research. AI tools are transforming this landscape by automating slide creation, integrating real-time data, and allowing effortless editing. Instead of starting from a blank slide deck, you can now generate high-quality presentations with minimal effort.

The key benefits of AI-powered presentation tools include:

  • Speed: Generate entire slide decks in minutes instead of hours.
  • Research Integration: Access up-to-date, sourced statistics and data.
  • Customization: Easily edit visuals and text with intuitive interfaces.
  • Export Flexibility: Download presentations as PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF, or HTML files with just one click.

With this in mind, I tested Skywork AI, ChatGPT agent mode, and Manus to see which one delivers the best balance of speed, quality, and usability.

⚔️ The Test Setup: Creating a 5-Slide Investor Deck

To ensure a fair comparison, I used the same prompt across all three tools:

Create a five-slide investor deck on the DTC skincare sector.
Slide 1: Introduction and market overview.
Slide 2: Key trends in DTC skincare.
Slide 3: Competitive landscape.
Slide 4: Growth opportunities.
Slide 5: Next steps and contact information.

Please source every statistic, keep visuals clean, and text minimal.

This prompt was designed to test the AI’s ability to conduct research, organize information logically, and produce visually appealing slides that are investor-ready.

⚡ Speed Comparison: Who Finishes First? 🕒

Speed is critical when you need to create presentations on tight deadlines. Here’s how the three tools performed:

  • Skywork AI: Completed the full deck in just a few minutes.
  • Manus: Took roughly double the time to finish all five slides.
  • ChatGPT Agent Mode: Still running after 20-25 minutes, unable to complete the deck.

Skywork AI was by far the fastest, delivering a complete, polished deck within minutes. Manus followed but lagged behind significantly. ChatGPT agent mode struggled to even start slide creation after completing research, showing major delays and inefficiencies.

🎨 Quality and Usability: How Do the Presentations Stack Up?

Skywork AI: Sleek, Research-Backed, and Editable

Skywork’s deck was impressive. It featured a clean, professional design with dynamic graphs and clear sourcing of every statistic. The slides followed the prompt closely, providing a logical flow from market overview to next steps.

Editing was a breeze. You could regenerate individual slides or tweak text and visuals with simple clicks. Skywork also allows you to upload brand templates, color schemes, and even professional data sources like government reports or crypto analytics to tailor your presentation perfectly.

Exporting was seamless—one click and the deck was ready in PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF, or HTML format.

Manus: Functional but Clunky and Less Polished

Manus delivered all five slides but with several drawbacks. The slide layouts suffered from excessive white space and odd color choices, like a pinkish-orange that felt out of place for professional decks. Images were large but lacked proper sourcing, which could pose copyright risks.

Editing was more cumbersome, with no support for image edits and limited formatting flexibility. The “next steps” slide felt generic and didn’t add much value. Manus also took more credits (resource units) per presentation, meaning higher costs for users.

ChatGPT Agent Mode: Slow and Incomplete

Despite its popularity, ChatGPT in agent mode failed this test. Even after nearly half an hour, it hadn’t completed the deck or started generating slides. This delay makes it impractical for presentation creation, especially when compared to the other tools.
