🚀 What is Perplexity Labs and Why It’s a Game Changer

Perplexity Labs is a newly released feature on the Perplexity AI platform designed to take deep research to unprecedented heights. Unlike conventional research tools that provide textual summaries or basic data, Labs offers a fully integrated research experience that includes:

  • Comprehensive source citation: Labs pulls information from dozens of credible sources and cites each one, ensuring transparency and trustworthiness.
  • Multi-tasking research process: It breaks down research into multiple tasks, performing several searches and data syntheses simultaneously.
  • Custom asset creation: Labs doesn’t just regurgitate information—it creates visual assets like graphs, charts, trend lines, and even images to enrich your research report.

Getting started with Perplexity Labs is incredibly straightforward. Here’s how you can harness its power:

  1. Log in to perplexity.ai and locate the new light bulb icon, which represents the Labs feature.
  2. Create a new project: Think of this as your deep research workspace. Enter your research prompt or question here.
  3. Customize your research: Specify parameters such as the geographic focus (e.g., US), sectors of interest (e.g., retail), and request visualizations or statistical analysis.
  4. Launch the research: Labs will automatically conduct multiple searches, synthesize data, and build custom assets.
  5. Monitor progress: You can track all tasks Labs performs, view sources, and watch assets being generated in real time.
  6. Export or share: Once complete, export your research in the format you prefer or share it directly with collaborators.
