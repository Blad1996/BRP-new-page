🛠️ How to Build Your First AI Agent: A Step-by-Step Tutorial

One of the best ways to understand the power of BuildThatIdea.com is to build a custom AI agent together. I chose to create a TikTok Hook Generator, an AI agent designed to craft viral, attention-grabbing hooks for short-form video creators. Here’s how you can do it too:

Step 1: Log In and Start Creating

Head over to BuildThatIdea.com and log in with your email. The platform offers a smooth login experience, instantly recognizing your credentials.

Once logged in, click on Create Agent to begin crafting your AI agent.

Step 2: Define Your Agent’s Purpose

In the AI Studio, describe what your agent will do. For my example, I typed: "I am creating a TikTok hook generator." This description guides the AI in understanding your agent's goal.

Step 3: Generate a Logo Instantly

No design skills? No problem. BuildThatIdea.com integrates with an AI image generator called Imagen Ultra Preview. Simply click “Run,” and within seconds, a unique logo tailored to your agent is created. You can download and upload this logo directly to your agent’s profile.

Step 4: Name Your Agent and Set the URL

Give your agent a catchy name—mine is Viral Talk. The platform auto-generates an available URL based on the name, which you can customize if you want.

Step 5: Craft a Compelling Description

Use AI to write your agent’s description. I used the built-in ChatChibuty assistant to generate a concise and engaging summary:

"Struggling to grab attention in the first three seconds? This AI-powered agent generates scroll-stopping, algorithm-boosting, and engagement-driving hooks custom-built for TikTok. Whether you're sharing a story, teaching a skill, or promoting a project, this agent crafts hooks that make viewers say, 'I need to watch this.'"

Step 6: Upload Training Data and Websites

The magic of AI agents lies in their knowledge base. You can upload PDFs, documents, or provide URLs for the AI to crawl and learn from. For my TikTok hook generator, I uploaded a PDF containing 100 viral hook ideas tailored to various niches.

Additionally, I added relevant websites explaining what makes a viral TikTok hook. While the platform may limit you to scraping one or a few websites for free, this data empowers your agent to generate accurate and relevant responses.

Step 7: Choose the AI Model

BuildThatIdea.com offers multiple AI models like Grok, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Claude 4 Opus, and ChatChibuty 4. The best model depends on your use case, so it’s wise to test a few to see which one performs best for your agent.

Step 8: Write System Prompt Instructions

System prompts guide how your AI behaves. I asked ChatChibuty to generate detailed instructions for my TikTok hook generator. You can also create your own or refine the AI-generated prompt to suit your needs.

Step 9: Add Conversation Starters

To improve user engagement, add conversation starters like “What’s your video about?” or “Please give me a viral hook.” These prompts nudge users to interact with the agent right away.

Step 10: Set Pricing and Publish

You can offer your AI agent for free as a lead magnet or set a paid pricing plan. While free usage is available, paid plans unlock more features like higher token limits and advanced monetization tools. Plans start at $20/month, which includes one million tokens, 30 training links, one agent, and payment integration.

Once you’re ready, click Publish. Your AI agent is now live and accessible via its unique URL. You can test it in an incognito window or share it with your audience.
