1. Nonverbia (Stella): AI that joins your sales meetings and optimizes deals

If your team runs on demos, discovery calls, or discovery-to-close meetings, this class of AI assistant is a game changer. Nonverbia's Stella acts like a real-time sales analyst, joining meetings, capturing signals, and turning conversations into action items.

What it does

  • Joins calls via calendar integration (Google Calendar or Outlook).
  • Analyzes transcripts and speaker behavior to generate buyer signals.
  • Scoring dashboards (audience score, presenter score, talk-time breakdown).
  • Action items and play-by-play so reps don’t miss follow-up tasks.
  • Manager insights to spot top performers and deals at risk.

Why it matters

Many teams still rely on notes or fragmented CRMs. Stella centralizes call intelligence, helps enforce best practices, reduces ramp time for new reps, and improves forecasting accuracy by turning noisy meeting data into structured signals.

Quick start

  • Connect your calendar and let the assistant join an upcoming sales meeting.
  • Review a single meeting report and implement two action items the rep missed.
  • Compare presenter scores week over week to identify coaching opportunities.

Pros and cons

  • Pro: Real-time intelligence that reduces manual CRM work.
  • Pro: Manager dashboards for scalable coaching.
  • Con: Requires buy-in from teams to trust and act on AI recommendations.
  • Con: Privacy and compliance should be verified for regulated industries.

2. Reap.video: Turn long-form content into viral shorts

Reap.video automates the painful process of clipping, captioning, and formatting short-form videos from longer recordings. Drop in a link, set preferences, and the platform finds, edits, scores, and exports clips optimized for social platforms.

Top features

  • Support for YouTube, Twitch, X, Vimeo, or direct uploads.
  • AI detects topics, transcribes audio, and suggests short clips with a virality score.
  • Caption styles, brand templates, split-screen edits, and multiple orientations.
  • AI editing suite for B-roll, text overlays, and export presets up to 4K.

Use cases

  • Creators who want consistent, high-volume short-form content from long videos.
  • Marketing teams repurposing webinars into ads and social snippets.
  • Educators extracting micro-lessons from lectures.

Quick start

  1. Paste a long-form video URL and choose clip length (for example, <15 seconds).
  2. Select caption style and orientation for your primary platform.
  3. Preview top clips, make minor edits, and export ready-to-post files.

Tips

  • Use custom brand templates to keep captions and colors consistent across platforms.
  • Target different languages with translations to test new markets quickly.
  • Use the virality score to prioritize the most promising clips for paid promotion.

3. Creslo: Create immersive 360 tours and flipbooks

Creslo lets you build interactive virtual tours, showroom walkthroughs, and realistic flipbook publications from PDFs. It merges simple uploads with dynamic interactivity so viewers can explore spaces or documents on desktop and mobile.

Key capabilities

  • Upload images and video to create 360-degree tours with hotspots and navigation.
  • Embed dynamic content, floor plans, and AI-generated virtual hosts.
  • Convert PDFs to flipbooks with page-flip realism, auto-play mode, analytics, and lead capture.
  • Advanced analytics to understand engagement within tours or publications.

Best for

  • Real estate agents showcasing properties remotely.
  • Event venues and museums creating immersive previews.
  • Businesses turning brochures and catalogs into engaging digital flipbooks.

Quick start

  1. Upload a set of 360 photos or video clips and map out hotspots.
  2. Add floor plans and label rooms to guide the user journey.
  3. Publish and use analytics to see which spaces get the most attention.

4. FirstBook.ai: Write your first book in hours, not years

Writing a book is often the hardest part. FirstBook.ai streamlines the entire process by asking targeted questions, generating a strategic book roadmap, and producing an editable manuscript that follows a professional structure.

How it works

  • Answer a guided questionnaire: working title, one-sentence premise, target audience, voice, and sample writing.
  • AI architects an eight-chapter framework with subchapter flow and strategic intent.
  • Approve the outline, then generate chapters that are formatted and ready for editing.

Why choose it over a general LLM

General language models can generate text, but they struggle with long-form structure, consistent voice, and pagination across large documents. FirstBook.ai is built for book creation, understands publishing structure, and keeps context over the whole project.

Quick start

  • Provide a one-sentence premise and your target reader profile.
  • Review the generated eight-chapter outline and make small edits to align with your angle.
  • Use chapter-by-chapter generation and then edit or hire an editor for polishing.

Use cases

  • Thought leaders producing a lead magnet or authority book.
  • Entrepreneurs documenting systems for internal playbooks or training manuals.
  • Non-fiction authors who want a head start on structure and draft content.

5. TryLapis.com: Personalized ads in any language for any platform

Creating localized, A/B-ready ad variations is tedious. TryLapis automates ad generation, scoring, and versioning across languages and platforms, with a central dashboard to manage assets.

Main features

  • Brand kit, product catalog, and creative templates in one place.
  • Automated ad generation for formats like story, feed, and display.
  • Ad scoring to prioritize the best candidates for testing.
  • Edit workflow to fine-tune copy, visuals, and vibe kits without rebuilding assets.

Best use cases

  • Small marketing teams that need scale without hiring large creative studios.
  • eCommerce brands generating localized variations for different markets.
  • Agencies that want a predictable way to produce high volumes of ads for clients.

Quick start

  1. Upload your brand kit and 3–5 top-performing product images.
  2. Select a campaign goal and generate 10–20 ad variations to test.
  3. Export the best-performing formats and run them in paid channels.

6. EmailIt.com: Cost-effective email API for developers and marketers

Sending transactional and marketing emails can get expensive with subscription-based ESPs. EmailIt provides an API-driven, pay-as-you-go alternative with templates, webhook integration, and sending limits to protect deliverability.

Why it stands out

  • No heavy subscription tiers—credits-based sending makes pricing predictable.
  • Domain setup, API keys, and webhooks for full developer control.
  • Templates, audience upload, contact verification, and robust metrics.
  • Sending limits to help avoid spam traps and preserve reputation.

Ideal for

  • Startups and indie makers who need affordable email at scale.
  • Developers building transactional workflows for apps.
  • Companies that want full control over deliverability and integrations.

Quick start

  1. Verify your sending domain and generate an API key.
  2. Upload a small audience and test a template with a low-volume send.
  3. Monitor metrics and gradually scale while keeping sending limits conservative.

7. Fusebase.com: Secure, production-ready portals, dashboards, and AI agents

Fusebase provides a platform to build customer portals, internal dashboards, and AI-driven apps with built-in security and integrations. Think of it as a low-code playground for production systems: workspaces, domains, knowledge bases, and AI agents ready to go.

Core capabilities

  • Create workspaces that include projects, nodes, and knowledge bases.
  • Make portals for customers, partners, or internal teams.
  • Embed AI agents to automate tasks like transcription, ticket triage, or knowledge retrieval.
  • Production-ready security and domain configuration out of the box.

Where it fits

  • SaaS businesses building client dashboards and self-serve portals.
  • Consultancies packaging deliverables in a branded client portal.
  • Teams automating workflows with AI agents tied to company data.

Quick start

  1. Create a workspace and pick a clean domain name for your portal.
  2. Choose a visual style, add files and tasks, and connect an AI agent for simple automation.
  3. Invite a small group of test users to validate flows and permissions.

How to pick which tools to try first

Focus on the smallest lift with the biggest ROI. Here’s a simple decision filter:

  • If you rely on calls to close deals, prioritize Nonverbia.
  • If content repurposing slows your growth, start with Reap.video.
  • If your business sells spaces or publishes brochures, test Creslo.
  • If you need lead magnets or want to publish a short authority book, try FirstBook.ai.
  • If ad creative speed is the bottleneck, TryLapis is the quick win.
  • If email cost is bleeding your budget, migrate to EmailIt for testing.
  • If you need client-facing apps or portals fast, prototype in Fusebase.

Integration and privacy considerations

AI tools are powerful, but integration and data policy matter. Before rolling any of these into production, make sure to:

  • Review data retention and encryption policies for each tool.
  • Run pilot tests with non-sensitive data when possible.
  • Set clear permissions and limit calendar or inbox access to necessary scopes.
  • Confirm compliance for regulated industries such as healthcare or finance.

FAQ

Are there deals or discounts available for these tools?

Many of these products run promotions occasionally, including AppSumo-style deals that offer discounted access or lifetime licenses. Check deal platforms and the vendors' official pages for limited offers. Always review the plan details to make sure the feature set meets your needs.

How do I protect sensitive customer data when using these tools?

Limit the permissions you grant (calendar, email, file access) to what is strictly necessary. Review the vendor's data encryption, retention, and GDPR/CCPA compliance. Run a pilot with anonymized or test data before connecting production systems.

Can I integrate these tools with my existing stack?

Most tools offer APIs, webhooks, or native integrations with common platforms (CRMs, ad platforms, CMS). Check each product's integration list and documentation. Use middleware like Zapier or native automation layers to connect systems when direct integrations are missing.

How much technical skill is required to start?

It varies: Reap.video and TryLapis are designed for marketers and creators with minimal technical skill. EmailIt and Fusebase have developer-friendly APIs that benefit from basic engineering knowledge. Nonverbia, Creslo, and FirstBook.ai are accessible to non-technical users but may require configuration for advanced use cases.

Will AI replace my creative or sales roles?

These tools augment and accelerate work rather than fully replace human judgement. They remove repetitive tasks, surface opportunity signals, and produce drafts that professionals can refine. The roles that leverage AI to scale decision-making and creative iteration win in the long run.

What is the quickest win across these seven tools?

If you need immediate impact with minimal setup time, Reap.video often delivers the fastest visible ROI by converting existing long videos into multiple high-performing short clips for social promotion. For sales teams, Nonverbia produces rapid improvements in meeting hygiene and follow-ups once adopted.
