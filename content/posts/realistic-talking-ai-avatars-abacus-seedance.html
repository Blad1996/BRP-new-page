How to Create a Realistic AI Avatar

The process is surprisingly simple. You can either choose from existing avatars or create a custom person from scratch.

Option 1: Choose a Ready-Made Avatar

Abacus AI Studio includes avatar options suited to different types of content and situations. This is the fastest route if you want to test an idea, create a product video, or publish UGC-style content without designing an entirely new character.

Option 2: Create a Custom Avatar from a Prompt

If you have a specific creator identity in mind, describe the person you want. You can define appearance, styling, energy, clothing, hair, accessories, and the overall personality of the character.

For example, you might create someone like this:

A warm creator in her late twenties with shoulder-length dark hair, a casual denim jacket, and friendly, confident energy.

You can go further by changing clothing, hairstyles, and accessories to suit the niche or campaign. A luxury travel creator, skincare reviewer, fashion try-on creator, tech presenter, or food content personality can all have completely different visual identities.

Option 3: Upload a Photo and Turn It into an Avatar

You can also upload a photo and turn that person into a custom AI avatar. Once the image is uploaded, you can name the avatar, describe the personality, select or create a voice, and use that character in templates or original video prompts.

This gives you a way to produce content featuring a consistent identity without needing to record every new piece of content manually.

A convincing avatar needs more than a realistic face. It needs a voice that matches the visual identity and the kind of content you are making.

Abacus AI Studio lets you define voice qualities such as:

  • Age range
  • Accent
  • Tone
  • Speaking pace
  • Energy level
  • Overall personality

You can describe a voice in highly specific terms, such as warm, youthful, feminine, smooth, slightly husky, confident, elegant, conversational, relaxed, sophisticated, intimate, or expressive. You can also work from a short uploaded voice sample.

Before committing, generate and test voice options so you can hear whether the delivery matches your creator concept. This is especially important for UGC-style content, where a voice that feels too scripted or disconnected from the avatar can make the content feel less natural.

How to Keep Your AI Avatar Consistent Across Scenes

One of the biggest challenges with AI video is keeping the character consistent from scene to scene. A creator can suddenly have a different face, body shape, hairstyle, or skin tone if the prompt is too vague.

The solution is to explicitly tell the model what needs to remain unchanged. For example, if you are creating a travel reel, include instructions that preserve the avatar’s face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, and body proportions throughout the entire video.

A useful prompt structure looks like this:

Create a cinematic luxury travel reel featuring me exploring Italy. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, and body proportions consistent across every scene.

This acts almost like a negative prompt. You are not only telling the AI what to create, you are also reducing the chances of the details you care about drifting between scenes.

Once your prompt is ready, the platform can build a script, create a production plan, map out scenes, and prepare the generation. You review the plan, approve it, and generate the final content.

Prompt Tips for Better AI Avatar Results

The output is only as good as the direction you give it. You do not need to write a massive prompt, but you do need to be clear about the details that matter.

  1. Describe the format. State whether you want a TikTok video, luxury travel reel, virtual try-on, product review, or cinematic advertisement.
  2. Specify your product reference. If a product appears in the video, ask the model to preserve its logo, shape, colour, packaging, typography, and proportions.
  3. Protect avatar consistency. Tell the model to keep facial features, hair, skin tone, and body proportions consistent throughout.
  4. Define the tone. Choose whether the content should feel casual, premium, conversational, luxury-focused, polished, energetic, or creator-led.
  5. Use templates when speed matters. Templates provide a strong starting point for formats such as UGC, try-ons, product ads, and short-form reels.
  6. Review the AI plan before generating. Approving the proposed script and scenes gives you another chance to make sure the direction makes sense.
