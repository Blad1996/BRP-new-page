What is Retriever AI? 🤖

Retriever AI is a Chrome extension designed to automate web-based tasks effortlessly. Unlike typical AI agents that operate within a single tab or have limited scraping ability, Retriever AI can interact with multiple tabs simultaneously, execute complex workflows, and even handle non-English websites with ease. Whether you want to extract data into Google Sheets, automate repetitive actions, or integrate with other tools like Slack, Retriever has you covered.

Getting started is simple: just visit Retriever.ai and add the extension to your Chrome browser. Once installed, a new interface opens that lets you create, manage, and schedule AI-driven tasks with just a few clicks.

How Retriever AI Works: Key Features and Functionalities ⚙️

Retriever AI’s power lies in its user-friendly yet robust interface that lets you control AI agents across multiple browser tabs. Here are some of its standout features:

  • Multi-tab Automation: Unlike other AI agents, Retriever can operate across parallel tabs, allowing it to perform multiple complex tasks simultaneously.
  • Data Extraction: Easily scrape websites and extract data like product prices, stock information, or social media content directly into Google Sheets or documents.
  • Task Recording and Repetition: The DLM recording feature lets you record sequences of actions on websites and replay them automatically, ensuring reliable, solution-free scraping.
  • Tool Integrations: Connect Retriever to your favorite productivity tools such as Slack, enabling automated messaging and workflow triggers.
  • API Configurations: Integrate Retriever with any external API, including AI models like Gemini, to enhance its capabilities without extra cost on credits.
  • Scheduling & Task Management: Pin, rerun, modify, and schedule tasks for automated execution at your convenience.
  • Security & Privacy: Retriever is built with privacy and security in mind, featuring sandboxed executions and limited permissions to protect your data.

All these features combined make Retriever AI one of the most versatile and reliable AI automation tools available today.

Getting Started with Retriever AI: Step-by-Step Guide 🛠️

Ready to start automating with Retriever AI? Here’s how to get going quickly:

  1. Install the Extension: Go to Retriever.ai and click “Add to Chrome” to install the extension.
  2. Open the AI Agent Interface: Click the Retriever icon in your browser toolbar to open the agent’s dashboard in a new tab.
  3. Explore Prompts and Actions: Use the pre-built prompts or create your own tasks. You can assign them to specific tabs and customize inputs.
  4. Record and Repeat Tasks: Use the DLM recording feature to capture complex sequences of actions that Retriever can replay later.
  5. Integrate APIs: Connect Retriever to external APIs or AI models like Gemini by configuring your API keys within the interface.
  6. Schedule and Manage Tasks: Pin important tasks, set schedules for automatic execution, and monitor progress all from the dashboard.

By following these steps, you can tailor Retriever AI to your unique workflow and start reclaiming hours of your day.
