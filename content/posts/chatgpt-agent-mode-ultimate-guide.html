🚀 What is ChatGPT Agent Mode?

Agent Mode is a revolutionary feature that combines the powers of ChatGPT Operator, ChatGPT Deep Research, and traditional ChatGPT into one seamless experience. This means ChatGPT can now perform multi-step actions, conduct in-depth research, interact with third-party applications, and even execute tasks automatically — all without constant human input.

Think of it as having a digital agent that can:

  • Book your trips, hotels, and rental cars.
  • Generate detailed reports and presentations.
  • Audit your schedules and emails.
  • Manage and analyze your spreadsheets.
  • Interact with apps like Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar, and more.
  • Schedule recurring tasks that run on daily, weekly, or monthly cadences.

All these capabilities unlock a new level of productivity and automation that previously required complex coding or multiple tools.

🔑 How to Access Agent Mode

Getting started with Agent Mode is surprisingly straightforward, provided you have the right ChatGPT subscription tier.

  • Pro Users: Immediate access with 400 queries per month.
  • Plus and Team Users: Access rolling out soon (within days or by Monday, as per OpenAI’s latest announcements) with 40 queries per month.

To enable Agent Mode:

  1. Go to your ChatGPT interface.
  2. Click on the Tools menu.
  3. Select Agent Mode from the list.
  4. Check your remaining query quota (e.g., 400 queries for Pro users).

Once enabled, you can also connect various apps through the Connectors settings, allowing Agent Mode to interact with your existing tools securely.

📅 Scheduling and Managing Tasks with Agent Mode

One of the most exciting aspects of Agent Mode is its ability to schedule tasks and automate them on a recurring basis. This isn’t just about asking ChatGPT a question; it’s about having it perform actions for you at the right time without you needing to prompt it again.

Here’s how scheduling works:

  • Navigate to Schedules in the settings.
  • Manage your existing scheduled tasks or create new ones.
  • Set tasks to run on various cadences — daily, weekly, monthly, or custom intervals.

For example, you can have Agent Mode:

  • Pull and send you a weekly sales report every Monday morning.
  • Catch you up on unread emails every day before you start work.
  • Build and update presentations or spreadsheets automatically.

This level of automation transforms ChatGPT from a reactive assistant into a proactive one that helps you stay ahead.

🤖 How Agent Mode Works in Action

Agent Mode operates by reasoning through tasks step-by-step, much like a human would. For instance, if you ask it to book a boutique hotel in Kyoto on Booking.com, it will:

  1. Search for highly rated boutique hotels for your specified dates.
  2. Prioritize options based on guest ratings, price range, and proximity to attractions.
  3. Present its recommendations.
  4. Request your login credentials securely if you want it to complete the booking.
  5. Proceed with booking if authorized, or prompt you to finalize payment details manually.

This process takes advantage of ChatGPT’s ability to interact with websites and services, but with built-in safety protocols to protect your privacy and security.

🔒 Safety and Privacy Considerations

With great power comes great responsibility. OpenAI has emphasized the importance of safety in Agent Mode, especially since the AI can take actions on your behalf online. Some of the key safety features include:

  • Explicit permission prompts before logging into any accounts or handling sensitive data.
  • Manual intervention required for critical actions like payment finalization.
  • Privacy controls that keep you in charge of your data and connections.
  • Secure connectors that manage app integrations responsibly.

These measures ensure that while the AI is powerful, you remain in control at all times.

Need a professional presentation on the DTC skincare sector for investors? Agent Mode can:

3. Building Financial Models and Cash Flow Forecasts 💰

Agent Mode can create in-depth financial models, such as a Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model for an energy company. It:

  • Gathers relevant financial data and industry information.
  • Builds an Excel spreadsheet with assumptions, revenues, cash flows, and debt.
  • Generates valuation estimates based on the model.

This process, which took around 17 minutes, is remarkably fast compared to manual analysis by financial experts.

4. Catching Up on Microsoft Teams Conversations 📧

If you missed a week of work and need to get up to speed, Agent Mode can:

  • Review your Teams conversations from the past week.
  • Summarize key updates and action items.
  • Prioritize who you should respond to first.

It also supports Gmail and Outlook, making it easy to stay on top of your communications without sifting through endless messages.

5. Auditing Your Calendar to Analyze Time Use 📅

Agent Mode can analyze your Google Calendar or Outlook calendar over the last six months to:

  • Break down how you’ve spent your time.
  • Provide detailed reports on work, commuting, personal, and relationship time allocations.
  • Help you understand and optimize your schedule.

For example, it might reveal a balanced lifestyle with 26% work, 9.9% commuting, and 44% personal time.
