🚀 Introducing Gemini in Google Meet: Your AI Meeting Assistant

One of the most exciting updates is the seamless integration of Gemini directly into Google Meet. Imagine having a personal AI assistant join your meetings, summarizing discussions, catching you up on what you missed, and extracting key takeaways—all in real time.

How it works: When you join a Google Meet session, you’ll notice a new option to activate Gemini in the top right corner. Once started, Gemini listens to the conversation and can provide you with:

  • Summaries of what’s been discussed so far
  • Key action points and takeaways
  • Answers to specific questions about the meeting content

This feature is a game-changer for anyone who struggles to keep up with fast-paced meetings or needs to catch up after joining late. It ensures that you’re always in the loop without having to take frantic notes.

But Gemini’s power in Google Meet goes beyond just summarizing. It can also access your Google Drive files, spreadsheets, and documents during the meeting. By simply typing @ followed by your query, Gemini can pull in relevant data from your Drive, providing instant context and answers without leaving the meeting window.

This integration is ideal for teams working on collaborative projects, enabling you to reference documents, spreadsheets, and emails on the fly. Gemini’s side panel in Meet acts as a dynamic research assistant, making meetings more productive and focused.

🎨 Gemini's New Image Model: Imagen 4 Ultra Preview

Google has upgraded Gemini’s image generation capabilities with the release of the Imagen 4 Ultra preview model. Accessible through AI Studio, this model offers superior image quality and more precise text editing features than previous versions.

Here’s why you should start using Imagen 4 Ultra:

  • Higher quality images: The Ultra preview produces sharper, more detailed visuals, perfect for marketing, content creation, or personal projects.
  • Advanced text manipulation: Unlike other AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini’s image model can accurately modify text within images, making it easier to create polished graphics.
  • Flexible aspect ratios: You can control the image dimensions with a wider array of aspect ratios, offering greater creative freedom compared to many other AI image generators.

For creators and marketers, this upgrade means you can generate professional-looking images directly from Gemini with minimal manual editing, saving time and effort.

🤖 Automate Your Workflows with Gemini’s Scheduled Tasks

Automation is key to staying productive in today’s fast-paced world, and Google Gemini now lets you automate routine tasks with scheduled workflows. This feature is accessible at gemini.google.com, where you can create and manage automated tasks tailored to your needs.

Setting up scheduled tasks: Click on the gear icon to access scheduling options. You can define tasks such as:

  • Sending daily summaries of your calendar, to-dos, and unread emails
  • Fetching the latest AI news at a specific time every day
  • Custom reminders or reports based on your Google Workspace data

What makes Gemini’s automation stand out is its deep integration with Google’s ecosystem. You can connect Gemini to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, YouTube, and even third-party tools like GitHub. This means your automated tasks can interact with multiple platforms simultaneously, streamlining your workflow like never before.

For example, you could schedule Gemini to compile a weekly report from your Google Sheets data, send it via Gmail, and create a presentation outline in Google Docs—all automatically.

This level of automation empowers individuals and teams to save countless hours on repetitive tasks, letting you focus on higher-impact work.

⚡ Gemini Flash Model: Faster AI Responses Without Sacrificing Quality

Speed and efficiency are critical when working with AI, and Gemini’s new Flash model delivers on both fronts. This update introduces two variants: 2.5 Flash and 2.5 Flash Light. These models are significantly faster than the traditional 2.5 Pro model but maintain comparable answer quality.

Here’s what you need to know about using the Flash model effectively:

  • Use Flash for general queries: For most everyday tasks like research, summarization, or generating content, the Flash model is the best choice due to its speed.
  • Reserve Pro for complex tasks: When your work involves advanced reasoning, coding, or mathematical calculations, the 2.5 Pro model still offers advantages in accuracy.
  • Compare models easily: AI Studio lets you run the same prompt through different models side by side, so you can see how Flash stacks up against Pro in real time.

This update addresses a common pain point where users default to the Pro model, resulting in slower responses without meaningful improvements. Switching to Flash will make your AI interactions smoother and less frustrating.

📂 Enhanced Google Drive and Workspace Integration with Gemini

Google Gemini’s integration with Google Drive and Workspace apps has been supercharged. You can now ask Gemini to summarize activity within specific Drive folders, giving you a quick overview of recent changes, new files, or updates without digging through folders manually.

For instance, if you want to catch up on project progress, Gemini can provide a summary of edits and comments across multiple documents in a folder, saving you from opening each file separately.

In Google Docs, Gemini offers:

  • Outline creation to structure your documents effortlessly
  • Brainstorming assistance to generate ideas quickly
  • Access to your “gems” — personalized projects or knowledge bases you create within Gemini, which follow you across different Google apps

“Gems” act like portable AI assistants tailored to specific tasks or domains. For example, you could have a “Writing Editor” gem that helps polish content whether you’re in Gemini, Docs, or Sheets.

In Google Sheets, Gemini can generate charts, analyze data for insights, and interact with your gems. This creates a powerful environment for data-driven decision-making without needing to switch between multiple tools or manually crunch numbers.

🌐 URL Context Tool: Research with Precision Using Specific Web Links

One of the lesser-known but incredibly powerful features in Gemini is the URL Context tool. This allows Gemini to fetch and analyze information directly from specific URLs you provide, instead of relying solely on its internal knowledge or web searches.

Imagine you want a detailed comparison of Caribbean travel destinations. You can supply Gemini with URLs of trusted travel sites, specify the criteria you care about—like climate, attractions, cost, and accessibility—and Gemini will generate a comprehensive comparison table based strictly on those sources.

This feature is perfect for:

  • Market research where you want to rely on vetted sources
  • Academic or professional reports requiring citation of specific materials
  • Content creation that demands accuracy and up-to-date information from particular websites

You also have the option to use Gemini’s “Grounded with Google Search” mode, which lets it perform its own web searches to gather information. But when precision and source control matter, the URL context tool is unmatched.

FAQs About Google Gemini’s New Features 🤔

What is Google Gemini and how is it different from other AI tools?

Google Gemini is Google’s next-generation AI platform that integrates deeply with Google Workspace and other tools. Unlike standalone AI like ChatGPT, Gemini can access your Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, and more, enabling it to provide personalized, context-aware assistance across your work environment.

How do I activate Gemini in Google Meet?

When you join a Google Meet session, look for the Gemini icon in the top right corner. Click ‘Start’ to bring Gemini into the meeting. It will then listen and provide summaries, key takeaways, and relevant file information during the call.

Can I automate tasks using Gemini?

Yes! Gemini’s scheduled tasks feature allows you to automate actions like sending daily email summaries, fetching news, or generating reports. You set the task frequency and link Gemini to your favorite Google apps for seamless automation.

What is the Flash model in Gemini and when should I use it?

The Flash model is a faster AI model that delivers quick responses without compromising answer quality. Use it for general tasks like summarization and content creation. For complex tasks involving math or coding, stick to the Pro model.

How does the URL Context tool work?

This tool lets you provide specific URLs for Gemini to research and generate content from, ensuring that the information is accurate and sourced from trusted sites you select. It’s ideal for detailed, source-specific research.
