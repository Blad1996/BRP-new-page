🛠 Getting Started: Building Your First AI Agent on Relay.app

Let’s dive into the practical steps for creating your first AI agent. Here’s a simple example of automating your email inbox to respond to specific messages automatically.

Step 1: Sign Up and Explore

Head over to relay.app and sign up for a free account. Once logged in, the interface is clean and beginner-friendly. You’ll see options to start new workflows, access templates, and explore the community and documentation.

Step 2: Create a New Workflow

Click on New Workflow to begin. This is where you’ll build your AI agent step by step.

Step 3: Choose Your Trigger

The trigger is what starts your AI agent. Relay.app offers a variety of triggers including:

  • Manual start (you prompt it yourself)
  • Scheduled runs (e.g., daily or weekly)
  • Webhook or mail hook triggers
  • Actions from other apps (like receiving an email)

For our example, we’ll automate responses to emails from a specific sender. Search for and select Gmail as your trigger app, then choose Email Received. You can filter emails, for instance, to only trigger on messages from a particular domain or email address.

Step 4: Add Filters to Narrow Down Your Trigger

Use the filter assistant to specify conditions like “from: robertqueencitymediagroup.com.” This ensures your agent only activates for relevant emails, avoiding unnecessary automation on unrelated messages.

Step 5: Summarize Email Content with AI

Next, add a step to summarize the email’s body text. This allows the AI agent to understand the email’s content before crafting a response. Relay.app makes it easy to select the email body as the source for summarization and lets you specify the summary length and detail.

Step 6: Generate a Polite Response Using AI

Now for the magic. Add an AI step to write a response based on the summary. You can provide custom instructions such as:

“Always answer in a polite tone but decline all requests.”

You can also map the summarized content from the previous step as input to this AI response generator, ensuring relevant and context-aware replies.

Step 7: Send the Email Automatically

Finally, connect the AI-generated response to the Gmail Send Email action. Map the recipient, subject, and body fields dynamically from the incoming email and AI output, respectively.

Step 8: Test and Activate Your Agent

Run a test by sending a sample email to your inbox. You can watch the workflow execute live in Relay.app’s Runs tab. Once you confirm it works as expected, turn the workflow on and let it handle your inbox automatically.

This simple example saves you from manually reading and responding to repetitive emails like sponsorship requests or inquiries, freeing up your time for higher-value work.
