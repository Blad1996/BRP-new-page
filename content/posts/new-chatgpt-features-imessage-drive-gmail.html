Lower GPT API Pricing Makes Advanced Models More Accessible

One of the biggest updates is not flashy, but it matters massively for developers and businesses building with AI. OpenAI has reduced pricing for Frontier GPT 5.6 Sol on its API for the next three months by more than 20%.

The updated pricing is listed at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, down from $5 and $30 respectively. If you are using the API at scale, particularly for workloads with significant output, that is a substantial reduction in cost.

The change also applies to eligible ChatGPT Work and Codex plans. With competition increasing from Anthropic and Chinese AI models, pricing is becoming a serious part of the AI race. This is good news for anyone building automations, internal tools, content systems, or customer-facing products with ChatGPT.

Other OpenAI models have also received price reductions, including cuts of approximately 20% for one model and 80% for another. The practical takeaway is simple: if API costs previously stopped you from experimenting more aggressively, it may be time to revisit the numbers.

Connect ChatGPT to iMessage on Your Mac

The ChatGPT desktop app on Mac can now connect with the native Messages app through a Messages plugin. Once it is enabled, ChatGPT can search, read, and send messages, including local file attachments, using your existing Messages setup.

To access it, update the ChatGPT desktop app, go to the Plugins section, and look for Messages. The integration works through an MCP server, giving ChatGPT a bridge to your local message data and actions.

What the Messages Integration Can Do

  • Find specific conversations.
  • Search message history for names, subjects, and commitments.
  • Identify follow-ups from recent conversations.
  • Draft replies based on context.
  • Send messages and local attachments through the Messages app.

A useful prompt could be: “Suggest everything from yesterday that I need to follow up on.” ChatGPT can then review relevant messages and turn scattered conversations into an action list.

Before allowing any message to go out, review it. You can approve sending on a case-by-case basis, allow sending within a specific chat, or continue manually. This is exactly the kind of feature that can save real time, but only if you keep control over what gets sent and to whom.

Use ChatGPT Directly With Google Drive Files

ChatGPT also has a new Google Drive integration. Connect Google Drive and you can work with Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Excel files, PDFs, and uploaded files without constantly switching between tabs and tools.

This puts ChatGPT much closer to the daily workflow most people already have. Instead of opening a document, copying information into a chat, generating a draft, and pasting it back into Google Drive, you can create and work with structured content in a more connected environment.

For example, you can tag Google Drive in a request to draft release notes in a Google Doc. The document can open right beside ChatGPT, where you can review and edit the content as needed.

Google already has Gemini integrated into its productivity tools, so this is a direct and important competitive move. The bigger point is that plugins are rapidly making ChatGPT more useful as an operating layer for the tools you already use.

Check the Plugin Library Regularly

New plugins are being added continually. Take a few minutes to browse what is available and think about where they fit in your own workflow.

For sales and outbound teams, Apollo is one example worth looking at. It can help accelerate prospecting and outbound execution, making it easier to find leads and organize outreach. The best plugin is not necessarily the most popular one. It is the one that removes a repetitive step from work you are already doing.

Generate Transparent Background Logos in ChatGPT

ChatGPT image generation can now create images with transparent backgrounds. This sounds like a minor update until you have spent hours trying to remove a bad background from a logo, product visual, or graphic.

Try a prompt such as: “Create a logo for my construction company with a transparent background.” The resulting image can retain transparency, making it much easier to place on websites, presentations, merchandise, social graphics, or branded documents.

Previously, the process often looked like this:

  1. Generate or download an image.
  2. Move it into a separate background-removal tool.
  3. Attempt to clean up rough edges and fine lines.
  4. Hope the logo did not get distorted in the process.

That last part is where things often went wrong. Fine details, especially thin logo lines, can look damaged or jagged after background removal. Native transparency removes a lot of that friction.

Why Prompt Optimization Still Matters

Transparent backgrounds are helpful, but the quality of the result still comes down to the quality of the prompt. A basic request may produce something usable. A more structured image prompt gives ChatGPT clearer direction on style, materials, composition, professionalism, and the visual standards you expect.

For example, instead of a vague logo request, specify that you want a professional transparent-background logo with a polished designer-made appearance. The difference between the two outputs can be dramatic.

Prompt optimization is not only for images. Better prompts improve writing, research, planning, coding, and practically everything else you do inside ChatGPT. Saving your best prompts as shortcuts also means you do not have to recreate winning instructions every time.

Computer History Gives ChatGPT More Context

A major desktop setting called Computer History can record what you are doing while the ChatGPT app is open. It tracks the apps and activity on your computer, then creates local Markdown memory files that ChatGPT can use to better understand your ongoing work.

This can make ChatGPT considerably more useful because it has more context than whatever you manually typed into a single chat. It may understand that you have been working through purchase orders, sponsored assets, documents, or other tasks across different applications.

You have controls over the feature. You can turn it on, pause it, exclude selected apps and websites, and clear history from the last 10 minutes, hour, or day.

This is powerful, but it is also personal. Before enabling it, think about the apps you use, the documents you access, and the amount of work context you want stored locally for ChatGPT to reference.

Computer Use, App Shots, and Remote Access

Inside the desktop app, Computer Use expands what ChatGPT can do with your computer. It can be configured to control apps and other actions, while a Chrome extension can sync data among the ChatGPT desktop app, web app, browser, and mobile device.

One setting can allow ChatGPT to use your Mac when it is locked. This creates a potentially useful setup for heavy ChatGPT users who need access to computer files or workflows while away from their desk.

The important thing is to understand the level of access you are granting. Remote access and local file access are incredibly convenient, but convenience should never become automatic permission.

Use App Shots to Ask Questions Faster

App Shots lets you use keyboard shortcuts to capture what is currently on screen and automatically import it into ChatGPT or Codex. It could be an article, an X post, a document, or something inside an app.

Instead of taking a screenshot, locating it in your files, and dragging it into a conversation, App Shots can eliminate those extra steps. That is a small workflow change with a big payoff when you use it dozens of times per day.

Find Restaurant Reservations Inside ChatGPT

ChatGPT can now help search for restaurant reservations directly in the app. A request like “I will be in New York City Tuesday and Wednesday next week. I want to eat omakase. Please find me reservations” can return available options, times, and booking details.

In the example, ChatGPT surfaced places including Omakase Room and Shiro, while also showing that Shion had no available reservations because it was closed. The value here is not just getting restaurant names. It is seeing useful availability information in the same place you made the request.

Another quality-of-life improvement is the ability to see whether an item in your chat history was a standard chat or a work chat simply by hovering over it. If you have a busy ChatGPT account, small organizational features like this make a real difference.

Connect Gmail for Email Drafting and Search

Gmail integration gives ChatGPT the ability to search email, use inbox context, draft messages, and potentially send emails on your behalf. For example, you could ask it to draft an email to Ferrari about a current car not being included on a purchase order.

ChatGPT can create the draft and offer options to open it in email or copy and paste it. With the appropriate connection and permissions, it can also provide a send option.

This is a huge time-saver for routine correspondence, follow-ups, and messages that require digging through past details. But email is high-stakes. Always check names, attachments, dates, numbers, and tone before approving a send.

Build, Edit, Host, and Analyze a Website With ChatGPT Sites

ChatGPT Sites takes the platform into entirely new territory. You can ask ChatGPT to build a website, open it in the browser, annotate it, edit it through chat, share it publicly, host it, and view analytics.

A sample use case was building a website for a TikTok growth and monetization accelerator. ChatGPT generated the site design and copy, then made it possible to request changes conversationally. Need a new section, different wording, or a revised layout? Describe what you want inside ChatGPT.

The Sites area also includes controls related to browsing data, such as cookies, passwords, password autofill, downloads, and history. You can review and clear browsing history as needed.

This means ChatGPT can now act as a much more complete website workflow: creation, refinement, hosting, sharing, settings, and analytics. You may not need a separate tool for every one of those steps.

Choose Local or Cloud Chats Based on Security Needs

When starting a new chat in the desktop app, you can choose where it runs:

  • On your computer: ChatGPT can read and edit local files.
  • In the cloud: ChatGPT cannot access local files.

You can also select the approval level for computer and internet access. Options include unrestricted access, approval from you, or requesting approval every time.

There is no universal best setting. It comes down to the work you are doing and how comfortable you are with ChatGPT accessing your local machine, files, browser information, and online tools. The more capability you enable, the more intentional you should be about your permissions.

Use the New ChatGPT Features Responsibly

The direction is clear: ChatGPT is moving from a chatbot into a connected workspace that can understand your files, messages, emails, browser activity, documents, and computer tasks.

That opens up incredible opportunities to move faster. It also creates a responsibility to set boundaries. Start with the integrations that solve a real problem, review permissions before enabling them, and use approval settings for anything that can send messages, access sensitive files, or perform actions on your behalf.

Try one workflow first. Connect Google Drive to draft a document, use transparent-background generation for a logo, or use App Shots to ask questions about what is on your screen. Once you see where ChatGPT genuinely saves time, expand from there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can ChatGPT read and send iMessages?

On a Mac with the updated ChatGPT desktop app and the Messages plugin enabled, ChatGPT can search, read, and send messages through the native Messages app. Review message permissions and sending settings carefully before using it.

Can ChatGPT create transparent background images?

Yes. ChatGPT image generation can create images and logos with transparent backgrounds, helping avoid the extra step of using a separate background-removal tool.

Can ChatGPT access Google Drive and Gmail?

ChatGPT can connect with Google Drive to work with documents, spreadsheets, slides, PDFs, and uploaded files. Gmail integration can also provide email search, drafting, inbox context, and sending capabilities depending on the permissions enabled.

What is ChatGPT Computer History?

Computer History is a desktop setting that records activity while the ChatGPT app is open and creates local memory files ChatGPT can use for additional context. It can be paused, cleared, and configured to exclude selected apps and websites.

Should ChatGPT have unrestricted access to my computer?

That depends on your comfort level and workflow. ChatGPT offers options for unrestricted access, approval before actions, or approval every time. Use stricter approval settings when working with sensitive files, messages, email, or browsing data.
