📱 Share Your Screen with ChatGPT for Real-Time Collaboration

One of the most groundbreaking features that Rob highlights is the ability to share your screen with ChatGPT on mobile devices. Imagine having ChatGPT see exactly what you see on your screen, enabling it to assist you in real-time with any task. Whether you’re troubleshooting a software issue, reviewing a document, or navigating a website, this feature allows ChatGPT to provide contextual help based on your screen content.

How to enable screen sharing:

  1. Open ChatGPT on your mobile device.
  2. Tap on the options icon (three dots).
  3. Select Share Screen.
  4. Choose the screen or app you want to share.
  5. ChatGPT will now have access to your screen and can collaborate with you interactively.

This feature is perfect for collaborative problem-solving or getting detailed help that requires visual context. It’s a game-changer for users who want personalized, screen-specific assistance.

📸 Use Your Phone’s Camera to Show ChatGPT the World

Taking interaction a step further, ChatGPT can now access your phone’s front and back cameras. This means you can literally show ChatGPT what you see, whether it's identifying a plant, reading a label, or examining a gadget. This visual input enriches the AI's understanding, making its responses more precise and relevant.

How to access camera sharing:

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone.
  2. Activate Advanced Voice Mode.
  3. Tap the camera icon to switch between front and back cameras.
  4. Show ChatGPT what you want it to see for immediate recognition or analysis.

Currently, this feature is exclusive to the mobile app, but Rob mentions that desktop support is on the horizon, promising even more seamless AI integration in daily workflows.

🧠 Control What ChatGPT Remembers with Personalized Memory Settings

One of the most critical aspects of using ChatGPT effectively is managing its memory. ChatGPT can remember past conversations to improve context and continuity, but sometimes you want to control what it retains or deletes.

Rob emphasizes that you can customize these memory settings to suit your privacy and productivity needs. For example, if there’s information you don’t want ChatGPT to reference in future sessions, you can easily remove it.

How to manage memory:

  1. Click on your profile icon in the top right corner.
  2. Go to Settings > Personalization.
  3. Toggle Reference Chat History on or off to control if ChatGPT uses past conversations.
  4. Click Manage Memories to view, edit, or delete specific memories.

This feature is particularly useful for maintaining privacy or refining ChatGPT’s understanding to better fit your evolving needs.

🕵️‍♂️ Use Temporary Chats to Keep Sensitive Conversations Private

Sometimes, you want to ask ChatGPT questions or share information without having it saved or used to train future AI models. Temporary Chats are your go-to solution for this.

Temporary Chats function like an incognito window in a browser. They don't save the conversation history, don't update ChatGPT's memory, and aren't used for training purposes (though OpenAI may keep the data for up to 30 days for safety and quality monitoring).

How to enable Temporary Chat:

  1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT.
  2. Click on the option to Turn on Temporary Chat.

This is invaluable when discussing sensitive topics, testing ideas, or simply wanting a fresh start without past context influencing responses.

🎨 Create Stunning Images and Videos Directly Within ChatGPT

Beyond text, ChatGPT now empowers you to generate images and videos effortlessly. Using the built-in Sora tool, you can produce visual content customized to your specifications.

How to create images and videos:

  1. Start a new chat and select Tools > Create an Image.
  2. Describe what you want in detail (e.g., “A sunset over a mountain lake in watercolor style”).
  3. Alternatively, click on the Sora tab on the left sidebar to access video creation and advanced image settings.
  4. Adjust resolution, aspect ratio, duration (for videos), and other parameters.
  5. Generate multiple variations and create storyboards for video sequences.

This feature opens up creative possibilities for marketing campaigns, social media content, and storytelling, all powered by AI.

Imagine telling ChatGPT to buy concert tickets, find discounted luxury items, or book a vacation rental, and watching it browse, compare options, and execute the transaction for you. Operator can handle multiple tabs simultaneously, performing different tasks independently.

Examples of tasks Operator can do:

  • Purchase event tickets.
  • Shop for specific items at the best price.
  • Search for travel accommodations with your criteria.
  • Order food, book local services, or find event details.

While still in development and not flawless, Operator hints at a future where AI acts as a personal browser assistant, saving tremendous time and effort.

🔗 Connect External Sources for Deep Research Integration

ChatGPT now supports connecting various external data sources to deepen its research capabilities. This allows you to pull in information from your Gmail, Google Drive, Dropbox, GitHub, HubSpot, Box, and more directly into your ChatGPT sessions.

How to set up external connections:

This feature is ideal for professionals who need to integrate AI insights with their existing data repositories, streamlining workflows and enhancing productivity.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ❓

What devices support screen sharing and camera features in ChatGPT?

Currently, both screen sharing and camera features are available exclusively on the ChatGPT mobile app. Desktop support for these features is expected in future updates.

How secure is the memory feature in ChatGPT?

ChatGPT allows you to control what it remembers. You can delete specific memories or turn off chat history referencing altogether. Temporary Chats provide an extra layer of privacy by not saving or using your conversations to train models.

Can ChatGPT really automate browser tasks?

Yes, the Operator feature acts as a browser agent that can autonomously perform tasks like booking tickets, shopping, or finding travel options. Though still evolving, it represents a significant step toward AI-powered browser automation.

How can I create my own customized GPT?

You can create a custom GPT by configuring its name, description, instructions, conversation starters, and upload files. Once saved, it can be shared publicly or kept private, tailoring AI to your specific needs.

Are the AI-generated images and videos royalty-free?

Generally, AI-generated content created via ChatGPT tools is free to use, but always review the terms of service and licensing agreements to ensure compliance with usage rights.
