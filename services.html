Scope agreed for your business

Every proposal lists the devices, users, locations, compatible systems and services included. Device counts are a guide, not a limit.

Monitoring is not human support

Automated monitoring watches the systems included and sends alerts. Support hours, response times and escalation are set in each proposal. We don't promise unlimited support or 24/7 emergency response.

Licences and extras

Licences, equipment, parts, special projects, travel and third-party services are quoted separately unless your contract includes them. Managing a tool doesn't mean its licence is included.

Not assumed

Network cabling and installations need their own scope and quote. Support for machinery, sensors, PLCs and industrial systems isn't included by default.
