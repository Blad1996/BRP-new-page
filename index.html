Three plans

Find the plan that fits how you work.

Each plan adds to the one before. We confirm the right one after a first look at your setup.

No fixed price list. Every proposal is quoted on your scope.

  1. 01Everyday support

    Essential IT

    For

    Family farms, small producers, shops and offices with a simple setup (around 1 to 5 devices).

    Solves

    Everyday problems with computers, email and accounts.

    • Remote help when something stops working
    • Devices kept updated and maintained
    • Basic security review and account protection
    Ask about Essential IT
  2. 02Prevention

    Business Care

    For

    Growing agricultural businesses, greenhouses, distributors and offices (around 6 to 20 devices).

    Solves

    Problems that grow before anyone notices.

    • Preventive maintenance and monitoring of compatible systems
    • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace looked after
    • A status report with recommendations every month
    Ask about Business Care
  3. 03Full management

    Managed IT & Security

    For

    Businesses with several systems, servers or more than one location.

    Solves

    Downtime and risk when the whole operation runs on technology.

    • Servers, infrastructure and business services managed
    • 24/7 automated monitoring and a managed firewall
    • Security assessments and continuity planning

    24/7 monitoring sends automated alerts. It isn't round-the-clock human support.

    Ask about Managed IT
